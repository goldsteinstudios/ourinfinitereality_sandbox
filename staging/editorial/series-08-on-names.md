# On Names
*Essay 8 · tracks RSM r13, DDJ r6d, methodology v7.7 r1 · the series' methodological center*

**The invariant:** naming instantiates a frame — so the constant structure can be pointed at from every frame and captured in none, and the difference between pointing and capturing is the whole discipline.

The most famous line in the tradition is usually translated as mysticism: *the Dao that can be spoken is not the eternal Dao* — reality ineffable, language defeated, bow your head. This project's ruling reads it as something drier and stronger: a heading followed by a theorem. 道可道，非常道；名可名，非常名 — the way that can be expressed is not the *constant* way; the name that can name is not the constant name. Not "words fail," but a precise typing claim: any explicit expression is a frame's expression, denominated in that frame's unit, cut at that frame's grain — and the constant structure is what holds *across* frames, so no single frame's expression is it. The text is not despairing of language. It is stating language's type signature. (Stratum honesty: this chapter is absent from the Guodian strips — the oldest physical witness does not contain the tradition's most famous line — so the parsing is a reading of received text [R], made with the strips' vocabulary as control.)

The framework derives the theorem the line states, three times over, as its three entailed inexpressibilities. The origin resists reference from within its own frame, because it is where the frame's distinctions give out — which is why the text's cosmological chapter, having met exactly this, says *I do not know its name; forced to name it, I call it great* [G]: the honest label for a referent real but unreachable, a naming that flags itself as forced. The recursion resists single expression, because it is a relation between two frames' expressions — the same locus is a parent's realized crossing and a child's unreached origin, and no one sentence speaks both frames at once. And direction resists single-mode expression — the previous essay's result — so that even mathematics' ∞ is a name doing double duty for two different endlessnesses. Strain, in all three cases, exactly where the structure predicts strain: the correction history at these loci is confirmation-shaped, and the project prices that shape as shape.

What, then, is responsible naming, if capture is off the table? The project's answer is its own working notation, and this essay is where the notation explains itself. The mark `::` — *maps to, never is* — sets two expressions from different frames pointing at one invariant while forbidding their identification; it is the constant-name theorem turned into punctuation. The strips have their own version: the particle 也, which this cycle's philology found doing exactly this work on strip A15 — asserting sameness-of-law across a *maintained* pair, presence and absence identified in their generation and never merged [G]. Identity that preserves twoness. Even the written form of the equals sign obeys it: two strokes, and the gap between them load-bearing.

That clause carries more than a flourish, and what hangs from it is worth stating as one move rather than three observations [reading — expository; nothing under it is new]. The move: take a mark the received reading treats as sameness or as negation, and re-read it as two poles flanking a center that none of them occupy. The strips' 非 wears it — two wings turning away from a shared spine, the gap between them the origin both sides diverge around rather than a door closing on a claim. The integers wear it — −1 and +1, which obtain, naming in their sum a middle that does not. And Euler wears it, where the zero sits in the throat of the sign rather than at the end of an addition: the pivot the half-turn turns on, and the one position no point of the circle occupies. What makes this a thread and not three coincidences is the recognition that the project's own notation has been doing it since the beginning without saying so. `X = Y` at the balance line was never an assertion that the modes are the same. It marks the locus where they would coincide and which therefore does not obtain — the sign as the name of a gap. Whether *every* equals sign has been doing this is the question the thread leaves standing, and it is a question about reading rather than about arithmetic: the identities are unchanged and true, and what moves is only what a reader takes the middle stroke to be doing.

The thread also has to be priced, and this essay is where the pricing belongs. The framework authored the equals-sign reading and it authored the glyph reading; that the two agree is one hand holding up two mirrors. Agreement of that kind is pedagogy and never evidence — the same discipline that prices the text's convergence with the mathematics at nothing, for the same reason, since the framework was built through the text. The three tiers stay apart: **resistance**, where the strips refuse a correspondence and carry weight precisely because they are blind to the reader; **attestation**, checkable and projection-flagged; and **reading**, which is free, which can be deep, and which is where all of this lives. A lens earns its place in the book by showing a reader something they can then check elsewhere, not by counting as a finding.

Because names instantiate frames, names also *do things*, and the project's severest discipline is about that. A vocabulary can smuggle structure: process-words import time upstream of its derivation; transfer-words invent a point-source where there is no point; developmental prefixes install a privileged final frame that the framework forbids; a single mistyped verb, left in a canonical document, teaches its error to every future reader, human or machine. So the project maintains a strike-list the way a workshop maintains a blade — words banned not for tone but for false structural claims folded into their grammar — and the deepest finding of that practice is philological: the tradition's own transmission drifted the same way. The strips' constancy-graph gained a heart and then, under an emperor's taboo, became a different word; the strips' *center* became the received text's *stillness*; the identity-particles were stripped out century by century. Two and a half millennia of copyists made, slowly, the same class of error the project's own drafting makes quickly — and the strips, being older than the drift, get to correct both.

So the essay ends where the method lives. Every name is a frame's cut; every frame's cut is real and partial; the constant is what the cuts converge on and none contains. To name well is to point and show your hand — tag the frame, price the claim, keep the gap in the equals sign. The alternative is not silence. The alternative is a name that has forgotten it is one.
