**The invariant:** space and time are not the container and the river — they are two readings of one conserved structure, and the largest frame is a frame like any other.

Begin with what is actually derived, so the candidates that follow stand on marked ground. Succession is derived, not assumed: traversal around a frame's excluded center is ordered — the quarter-turns come in sequence because the closed path has a winding — and *time*, in this framework, renders as that ordering read along a lineage [derived in structure; the physics reading P2]. Linearity is derived with it: generation is irreversible (reversal would require the impossibility to obtain), so the lineage-reading of succession runs one way. And the framework's single most consequential exchange is verified where it can be: one conservation law, two real slices — a difference-form and a sum-form, hyperbolic and circular, swapped by the substitution physics calls Wick rotation. The physics chain's flagship stands on that: the Lorentzian signature of spacetime and the Euclidean signature of statistical mechanics as *the two slices of one law*, the light cone as the null set of the difference-slice, boosts as the slice's own symmetries. Space-like and time-like are not two substances; they are the two signs one quadratic form takes, and the cone is their boundary.

Now the new material, stamped as such. This cycle proposed, and provisionally mapped, the **largest frame**: R₁, the first Recursive Frame, the one whose origin sits at the impossibility itself — the only frame that stands alone, parent-side not a parent but the non-option of the first essay. The claim is exactly as large as it sounds, which is why it is gated.

Two disciplines keep this essay from floating away. First, frames do not inflate into a single four-dimensional container: each frame carries its own coordinates, its own unit, its own reading of the shared law, and the relations between frames are lineage-relations, not positions in a master grid — there is no view from no frame, cosmological edition. What the essay asserts without hedging is only the shape of the offer — that the framework derives ordered, linear, one-way succession and a two-signature geometry from a conditional about non-termination, before any physics is consulted; and that when physics *is* consulted, its deepest structural commitments are already sitting where the derivation put them.
