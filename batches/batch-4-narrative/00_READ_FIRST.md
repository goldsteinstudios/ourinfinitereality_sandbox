## What each does

**01 — The True Punishment of Sisyphus.** A counter-myth: the curse was never the boulder, it was
the **promise that the summit means release**. Tight (~6 KB), finished, publishable as-is. Its
structural job is to convert "you can never reach the center" from a defeat into a description —
the same move the whole framework needs a reader to make, done in one page with no notation.
Companion piece if you want the argued version: `archive/rsm-versions/v0.971/089-sisyphus-breathmd.md`
(anchors on 反者道之動).

**02 — The Sailor and the Waves.** A woman rows toward a shore that never arrives; her hands go
burning → blisters → numbness → callus; she learns to hate the waves. Realized narrative fiction,
no framework vocabulary at all. The development transcript
(`archive/notes/misc/sailor_waves_chat.txt`, 114 KB) contains the authorial reading worth knowing
before you edit: *"she never goes into the water… she never fully dissolves into the system."*

**⚠ There is a longer, different Sailor** on branch `docset-pipeline`:
`sets/nlm-parables/02_the_sailors_paradox.md` (193 lines). It adds the **effort-cost divergence**
arc — *"Every correction required another correction… The closer she got to that elusive
equilibrium, the more energy it took to maintain it"* — and a collapse-as-revelation ending
(*"She waited for disaster. It didn't come."*). Not in this batch because you asked for the archive
copy, but it is the version with the structural argument in it. Decide which is the base before
editing; they are two different stories, not two drafts of one.

**03 — The Parable of the Unmeasurable Field (Kai).** Branch-only. A farmer inherits an infinitely
dense field and a single perfectly sharp **knife (為)**; between any two stalks there is always
another. The knife is a tool for the discrete meeting a world of the continuous, and it can only
fail — *"for every drop of water he bailed, the ocean seeped in behind him."* Kai's own body is the
obstruction (躬 crammed into the 穴 that must stay open — exhaustion 窮 as "not a lack of energy,
but a surplus of obstruction"). The turn comes from a **hawk**, which holds position not by
stopping but by orbiting an unoccupiable center: *"The hawk wasn't measuring the air; it was
referencing a center and moving."* Then he picks up his grandfather's curved tool.

This is the batch's keystone. It is the **scythe-not-knife** correction (Batch 3, doc 03) and the
**unoccupiable center** (Batch 2) delivered as a single story, and it ends on the line that makes
無為 teachable: *"the center does not need to be located; it needs to be referenced."*

**04 — The Seven Dialogues.** Laozi and Einstein, seven exchanges, each pinned to a structural
transition (Dialogue One: V₀ → O₁) with Chinese text, pinyin, and the matching physics. The
role-inversion is the good idea: **Einstein as the sage** (knows, cannot explain — bound by
finitude), **Laozi as the master** (knows *and* explains, through paradox). Quarry, if you want
more: `archive/notes/ai-sessions/conversations_with_laozi_8.11.25.txt` (142 KB, unedited).

## Suggested order

**03 → 01 → 02 → 04.** Kai first (it teaches the move), Sisyphus second (it removes the false
promise), the Sailor third (it shows the cost of not learning), the Dialogues last (they name what
the first three demonstrated). 04 is the only one with notation and should stay last for that
reason.

- **01, 02, 04 are Jan 2026** — pre-v7.7, so they predate everything in the current typing. But

  re-type. 04 is the exception (V₀, O₁ and the physics pairings are explicit-register claims under
  v9.3 §0, and `V₀` is old vocabulary — the current object is P₀, and it is **not** "the void").
- **The one real doctrinal check:** all four run on "the center that is never reached." Under v9.3
  that splits into **Oₙ** (co-vanishing, bent around) and **Pₙ** (the balance paradox, which also
  does not obtain). In narrative this distinction will usually not surface — and it does not need
  to. Just don't let a story assert that something *is* at the center, or that anything was
  *removed* from it. The vortex guard applies: nothing was taken out; the exclusion is
  constitutive, not historical.
