## The headline: 02 is already v9.3's §0

**`packed-hanging` is the register split, discovered independently and taught better.** v9.3's most
load-bearing addition — the §0 implicit/explicit division that now heads every chain — is this
document's entire subject, arrived at through a party decoration:

> Those paper honeycomb decorations that come flat in a sleeve. The structure is already there.
> All of it. Every fold, every glue point. The 3D form doesn't get created when you open it.
> **The 3D form was always present — folded.** You just give it room.

> **常 = Packed Flat.** Not "eternal," not "constant." Implicit. Complete but compressed.
> **可 = Hanging.** Not merely "expressible." Explicit. The same structure given room.
> These aren't opposites. They're **compression states of the same structure.**

And the payoff on the opening line: *道可道非常道 — "The pattern unfolded is not the pattern
folded. Same structure. Different compression state."*

Why this matters: v9.3 says 可 and 常 *are* the explicit and implicit registers and that
道可道非常道 "declares this and nothing else." This document reached the same place from a
decoration, and it dissolves the 2,500-year translation problem it names — if 常/可 are compression
states rather than temporal qualities, **there is no unbridgeable gap** between the real Dao and
anything sayable. That's the teaching move the chains don't have.

**It also self-corrects, and keep the correction.** A "Critical Correction" section retracts the
honeycomb's implication that the structure expands into pre-existing room:

> The structure doesn't expand INTO pre-existing room — it **creates the space it expands into**.
> Think of a whirlpool: the water doesn't find an empty hole and fill it. The spinning water
> CREATES the void at the center by its own motion.

That reframes 無 (not pre-existing room, but what structure's own operation produces) and
為無為 (enact the void-creation without adding extra). Displayed corrections are one of the
project's best rhetorical assets — this is one of the good ones.

## The rest

**01 — The Guided Walk** (branch-only, 23 KB). The register split taught **kinesthetically**: a
60-minute suburb → woods → suburb walk in four quadrants (妙 of 可 → 妙 of 常 → 徼 of 常 → 徼 of 可),
21 stations, each mapped to a chapter. The pedagogy is stated outright: *"Layers come off without
announcement on the way out. Layers go back on explicitly on the way back. The reader feels the
difference in their legs."* Best line: *"The suburb is a standing wave of effort. The moment the
effort stops, the pattern collapses."* Its thesis — *"We thought it was philosophy. It was always
a walk"* — is a whole-book framing option, not just a chapter.

**03 — Scythe, not knife.** Short and load-bearing. 利 is the **π-operation** (arc, circulation),
not linear cutting: *"You cannot harvest a field with a knife."* This correction has already
propagated into the Ch. 11 site copy and the DDJ chapter pages, so it is effectively canon in the
public layer — worth keeping consistent.

**04 — The loom.** *"The loom doesn't illustrate the axiom system. The loom IS the axiom system,
running in thread and wood."* Warp/weft (經/緯) as the orthogonality requirement; derives i
(rotation past an unoccupiable midpoint), π (closure), ⊥, and e from one premise. **Caution:**
v9.3 holds that **"orthogonal" is a rendering fact** — the skeleton says *independent*, and
perpendicularity enters only with the measure, which is still an open item. The loom's
perpendicularity claim needs re-typing to the explicit register; it is exactly what the chart test
was built to catch.

**05 — The Technology Index** (your requested finding aid). *"The DDJ is a navigation system. Each
craft has its entry point into the same geometry."* Ten crafts, each with core principle, entry
chapters, what the tool teaches, and **what is readable on the tool's surface**: Weaving (糸),
Metallurgy (金), Archery (弓), Hydraulics (氵), Agriculture (禾), Ceramics (土), Carpentry (木),
Optics (勺), Cooking (火), Navigation (舟).

**Use it as a commissioning brief, not just an index.** The index covers ten crafts; the written
metaphor cluster covers a different, overlapping set. **Five indexed crafts have no essay yet** —
**archery, ceramics, carpentry, cooking, navigation** — and each already has its principle, entry
chapters, and teaching points drafted. The bow in particular is sitting there unwritten: *"the
string bridges the void without occupying it… 玄 = the operational paradox of
connected-not-touching."* Conversely, `helix` and `packed-hanging` are in the cluster but are not
crafts, so they don't appear in the index at all.
