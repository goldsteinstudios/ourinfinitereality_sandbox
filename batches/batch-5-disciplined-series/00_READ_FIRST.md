## Why these four essays are the batch

Each is a **different kind** of intellectual honesty, and that variety is the point:

- **01 (essay 10, the tree)** — a *correction* essay. The tree is the register's home because the
  tree **corrected the framework**: the drafting put the generative engine at the pith, and the
  botany refused. Pith is dead tissue — a point in cross-section, an axis in the standing whole,
  the **unmet** center in both readings; the generative layer is the **cambium**. Canonical
  mapping: pith :: Oₙ · cambium :: Gₙ · medullary rays :: Bₙ · branch node :: Pₙ. Also carries the
  **tree/whirlpool pairing** (girth × height in wood, speed × radius in water — one law, two
  registers) and the guard that matters: *the vortex is not a river with a tree removed. Nothing
  was taken out. The hole in the water was never filled.*
- **02 (essay 12, energy)** — a *refusal* essay, "different in kind from the others, and says so up
  front." It publicly retires the **energy-as-misalignment-cost** through-line because "cost" is
  agentive and invents a debtor, then attempts the replacement in permitted vocabulary and lands it
  at `[candidate]` — and leaves it there. Displacement from the seat, a magnitude, not a price.
- **03 (essay 14, dimensionality)** — an *open-problem* essay that **ends without concluding, on
  purpose**. "The second dimension is a theorem; the third is a contest; and the difference between
  those two sentences is what intellectual honesty looks like in public." Two rival derivations for
  three, stated side by side, unadjudicated, with the temptation to announce "three-dimensionality
  derived!" specifically and repeatedly refused.
- **04 (essay 15, pi)** — the *curing room*: everything held at `[observation]` grade by ruling.
  π as the exchange rate between the way around and the untakable way through; the Archimedean
  demonstration as the sturdiest item on the shelf — each polygon a frame, exact at its own
  resolution, π the lineage-invariant "no polygon states and every polygon bounds." It declines to
  promote the one item that is closest, *"because 'close' is the curing room's most dangerous
  word."*

If the book needs to demonstrate that the project can lose an argument, **02 and 03 are the
evidence**, and they are more persuasive than any result.

## The three long-form companions

- **05 — Why Pi Starts at Three** (32 KB). Self-contained, publishable, and needs *no* framework
  knowledge to enter: *"Almost nobody asks: why does it start at three?"* The natural popular
  companion to 04 — and note the two overlap on Archimedes, so decide which one owns that material.
- **06 — CAVP long-form** (30 KB). The only narrative exposition of **constant accuracy, variable
  precision** (accuracy belongs to structure, precision to description). CAVP is load-bearing
  across every later chain but is stated nowhere else at length; the introduction in Batch 1 uses
  it in one paragraph.
- **07 — The Tree essay** (13 KB, Jul 2025). The most thorough worked biological example — but see
  the conflict immediately below.

## ⚠ 01 and 07 contradict each other. Read 01 first.

**`07_tree_essay_updated.md` types the pith as `P₁` — "the Paradox Center."** That is the reading
the botany refused, and **01 in this same batch records the refusal.** Under canon the pith is the
**unmet** center (Oₙ); the paradox seat is the **branch node** (Pₙ); the cambium is the gradient
(Gₙ). 07 does get cambium ≈ Gₙ right in a later section, so the document is half-corrected and
half not — which is worse than uniformly old, because it reads as consistent.

Do not merge 07 into 01. Either re-type 07's center mapping wholesale, or use it only for the
material 01 doesn't cover (cellular/molecular scale, the growth equations) and let 01 own the
typing. The correction itself is worth *displaying* rather than silently fixing — a source with no
stake in the framework forced it, which is exactly the kind of resistance the project counts as
evidence.
