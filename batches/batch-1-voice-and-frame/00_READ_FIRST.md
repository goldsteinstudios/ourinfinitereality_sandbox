## What each one is actually for

**01 — the introduction.** The cold open, in first person: *"I'm a stay-at-home dad and a
woodworker. I have no academic credentials in any of the fields this project touches."* Then the
question, posed as an ask rather than an assertion — *"I'd ask you to actually sit with it for a
second rather than skip past it."* Also carries the three-instrument frame (math / physics /
ancient text, kept strictly separate), the *what this is not* section (not a religion, not a ToE,
not finished), the CAVP principle in plain words, and the "enter anywhere / no wrong door" close.
This is the warmest voice in the corpus and the only one that says the credentials problem out
loud before a reader can raise it.

**⚠ Use 01b as your base, not 01.** They differ in exactly two sentences, and `2` is the later,
more careful pass:
- "A few years ago I asked myself…" → **"At some point I asked myself…"** (drops a false precision)
- "a 2,400-year-old Chinese text" → **"a roughly 2,300-year-old Chinese text"** (matches the
  Guodian ~300 BCE dating used everywhere else in canon; 2,400 was wrong)

**02 — the three contemplations.** Written explicitly *for the landing page* — "Three icons. Any
order. No obligation." **The Bundle** (one cut through an infinite thread bundle reveals a void
that was always there), **The Wind** (breath is inaudible until a flute shapes it into a note —
the project's own predicament: you can only hear the instruments, never the wind), and **The Toss**
(winnowing as two operations in one gesture — the crashing breaks the chaff loose, the wind carries
it away). Then a bridge line, then the reveal *as a footnote, not a climax*: the three correspond to
the first three operational positions on the Guodian strips.

This is the highest reuse-per-word document in the archive, it was built for exactly this job, and
it has never been used. Note the structural move it teaches: **the DDJ arrives as a footnote after
the reader has already felt the shape.** That ordering decision is worth carrying into the book
whole.

**03 — the front-page spine.** Nine steps (0–8 + Landing), each with the *logical move*, draft
prose, the canon citation it's faithful to, and a `[RULE]` flag where it needs your hand. This is
the argument-order document. Key things it has already settled and that you should decide whether
to keep:

- **"One bet, then commit."** All the doubt lives at the premise (Step 0). Past that door the prose
  commits to the logic — every downstream claim reads *"given the premise, this follows,"* never
  *"this might be true."* This is what lets it be fully confident and fully honest at once.
- **The tenseless rule.** P₀'s incoherence is not an event. "First / begins / bootstraps / switched
  on / for one instant" are struck throughout; every touched step carries a `[DE-TENSED]` flag.
  Open question inside it (R-C): does tenselessness bind the experiential steps (5–6) too, or may
  circulation imagery move? Proposed split: skeleton claims tenseless, renderings may move.
- **R-A, the biggest open call:** the **deep spine** (start from the impossibility, Steps 1–3) vs.
  the **intuitive spine** (start at "you can never reach the center, so things circle," Steps 5–6).
  The draft recommends deep, paced gently. This is the single largest structural decision in the
  batch and it is still unruled.
- **`[TRAP AVOIDED]` at Step 6** — the best rotation prose in the corpus (the "measurement crisis /
  where am I exactly" passage) is deliberately *not* used, because it is the circular v5 argument.
  Worth knowing so it doesn't get re-imported by someone mining the old essays.
- Best inherited images: **the spring coil** (Step 5) and **"keep it on your left and keep going"**
  (Step 6).

**04 — One Distinction.** The whole framework in fourteen sections, plain language, audited against
its own rules. This is the **epistemic register** the book has to live up to. Two things make it the
most important document in the batch:

1. **The preface states provenance first**, and argues that under single authorship *agreement is
   cheap* — a model prompted with the framework will find it coherent, and that assent carries no
   evidential weight. What counts is **resistance**.
2. **§XIII "What was given up"** is a plain list of twelve surrendered arguments, offered as *the
   evidence*: "A framework that could not lose any of these arguments could not win any of them
   either." §XII "What is owed" lists the debts with the same care as the results.

Also note the once-issued disclaimer in the preface — *"The exposition is ordered. The obtaining is
not"* — which governs the whole document and is repeated as the last line. If the book takes one
sentence from this batch as its governing rule, that's the candidate.

## ⚠ Version-era conflicts — read before editing

All four predate current canon. **Canon is v9.3** (PDF-only; markdown transcription now at
`rsm/canonical/chains/v9.3/transcription_2026-08/`). 01/01b and 04 are **v7.6/v7.7-era**, 03 is
built against `structural_v7.7_r3.md`, 02 is v5.5-era but almost register-free so it ages best.

Where they now conflict with v9.3 — these are the edits to make, not defects in the writing:

| In these docs | v9.3 says |
|---|---|
| **04 §III:** "Absolute indistinction — written P₀" | **P₀ is *not* indistinction.** It's the master pair (distinction/indistinction) *at equality and cancellation*; indistinction is only one side. This is the biggest single re-typing to apply. |
| **04 §VIII "The joint"** (a whole section) | **"The joint" was struck** as a surplus noun — Pₙ is a paradox, not a place. "The reciprocity joint" → **"the reciprocity requirement."** |
| **04 §X "The two tests"** | v9.3 **removed both as named tests**; the grammar test survives as a standing list, the chart test as §6's "Rendering guard." (Keep applying them — just don't call them *the two tests*.) |
| **04 §XII debt: "The involution"** | **Closed in v9.2** — mode-swap `σ_B` structural, sign-flip `ν` explicit. That debt is paid; the section overstates what's open. |
| **04 §XII debt: "Priority and uniqueness of the first distinction"** | **Dissolved in v9.2** — withdrawn, not answered. §2 now reads "at least one is what the impossibility gives. Nothing here counts the roots, and nothing here excludes more than one." |
| **03 Step 5:** center = "the thing that can't exist" | v9.3 splits three loci: **Oₙ** co-vanishing locus · **Bₙ** balance line (no point obtains along its whole length) · **Pₙ** balance paradox. The spine collapses these into one "center." |
| **03 / 04:** "met at resolution" framing | **Struck by ruling.** Pₙ *does not obtain*; what a frame has is a **resolution limit** (indiscriminability), not a locus reached. |
| **04:** status tags in prose | v9.3 **removed the epistemic tag apparatus entirely**. `[G]`/`[R]` survive for manuscript strata; `[derived]`/`[candidate]`/etc. do not. |

**New in v9.3 that this batch predates entirely:** the **§0 implicit/explicit register split** — the
single most load-bearing addition, and it heads every chain. Since 04 is the document that states
the framework in plain language, §0 is the thing it most needs added. In DDJ terms it's already
here: 可 and 常 *are* the explicit and implicit registers, and 道可道非常道 declares that and
nothing else.

## Suggested order of work

1. **01b first** — settle the voice (first person, credentials-forward, asks rather than asserts).
2. **04 next** — settle the epistemic register (provenance first, surrender ledger, "the exposition
   is ordered, the obtaining is not"). Apply the v9.3 re-typings above while you're in it.
3. **03 third** — settle argument order. Rule **R-A** (deep vs. intuitive spine); that decision
   propagates to everything downstream.
4. **02 last** — it's the on-ramp, and it should be fitted to whatever 03 decides. Its footnote-
   not-climax handling of the DDJ reveal is probably a rule for the whole book, not just the page.
