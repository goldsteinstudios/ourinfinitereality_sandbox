## What each contributes

**01 — The Bridge.** The most developed standalone thought experiment in the corpus (21 KB).
A reader stands on a stone arch bridge: *"You're standing on a structure held up by something
that isn't there."* Runs obstacle-you-cannot-cross → the arc → the excluded center, and reaches
for quantum mechanics from an engineering solution 2,000 years old. This is the **entry** version
of the center — concrete, physical, no notation.

**02 — The Unpluggable Hole.** The best single metaphor document anywhere in the project, and it
has **never been on `research-archive`** — it lives only on the `sphere_model` branch. Structure:
try to stand at the geometric center of a wheel → the hose that narrows → the plug problem →
compression and expansion as the two failure directions → the hollow tree → the valley. Its
strongest move is the **four graded impossibilities**:

> Physically impossible (instruments fail) · Intellectually impossible (concepts fail) ·
> Epistemologically impossible (knowledge fails) · Ontologically impossible (being fails) —
> **but logically necessary.** The system presupposes what it cannot contain.

And the line the batch is built around: *"The hollow isn't damage. It's requirement."* /
**"The impossibility is the engine."**

**03 — Motion Unification.** Carries the circulation-metaphor set in one place: bellows + vortex +
gyroscope/spinning top + pendulum.

**04 — The Helix.** Short and dense. The **rain chain** — water spiralling "around the absence of a
downspout"; the path orbits nothing, and *because* of that nothing the water arrives. Then the
taijitu re-read as a **2D projection of a helix** (the two fish aren't swimming in a circle,
they're corkscrewing through depth — we flattened it and forgot), and DNA as two strands orbiting
a shared empty axis, each encoding its complement.

## How they fit together

There is a natural ladder here, and it is probably the chapter order:

1. **01 Bridge** — you are already standing on one (recognition, no theory)
2. **02 Unpluggable Hole** — why you *cannot* stand at the center (the four impossibilities)
3. **03 Motion** — therefore everything that persists circulates (the mechanism, many instances)
4. **04 Helix** — and the circulation has depth, not just rotation (the shape, and the correction
   to a symbol everyone thinks they know)

04 also hands directly to **Batch 3**: it closes by mapping 常 = the helix structure (implicit,
complete) / 可 = the water flowing (explicit, manifesting) / 無 = the empty axis — explicitly
citing "the packed/hanging discovery," which is Batch 3's second document. The two batches are
joined at that seam.
