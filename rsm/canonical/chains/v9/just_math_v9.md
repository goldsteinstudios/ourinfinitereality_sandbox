## The reciprocity joint

1. **Co-obtaining (P2).** Neither mode reaches a terminal while the other obtains.

3. **Class selection** *[verified]*. Additive conservation fails twice: it admits X = 0 (a terminal as an attainable value) and it caps both modes (contradicting P1's vastness clause). Multiplicative conservation does neither: XY = 1ₙ admits no zero and no ceiling.

## The one law and its two slices

Over ℂ with z = a + bi, one equation Q(z) = 1ₙ carries two real slices:

- **Q_i = a² + b²** — the circle slice (the orbit register's invariant).
- **Q_j = a² − b²** — the gradient slice, with split unit j, j² = +1; in split coordinates a = (X+Y)/2, b = (X−Y)/2, the gradient XY = 1ₙ is exactly Q_j = 1ₙ.

The two slices meet tangentially at the seats and only there. On the Q1 branch, a ≥ √1ₙ — the **Amplitude Floor**: the branch never reaches the circle's lower range, and the full orbit is not mode-expressible. The two slices are two readings of one law; neither substitutes for the other.

**Scale and lineage, split.** Within a frame, scale is motion along G: the split action e^{jφ} — hyperbolic rotation — is the orbit of rescalings (λX, Y/λ), product invariant. The family parameter c = XY names the **lineage** direction along B: c→0 child-ward, c→∞ parent-ward along a lineage, root-capped (the ground has no parent); a lineage coordinate, never a global count. The lineage direction is not scale. *[derived]*

**The two non-terminations exchange, pivoting on the seat** *[verified]*: the inversion x ↦ 1ₙ/x — σ_B on G — swaps the divisible center-ward direction and the vast extension; its fixed point is √1ₙ. Both non-terminations are the gradient's own ends, within the frame.

## Theorems

**T1 — disconnection** *[verified]*. In the gradient slice, the level set Q_j = 1ₙ has two components; Pₙ and ν(Pₙ) lie in different components, and no path within the slice joins them. As topology this is unconditional. Its interpretation — whether the components *must* be bound (through the circle slice: the door) or whether the second component is the chart's image of the first — is exactly open item 10.

**T2 — crossing** *[verified]*. Any closed spanning path crosses the null cone Q_j = 0; at the conjugate seats the gradient form reads −1ₙ (the toll). The null cone is the two directions a = ±b; the gradient approaches and never meets its asymptotes.

**The two returns** *[verified]*. Along the circle, Q_j = 1ₙ·cos 2θ: the invariant returns at π (return-of-structure, landing on the conjugate), the position at 2θ = 4π i.e. 2π (return-of-place). A double cover; two distinct returns.

**i² = −1, two routes** *[verified; both conditional on the binding requirement — item 10]*. By elimination over the five candidate closures, and by the antipodal-binding argument (the conjugate pair {+1, −1} joined continuously only around, never through). The four-cycle {1, i, −1, −i} is the minimal closure carrying the binding.

**The parturition map** *[verified]*. w = z² carries the parent's orbit register to the child's: 2-to-1, ν-identified — the branch and its ν-image read as one structure in the child's register; the seat maps to the child's origin. O₍ₙ₊₁₎ reads Pₙ, as constitution, not site-selection.

## The engine (realized)

The demand: continuity entails the balance locus; (√1ₙ, √1ₙ) is on the curve, and balance-at-frame-resolution obtains — equal magnitudes is not collapse. Denial A (origin): one constitutive pair per frame; n has no spare pair to grant the locus. Satisfaction as organizing-locus: in-frame, circulation (the spanning structure winds the locus; traversal realizes it as ordered passage; motion only after succession); trans-frame, generation — **the child is the pursuit of exactness continuing below the floor**, its origin the approached locus read from below, realized by the parturition map. Recursion is exactness, permanently deferred. *[derived]*
