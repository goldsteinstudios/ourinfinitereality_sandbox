# RSM — The Structural Chain (v9)

*Logic only. Status tags mark epistemic standing, not history: [derived] follows from the premises; [candidate] proposed, not forced; [open] named and unresolved; [unaudited] machine-closed, awaiting the author's adversarial read. History lives in the editorial record, not here. Not sealed.*

## Premises

**P1 — the Conditional.** Reality is infinitely vast and infinitely divisible: no terminal resolution in either direction — no edge outward, no floor inward.

**P2 — the Bridge.** What obtains is distinguishable. Contrapositively: what is not distinguishable does not obtain.

Everything below is what these two premises jointly require. Where a further assumption operates, it is named in the open items.

**Definition (frame).** A frame Rₙ is the coinherence of one distinction: two conjugate modes, their reciprocal relation, their balance, and their origin — arriving together or not at all. One constitutive pair per frame.

**Corollary.** Every distinction constitutes a frame; distinction beneath a frame's unit is therefore another frame, not a smaller item within this one.

## 1. The total paradox: P₀

P₀ is the total paradox: the condition in which distinction and indistinction — the master conjugate pair, each constituted by the other — stand equal and cancel. It is not indistinction: indistinction is one side of the pair, and P₀ privileges neither side; it is the pair's collapse. Its presenting faces are **|0|** (the collapse via emptiness — nothing to distinguish) and **|1|** (the collapse via fullness — nothing standing out; the bars guard the reading: never the numeral, never any frame's unit 1ₙ). One pair at equality within a frame is a Pₙ — occupiable at resolution, because the frame's other distinctions carry the load. P₀ is the case with no frame and no load-bearer: every pair at equality at once, including the pair that every pair presupposes. By P2 it cannot obtain — at the total cancellation nothing is distinguishable — and it cannot even be asserted without contradiction, since stating the equality distinguishes its terms. **P₀ and Pₙ are one species — paradox, a conjugate pair at equality — at two scopes.** The two faces map onto P1's two directions: infinite divisibility excludes |0|; infinite vastness excludes |1|. *[derived]*

**Face-unity.** The two faces are the two counterfactual terminals of one journey along the gradient: toward all-form-no-void and toward all-void-no-form. Neither is reached; the journey is one. *[derived]*

## 2. The ground

Because P₀ cannot obtain, at least one distinction obtains — an origin, a frame: O₁, R₁. The ground relation is the joint relation at zero scope: every origin is a paradox read from below, and O₁ reads P₀. Because P₀ is unframed, R₁ has no parent: the tree is rooted. The ground's mechanism (identity through opposition — required directly by the impossibility) differs from every later frame's (generation at a joint); both are real, and the structure needs both. *[derived; priority and uniqueness of the first distinction: open]*

**Where the two infinities live.** Vastness is in-frame: each mode's reach along the gradient is unbounded. Divisibility is across-frame: the descent at the joints, inward only. The two non-terminations are the two ends of the one journey along the gradient, not two directions of the nesting. *[derived]*

## 3. The coinherence

- A distinction entails its two co-obtaining modes (X, Y) — one distinction, two modes, each constituted by the other; nothing prior was divided.

- The modes are **magnitudes**, not signed quantities: the conjugate of a mode is another mode, not its negative. The sign belongs to the chart — an orientation of reading. (The structural status of the signed register is a standing open item; see the math chain.)

- A mode can be noticed without its conjugate having been measured; its **amount** exists only as a ratio to its conjugate. Existence-dependence is not measurement-dependence.

- Both modes are measured from one reference — the point at which both would vanish. That is the origin O: a locus, never a position; nothing stands there.

## 4. The frame; the unit; the floor

**1ₙ is the constitutive distinction's own magnitude** — the conserved product of *the* pair that constitutes frame n — and simultaneously the frame's minimal unit: nothing beneath the constitutive magnitude obtains *within* frame n, because any distinction beneath it is, by the Corollary, another frame. "Sub-1ₙ" is unit-relative typing, not size: magnitude comparison across frames is not defined, and the child is not smaller — the child is the approach that does not finish, continuing. The floor is the boundary of the frame's own definition, not an added restriction. *[derived given the reciprocity joint]*

**The law.** The two modes' amounts are locked: their product is conserved, X·Y = 1ₙ. A conserved sum fails twice — it admits the terminals (one mode at nothing, the other at everything: P₀'s faces as attainable values) and it caps both modes, contradicting infinite reach. The product's curve has no endpoints and no ceilings; the exclusion of the impossible is the law's own shape, not a rule imposed on it. The native coordinates are fixed by the impossibility itself: they are the ones in which P₀'s faces sit at *nothing* and *without limit*. The constant is the unit, and the unit is the frame's reality: things differ in their proportion of the modes; reality is the invariant the proportions hold. *[derived; the joint's full derivation is realized in the math chain; its native-magnitudes step is [unaudited]]*

**Scale.** Within a frame, scale is motion along the curve: rescaling one mode by λ and the other by 1/λ, product invariant — the complete set of rescalings of one reality. No rescaling reaches the center. Scale is within-frame; the lineage is not scale. *[derived]*

## 5. The two centers

- **O = the co-vanishing locus** (unmet in its own frame's reading). O is where both modes would reach operational absence together; nothing on the curve approaches it — the curve's nearest locus to the center is the balance. The curve bends around O: the connection between the mode-dominant extremes is the curve itself, and "no through, only around" is a description of it, not an added rule. *[derived]*

- **P = the balance paradox.** By continuity the balance locus is entailed, and balance *at frame resolution* obtains: equal amounts of the two modes is not the modes becoming one. What cannot obtain is **exactness** — equality carried through every depth is a terminal resolution, and P1 denies terminals; beneath any frame's floor the equality opens into finer-grained imbalance the frame cannot state. And P cannot serve as *frame n's origin*: one constitutive pair per frame, and n has no spare pair to grant it. Met at resolution; no exactness anywhere; no origin here. *[derived]*

- "Met"/"unmet" are frame-reading statuses only; occupancy language does not type these loci.

## 6. The engine

The demand: continuity entails the balance locus, and balance-at-resolution obtains — the practical register is real. Denial A (origin): the locus cannot be frame n's origin. Denial B (exactness): exact balance cannot obtain. Must exist; met at resolution; no origin here; no exactness anywhere — simultaneously, locally. The demand is satisfied in the one mode of existence left to such a center: **existence-as-organizing-locus** — in-frame, as the locus circulation organizes around; trans-frame, as the origin a new structure organizes around. **The child frame is the pursuit of exactness, continuing below the floor:** the residue of imbalance beneath 1ₙ — the not-quite-balance the frame cannot state — is sub-unit distinction, hence a frame, whose origin is this very locus read from below. Recursion is exactness, permanently deferred. *[derived]*

**Rendering guard.** A drawing shows a smooth curve through an unproblematic point; the constitution-layer facts (no origin here; no exactness anywhere) have no rendering. The drawing's verdict never overwrites the constitution's.

## 7. Recursion

One locus, two frame-readings: a point in the parent's register, an origin in the child's — O₍ₙ₊₁₎ reads Pₙ. Nothing in the parent selects the child's orientation: a preference would be a distinction, and the parent has nothing to constitute it with. The joint, read as potential, is therefore a **ring** of orientations, no one privileged; what obtains is always one selection (all at once would be saturation), and different selections are different branches. The nesting is a tree, not a ladder — and a rooted tree: index-relationality holds within it (every frame reads itself as "n"; the labels are gauge), but no relabeling makes the root a child, because the root's parent-side is P₀, an impossibility, not a frame. *[derived; branching at loci other than P: open]*

## 8. The two tests

Every claim passes two tests before it enters.

**The chart test:** does it survive changing the drawing? Perpendicularity, straightness-as-driver, ambient space for the sphere, the sign on a mode — each failed it; each was a fact about a presentation.

**The grammar test:** does the word's grammar assert what the framework denies? Agentive verbs for structural facts, transfer-language for non-events, removal narratives for constitutive exclusions, staging conjunctions for simultaneous constitution, reified infinities. The strike list is this test in standing form.

Both failures look like clarity from the inside; that is why the tests are written down.
