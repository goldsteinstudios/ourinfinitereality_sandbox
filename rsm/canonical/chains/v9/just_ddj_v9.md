# RSM — Just-DDJ Chain (v9)

*Correspondence register. `::` denotes imperfect structural correspondence — never identity, translation-equivalence, or congruence; substitution is forbidden. Stratum tags: **[G]** attested in the Guodian bundles (~300 BCE); **[R]** received-text or Mawangdui only.*

**Evidential posture.** The DDJ is generatively upstream of the framework — the author built the framework through the text — so agreement between them carries no public evidential weight. The text is the subject of a translation, not a witness; this chain's success criterion is translation-fidelity, independent of the derivation's criterion. What the text *can* supply as evidence is resistance: manuscript readings that refuse a correspondence, and preregistered tests scored against the strips.

**The instrument.** Text and notation are two compressions with non-overlapping failure modes; each audits the other. Text→math at the notation layer (two lexical items against one symbol: flag a candidate distinction; the math must then verify with its own resources) and math→commentary at the reading layer (structural coherence discriminates rival glosses) are legitimate. Text→math at the premise layer and math→text at the attestation layer are forbidden: no character enters a derivation; no structure overrides the strips.

## The referent: 道

道 :: **the recursive structural model itself** — the structure entailed by P₀'s standing non-obtaining ("P₀ has never obtained, does not obtain, and will never obtain" — three tenses as the in-frame rendering of a tenseless modal fact; the text's tenseless grammar states it natively). **常道 :: the entailment as such, unindexed. 可道 :: any framed statement or traversal of it.** Consequences: 道法自然 — a structure grounded in an impossibility needs nothing to hold it up; an impossibility cannot lapse, be consumed, or require maintenance (谷神不死, 用之不勤 as entailments, not praise). In Chapter 25's dual styling, 道 is the model and 大 its first instance, R₁.

## The opening: 道可道非常道 [R — Ch. 1 not in Guodian]

Heading-and-theorem: the frame-relative is not the frame-independent. ∀X: 可X 非 常X.

- **常** :: frame-independent, parameter-free (register marker; not "eternal"). The oldest stratum reads 恆 (persisting-across); see the checksum item on 恆/常.

- **可** :: frame-relative, parametric, indexed within the frame. (The modes are magnitudes; the sign is orientation-of-reading. This is the text's own testimony — its conjugate pairs are co-present magnitudes throughout, and no negative quantities appear anywhere in it.)

- **非** :: the divergence of two registers around a shared invariant. In the only copular uses the text contains (Ch. 1's two), 非 stands between two readings of one term — 可道 非 常道 — and its middle is the invariant both flanks share. The survey: 非 is rare (on the order of six tokens in five chapters); the non-copular tokens are rhetorical (Ch. 7), purposive (Ch. 65), class-negation (Ch. 31), predicative (Ch. 53); 是非 as a compound does not occur; **no token negates between conjugate opposites.** *[survey run against the corpus; Guodian graphs to be verified against facsimiles]* Which involution 非's structure ultimately tracks is the standing involution item (math open item 9; structural item 6).

**Line 2: 名可名非常名.** 常名 :: XY = 1, the unindexed law; 可名 :: XₙYₙ = 1ₙ, the same law indexed. The 可/常 split applied to the law itself. *[candidate]*

**Lines 3–4.** The Mawangdui manuscripts read 萬物 in both clauses (the Shiji quotes the line that way; 天地 is the received substitution). One referent, twice related: **始 and 母 are the two relations** the unnamed and the named bear to the ten thousand things — what conditions them and what keeps constituting them — i.e., the derivation's two mechanisms: identity-through-opposition at the ground; generation-at-the-joint thereafter. 始 :: Oₙ (the unmet origin, prior to any position); 母 :: Pₙ (the generative persisting locus, the parent-side of 生). Shared 女 :: the center-role genus; different determinatives :: the two species. *[R; convergence, P2]*

**The methodology lines** (常無欲以觀其妙／常有欲以觀其徼) **[R]**: two observational vantages, one from each face, both required — the parallax method stated inside the text. 欲 :: directed orientation, zero agency; 妙 :: relational pattern (the 無-vantage); 徼 :: boundary (the 有-vantage). Philological exposure carried: this reading requires the split parsing (常無，欲…); the Mawangdui phrase-grouping leans toward the unit parsing (恆無欲…), and no older stratum arbitrates. *[falsifiable at the manuscript layer; cited only with this exposure attached]*

## 有無相生 [G — Ch. 2, slip A15; 亡 for 無; the shorter formulation]

有 :: the form mode; 無 :: the void mode — two co-present magnitudes with the gradient reading **有 × 無 = 1ₙ**. Neither reaches zero; each is constituted by the other; every position carries both. Void is not zero: zero is the center, where both modes would vanish; 無 as mode is a bounded, shaped, functional magnitude (Ch. 11: 有之以為利，無之以為用 — the conserved product in nine words). Guodian 亡 (relational absence) fits the mode reading better than received 無; the drift 亡→無 runs toward exactly the confusion this register discipline guards against.

Chapter 2 is also the method: no definitions, six conjugate pairs listed, each arising mutually, the one relation induced from six instances — how a mutually-constituted circle is entered, since it cannot be derived into from outside. The chapter with the oldest witness carries the structural heart; the staged cosmogony (Ch. 42) is absent from the strips.

## Chapter 40 [G — slip A37; the scored test]

The slip reads: 返也者道僮也／溺也者道甬也／天下之勿生於又生於亡。

- **The engine lines are definitions.** Both carry the 也者…也 formula — the classical definitional construction: "Returning — it is the pattern's movement; 溺-ing — it is the pattern's function." The received text strips the stamps to bare assertions; the 也-erosion, documented at the two most load-bearing lines in the text.

- **返** (return-in-motion, the walker radical), where the received has 反. 反/返 :: the turn along G around the unoccupiable center — circulation, the in-frame face of the engine. Which of 反/復 names return-of-structure (at π) versus return-of-place (at 2π) is a checksum item.

- **溺 — attested-uninterpreted.** The graph is 溺, not 弱 (the inventory's 弱 was the received normalization). The interpretive ladder is mandatory and unfilled: (1) 溺's Chu-period semantic range; (2) its local reading, framework-blind; (3) only then any correspondence. A37 is the highest-projection-risk location in the corpus. *[no :: assigned]*

- **The preregistered test, scored positive.** The preregistration: if the strip reads symmetrically (生於有，生於亡), the older text sits on the modal side and the received hierarchy (有生於無) is the later emendation. The transcription shows **no second 有**: born of 有, born of 亡 — the balanced parallel. The oldest witness reads co-emergence; the staged arrow was added in transmission. Threats to validity, carried: (i) haplography — a repeated 有 is the classic eye-skip environment; against it, the 3+3 balance, the definitional framing of the preceding lines, and Ch. 2's 相生 on the same bundle; (ii) the Mawangdui bracket — whether the silk texts carry the repetition dates the emendation; check the facsimiles before citing the date. Consequence: the oldest witness places this line on the modal side; 生 at this line reads as co-emergence, and the three-forms taxonomy below is untouched.

## The cluster: 玄／牝／玄牝 [G — 玄 attested, slip A08; 玄牝 R]

玄 :: Paradox :: Pₙ. 牝 :: generative. 玄牝 :: generative paradox :: P₀. The compound is compositional — the character for paradox plus the character for generative capacity computes the name of the generative paradox — so the assignment is motivated, not brute. The differentiator: every Pₙ has two faces (in-frame organizing locus; trans-frame seat); P₀, unframed, has no in-frame face — it is *purely* generative, and 牝 marks that purity (可以為天下母; 玄牝之門是謂天地根: the mother of the whole tree). 玄之又玄 :: **the species iterated** — paradox at every joint of the tree: the reduplication reads as the recursion itself. 眾妙之門 (Ch. 1) and 玄牝之門 (Ch. 6): 門 at exactly the two places the structure has gates — the recursion's and the ground's.

**The three generative forms**, one ratio, three mechanisms, never conflated: (1) entailment-生 :: the ground, P₀ : O₁ (道生一 — 道 is not a mother; P₀ has no prior state); (2) parturition-生 :: Pₙ → Pₙ, O₍ₙ₊₁₎ (severance with a persisting parent); (3) 相生 :: mutual constitution within the frame. 生 ≠ 相生; entailment-生 ≠ parturition-生.

## The count: 道生一，一生二，二生三，三生萬物 [R — Ch. 42 not in Guodian]

Exposition-order, never event-order. 一 :: the first distinction; 二 :: the two modes (nothing added — the two came with the one); **三 :: the pair plus its bond** — the text's own gloss in the next line, 沖氣以為和: the blend, not a third mode and not a third axis. The three's seat is the joint, and the joint is where children stand: 三生萬物 because only a held relation has an address. The chapter's staging is absent from the oldest witness; read as a timeline it re-imports what the strips refuse.

## 天地不仁; 守中 [mixed strata — only the bellows lines are strip-attested (A23)]

天地 :: Gₙ; 天地不仁 :: no preferred position along the gradient **[R]**. Extended to the tree: no preferred frame — every trunk is a branch of its parent and every branch a trunk to its children, save the root (the tree is rooted; O₁'s parent-side is P₀, an impossibility, not a frame; the ground is exempt by mechanism, not favored). The bellows (虛而不屈，動而愈出) :: the functional void, strip-attested **[G]**. 守中 :: Bₙ — the balance structure through both centers; descriptive, not prescriptive **[R]**.

## 弗居 [G — slip A17; the strongest strip-attested match in the chain]

成而弗居: completes, yet structurally-cannot-dwell. 成 :: met-at-resolution (the completion is real); 弗居 :: the origin-denial (弗, structural impossibility — exactly not 不, contingent negation). The engine's two-denial structure, in two characters, on a strip. 無為 :: aligned persistence; 為 :: effortful action under misalignment.

## 此兩者同出而異名 [R]

Same origin, different register — the slice identity is its sharpest reading: the gradient and the orbit are literally 同出 (one law) 而異名 (two slices, two names); 同謂之玄 — the shared origin is viewed at the seats, where the slices coincide: 玄's own locus. *[convergence remark; never flows backward as support]*

## The 法 cascade [G — slip A23]

人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然. 法 :: the child-parent structural relation at the joint. 道法自然 :: the terminus of the regress of grounds — the model takes its pattern from its own self-sufficiency, because beneath an impossibility there is nothing to stand on. 域中有四大: the fourth term 王 not yet derived. *[open]*

## The anthology as curriculum [G]

Bundle A's first unit is one physically continuous written entity (the text runs across slips; internal order secured): 19 → 66 → 46 → 30 → 15 → 64 → 37 → 63 → 2 → 32. It opens with 絕智棄辯 — discard cleverness and *disputation* (the Guodian wording; the received retargeting at Confucian virtues is the later layer) — passes through 為無為 (practice the paradoxical mode), and arrives at the six pairs (the structural primer). Entrance, practice, structure: the received arrangement opens by *declaring* the limit of the instrument (Ch. 1); the older arrangement opens by *commanding* its abandonment — and the instrument it names, 辯, is the one the circle at the base defeats. The unit-arrangement within the bundle is editorial; the sequence within the unit is physical. Section-break data adds: chapters 15→64 and 64→56 flow with no marker — originally continuous teaching.

## Verbal and stamp discipline

In prose without `::`, correspondence is "to me, that looks just like…," never "X is Y." 也 (the Guodian 也者…也 definitional stamp) :: the derivation's own ≡; the multi-century stripping of 也 (Guodian → Mawangdui → received) is the tradition treating theorems as observations, one copyist at a time — now documented at the engine lines themselves (Ch. 40, above).
