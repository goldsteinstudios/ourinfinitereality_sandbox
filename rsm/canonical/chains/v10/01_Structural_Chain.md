## 1. Infinite Reality, the Terminal Faces (|0| and |1|), and the Generative Paradox (P₀)

Begin with one foundational conditional:

> If Reality ($\mathcal{R}$) is infinitely vast and infinitely divisible, it has no final outside
> and no final inside. **[PREM]**

The antecedent already entails that Reality obtains; no separate existence premise is required.

Distinguish two relational roles:

$$ \mathsf{D} :: \text{distinction (Not-Void)}, \qquad \mathsf{I} :: \text{indistinction (Void)}. $$

Indistinction is coherent only relative to distinction: to state that terms are indistinct is
already to contrast that condition with their being distinguishable. Conversely, distinction is
coherent only relative to indistinction: a difference is stated against the reference of no
difference. Neither role is independently self-defining. **[RELATIONAL DEFINITION]**

Thus:

$$ \mathsf{D} \mathrel{\text{相}} \mathsf{I} \quad (\text{mutual definition}), \qquad \mathsf{D} \mathrel{\text{生}} \mathsf{I} \quad (\text{co-emergence}). \quad [\textbf{CORR}] $$

Let $P_0$ name their impossible coincidence — Absolute Indistinction:

$$ P_0: \quad \mathsf{D} = \mathsf{I} \quad (\text{玄牝 :: The Generative Paradox}). $$

At this condition, the contrast by which either role is defined disappears. Its two unreachable
terminal faces are:

- **Absolute Void** ($|0| :: \text{恆亡} :: \text{常無}$): Absolute absence, where nothing is
  distinguishable.
- **Absolute Not-Void** ($|1| :: \text{恆又} :: \text{常有}$): Absolute saturation, an undivided
  totality with no outside or internal openness by which anything could be distinguished.

Both $|0|$ and $|1|$ are examples of Absolute Indistinction ($P_0$). Because infinite Reality has
neither a final inside nor a final outside, neither terminal face can ever obtain. $P_0$ is
therefore the Generative Paradox (玄牝): it must be available as the limit-reference defining the
opposition (綿綿若存 — "seemingly there"), yet it cannot obtain as a state inside the frame of the
duality it defines without abolishing that duality. Because an impossibility requires zero effort
to remain unreached (用之不勤), the non-obtainment of $P_0$ effortlessly forces Reality to
articulate through distinction-in-relation:

$$ \mathcal{R}\text{ infinite inward and outward} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad P_0\text{ (}|0|\text{ and }|1|\text{) cannot obtain} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathsf{D}\mathrel{\text{相生}}\mathsf{I}. $$

When inquiry follows local paradox down into paradox infinitely ($P_n \to P_{n+1} \to P_{n+2}
\dots$) and never reaches a bottom, that bottomless fact is $P_0$ (玄牝).

## 2. The Infinite Gradient (Gₙ) and the Origin Reference (Oₙ)

Because neither terminal face $|0|$ (Absolute Void) nor $|1|$ (Absolute Not-Void) can obtain, what
must exist between them is the infinite asymptotic gradient:

$$ \text{Void} \longleftrightarrow \text{Not-Void} \quad :: \quad \text{天拖} \quad :: \quad \text{天地} \quad :: \quad G_1 \quad :: \quad XY = 1. $$

Select a distinction for inquiry and index its local frame by $n$:

$$ 1_n \neq 0_n, \qquad R_n := \operatorname{scope}(1_n \neq 0_n). \quad [\textbf{DEF}] $$

**The Origin Reference** ($0_n$ / $O_n :: \text{無名 [天地之始]}$): To obtain and measure the
contrast between Void and Not-Void along the gradient, the frame must have a shared reference from
which the two coordinate directions diverge:

$$ O_n = \{\mathrm{xAxis}_n \cap \mathrm{yAxis}_n\} = (0,0). $$

Globally, this initial framing is indexed at $n=1$. Generically, $O_n$ is the "no" in the contrast
between thing and no-thing. It is neither $P_0$ nor an occupied operative state; it is the origin
reference holding the coordinate axes in place.

**The Constitutive Unit and Gradient Curve** ($1_n$ / $G_n :: \text{有名 [萬物之母]}$): Held by
$O_n$, the reciprocal curve $G_n$ represents the frame's constitutive unit $1_n$, along which
emerge the infinite scattering articulations (萬) and the cutting boundaries (勿) that separate
them.

Loss of this particular distinction removes $R_n$ as that frame (as when a dead tree ceases to
maintain $1_{\mathrm{tree}} \neq 0_{\mathrm{tree}}$ and returns its timber to the forest) without
erasing unrestricted Reality $\mathcal{R}$.

## 3. Conjugate Modes, Homogeneous Squeeze, and the Scale Bridge

Let $X_n$ and $Y_n$ name the mutually determining directions through which $1_n$ is expressed. The
reciprocal class adds four quantitative hypotheses (**[DEF]**):

1. **Continuity:** The modes admit positive, comparable, continuously varying coordinates
   $(x_n, y_n) \in D_n = (0,\infty)^2$.
2. **Swap Symmetry:** Interchanging the modes via $\sigma_n(x_n,y_n) = (y_n,x_n)$ preserves the
   relation.
3. **Homogeneous Exchange:** Exchange is an orbit of the multiplicative squeeze action:
   $$ S_q(x_n, y_n) = (qx_n, q^{-1}y_n), \qquad q > 0. $$
4. **The Scale Bridge:** The intrinsic measure is scale-invariant: changing multiplicative scale
   changes the coordinate description without changing intrinsic distance. This connects
   ontological nonterminality ("no final inside or outside") to geometry by declaring that no
   multiplicative scale is metrically privileged.

Every squeeze orbit through $(x_0, y_0)$ satisfies $x_n y_n = x_0 y_0 = k$. A dimensionless
normalized chart $\chi_n(1_n) = 1$ sets $k = 1$:

$$ G_n: \quad x_n y_n = 1, \qquad (x_n, y_n) = (e^t, e^{-t}), \quad t \in \mathbb{R}. \quad [\textbf{MATH under DEF}] $$

On the open additive share interval $a+b=1$ ($a,b>0$), reciprocal odds $x=a/b, y=b/a$ satisfy
$xy=1$. What distinguishes the two charts is the measure: in the Euclidean measure on $a+b=1$, the
endpoints lie a finite distance away; under the logarithmic Scale Bridge measure, the terminal
limits $|0|$ and $|1|$ lie infinitely far away.

## 4. The Intrinsic Scale-Invariant Geometry (gₙ)

Adopt the quadratic Riemannian metric class (**[DEF]**). Introduce positive scale parameters
$\alpha_n, \beta_n > 0$ and define logarithmic ratio-product coordinates:

$$ u_{n+1} = \alpha_n \ln\frac{x_n}{y_n}, \qquad v_{n+1} = \beta_n \ln(x_n y_n). $$

In these coordinates, multiplicative scale transformations act by translations, so a
scale-invariant quadratic metric has constant coefficients. Mode swap $\sigma_n$ sends
$u_{n+1} \mapsto -u_{n+1}$ while preserving $v_{n+1}$, eliminating the mixed term
$du_{n+1}dv_{n+1}$. The positive-definite intrinsic metric on $D_n$ is uniquely forced to take the
form:

$$ g_n = \alpha_n^2\left(d\ln\frac{x_n}{y_n}\right)^2 + \beta_n^2\left(d\ln(x_n y_n)\right)^2 = du_{n+1}^2 + dv_{n+1}^2. \quad [\textbf{MATH}] $$

In the symmetric convention $\alpha_n = \beta_n = \frac{1}{2}$:

$$ g_n = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{dx_n^2}{x_n^2} + \frac{dy_n^2}{y_n^2}\right). $$

Under $g_n$, the boundary axes $x_n=0$ and $y_n=0$ and unbounded directions lie at infinite metric
distance. In the ordinary flat Euclidean drawing, $G_n$ appears as a hyperbola and the balance
locus $B_n = \{x_n=y_n\}$ appears as a diagonal line. In the intrinsic metric $g_n$, their
complete product and ratio level families are straight geodesic coordinate lines that cross
orthogonally throughout $D_n$ (visualized in Chu 又 as curved lines intersecting at right angles).
Curvature in the ordinary drawing is a chart artifact; perpendicularity is an intrinsic metric fact
(**[MATH]**).

## 5. The Held Boundary (曰), Local Paradox (Pₙ :: 玄), and Divergence (非)

Define product and ratio coordinates $p_n = x_n y_n$ and $r_n = x_n/y_n$. Then $G_n: p_n = 1$ and
$B_n: r_n = 1$ intersect orthogonally at the unique positive swap-fixed point:

$$ P_n^{\chi} = (1,1), \qquad \sigma_n(P_n) = P_n. \quad [\textbf{MATH}] $$

At the center of the $G_n$ curve sits this point where $\text{Void} = \text{Not-Void}$
($x_n = y_n$). It is the held boundary (曰 — the mouth 口 holding the line 一) that must exist as
the reference defining the contrast between the two modes, yet cannot obtain as an operative state
because a state where $\text{Void} = \text{Not-Void}$ has zero modal dominance ($x_n - y_n = 0$).
The fact that these two conditions must both hold is what we call Paradox (Pₙ :: 玄).

Excluding $P_n$ defines the punctured operative curve:

$$ G_n^* = G_n \setminus \{P_n\} = G_n^+ \sqcup G_n^-, \qquad G_n^+ = \{t > 0\}, \quad G_n^- = \{t < 0\}. \quad [\textbf{DEF; MATH}] $$

Applying this modal-dominance rule across the complete product-level foliation removes the entire
balance line $B_n$, splitting the reference domain into two diverging wings (非 — two wings
diverging from a shared origin):

$$ D_n \setminus B_n = \{x_n > y_n\} \sqcup \{y_n > x_n\}. \quad [\textbf{DEF; MATH}] $$

No continuous path inside the scalar operative domain connects $G_n^+$ and $G_n^-$ (**Scalar
Disconnection Theorem**).

## 6. Recursion as an Isometric Rereading (Φₙ :: 玄之又玄)

On $D_n = (0,\infty)^2$, the logarithmic map $\Phi_n(x_n,y_n) = (u_{n+1}, v_{n+1})$ is an exact
global isometry ($g_n = du_{n+1}^2 + dv_{n+1}^2$) realizing the recursive reference assignments:

$$ G_n \longmapsto \{v_{n+1} = 0\} = \mathrm{xAxis}_{n+1}, $$
$$ B_n \longmapsto \{u_{n+1} = 0\} = \mathrm{yAxis}_{n+1}, $$
$$ P_n^{\chi} = (1,1) \longmapsto O_{n+1}^{\chi} = (0,0). \quad [\textbf{MATH}] $$

The parent's paradoxical center $P_n$ (玄) becomes the child's additive origin reference $O_{n+1}$
(始 / 亡, the orthogonal sideways T ⊢ nested inside the parent curve). Selecting a child sector
$Q_{n+1} = \{u_{n+1}>0, v_{n+1}>0\}$ and instantiating the repeated frame-form
$G_{n+1}: X_{n+1}Y_{n+1} = 1_{n+1}$ constructs consistent reference chains to arbitrary finite
depth via $\Phi_n^{-1}$ (**[MATH under DEF]**).

## 7. Rotational Extension, Vector Orbit (心), and Unprivileged Orientation (天地不仁)

**Vector Distinction Extension (心 — Orbit Around an Empty Center):** Extending scalar comparison
$h_1 = x_n - y_n$ to a vector $\vec{h} = (h_1, h_2)$ and excluding only total zero
$\vec{h} = (0,0)$ permits a continuous circular phase path $e^{i\theta}$ (心) that reverses the
sign of $h_1$ (反) while holding open an interior void (無).

**Conditional Rotational Extension in $\mathbb{R}^3$:** Revolving the positive frame about the
$y$-axis ($\rho = \sqrt{x^2+z^2}$) sweeps $G_n$ into $\rho y = 1$ and $B_n$ into the balance cone
$\rho^2 = y^2$, intersecting orthogonally along the balance ring
$\mathcal{P}_{n,y} = \{(\cos\theta, 1, \sin\theta)\} \cong S^1$.

**Protected Winding in $d=3$:** For an affine balance line $B_n \subset \mathbb{R}^d$,
$\mathbb{R}^d \setminus B_n \simeq S^{d-2}$. Path-connectedness plus nontrivial integer loop
winding ($\pi_1 \cong \mathbb{Z}$) uniquely selects $d = 3$.

**No Preferred Orientation (天地不仁):** Admitting all equivalent axis orientations in
$\mathbb{R}^3$ without privilege yields the non-trivial fiber bundle
$SO(2) \longrightarrow SO(3) \longrightarrow S^2$.
