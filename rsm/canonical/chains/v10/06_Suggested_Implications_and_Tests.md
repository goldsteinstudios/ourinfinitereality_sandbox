## Paper 1: Constant Accuracy, Variable Precision (CAVP) and the Resolution of the Measurement Crisis

**The Crisis of Linear Subdivision:** When a scientist measures a continuous phenomenon using a
flat, linear ruler (可名), increasing precision subdivides the unit interval into smaller fractions
($10^{-1}, 10^{-2}, \dots, 10^{-k}$). Because Reality has no final inside (**[PREM]**), linear
subdivision never hits an ultimate "bottom brick," producing renormalization divergences when
point-particles are pushed to $r \to 0$.

**The CAVP Principle:** Consider an Archimedean sequence of regular $N$-gons inscribed in a circle
of diameter $1$:

- **Variable Precision (可 / 徼):** Each finite polygon ($N = 6, 12, 24, 96 \dots$) is a discrete
  polygonal chart whose perimeter $L_N = N\sin(\pi/N)$ refines its numerical approximation of $\pi$
  without ever becoming a circle at any finite $N$.
- **Constant Accuracy (恆 / 妙):** The circle itself is defined by the closed orbital relation
  $|e^{i\theta}| = 1$. Its circumference-to-diameter ratio is 100% accurate ($\pi$) at all times,
  prior to any polygonal subdivision.

**Implication:** Reality operates at Constant Accuracy through closed relational invariants
($XY = 1$, $|e^{i\theta}| = 1$); human instruments and discrete coordinate grids (曰 / 可名)
operate at Variable Precision.

### Relativistic Energy-Momentum as a Canonical RSM Reciprocal Frame ($G_n$)

$$ E^2 - (pc)^2 = (mc^2)^2 \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \left(\frac{E + pc}{mc^2}\right)\left(\frac{E - pc}{mc^2}\right) = 1. $$

- **The Balance Point ($P_n = (1,1)$):** Corresponds to $\phi = 0$ ($pc = 0, E = mc^2$), the rest

- **The Asymptotic Axes ($x_n=0, y_n=0$):** Correspond to the speed-of-light horizon
  ($E = \pm pc$, $\phi \to \pm\infty$), which lies at infinite intrinsic distance under the Scale
  Bridge metric $g_n = d\phi^2$ — explaining geometrically why no massive frame can ever reach the
  terminal limits $|0|$ or $|1|$.
