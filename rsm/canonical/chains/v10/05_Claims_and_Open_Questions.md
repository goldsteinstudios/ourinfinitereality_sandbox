## 1. Master Decisions Ledger (D01–D09)

**D01 ($P_0$, 玄牝, and the Terminal Faces $|0|$ and $|1|$):** Infinite Reality ($\mathcal{R}$) has
no final inside and no final outside (**[PREM]**). Absolute Void
($|0| :: \text{恆亡} :: \text{常無}$) and Absolute Not-Void
($|1| :: \text{恆又} :: \text{常有}$) are the two unreachable terminal faces of Absolute
Indistinction ($P_0: \mathsf{D}=\mathsf{I}$). In the textual correspondence, 玄牝 :: P₀ names the
Generative Paradox — the ultimate fact that following paradox down into paradox infinitely never
reaches a bottom, and that the cut defining every duality never obtains as a state within the frame
of the duality it defines (綿綿若存，用之不勤) (**[PREM; CORR]**).

**D02 (The Infinite Gradient $G_1$ and Local Origin Reference $O_n$):** Between $|0|$ and $|1|$
stretches the infinite reciprocal gradient ($G_1 :: \text{天拖} :: \text{天地} :: XY=1$). The
origin reference where the axes meet is
$O_n = \{\mathrm{xAxis}_n \cap \mathrm{yAxis}_n\} = (0,0)$ (無名 [天地之始]), which serves as the
"no" in the contrast between thing and no-thing ($1_n \neq 0_n$) (**[DEF; CORR]**).

**D03 (Held Boundary 曰, Local Paradox $P_n :: \text{玄}$, and Divergence 非):** Along $G_n$
(有名 [萬物之母] :: $1_n$) emerge the myriad articulations (萬) and their separating boundaries
(勿). At the center of $G_n$ sits $P_n = (1,1)$ (玄 / the held boundary 曰), where
$\text{Void} = \text{Not-Void}$ ($x_n = y_n$) must exist as the reference defining the contrast yet
cannot obtain as an operative state. Excluding $P_n$ (and $B_n$ across the foliation) splits the
domain into two diverging wings ($D_n \setminus B_n$, mapped to Chu 非) (**[DEF; MATH; CORR]**).

**D04 (The Scale Bridge and Intrinsic Metric $g_n$):** Homogeneous squeeze + swap symmetry + the
Scale Bridge uniquely force the intrinsic quadratic metric
$g_n = \alpha_n^2(d\ln(x_n/y_n))^2 + \beta_n^2(d\ln(x_n y_n))^2 = du_{n+1}^2 + dv_{n+1}^2$. Under
$g_n$, the axes $x_n=0, y_n=0$ lie at infinite distance, $G_n$ and $B_n$ are flat orthogonal
geodesics everywhere in $D_n$ (mapped to Chu 又, curved lines intersecting at right angles), and
$\Phi_n$ is a global smooth isometry (**[DEF; MATH; CORR]**).

**D05 (Exact Isometric Recursion $\Phi_n :: \text{玄之又玄}$):** $\Phi_n$ sends
$G_n \mapsto \mathrm{xAxis}_{n+1}$, $B_n \mapsto \mathrm{yAxis}_{n+1}$, and

**D07 (Definition Operator 名 and Channel Orientation 欲):** In Chapter 1, 名 operates as the
