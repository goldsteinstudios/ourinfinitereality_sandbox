# RSM — The Structural Chain (v9.53)

*The implicit register: the structural requirement of logic, stated without measurement, units, or chart. What instantiates it — physical, biological, textual — belongs to the explicit register and is not this chain's subject. Not sealed.*

## 0. The two registers

Every claim in this project belongs to one of two registers, and the division is decidable before any result arrives.

- **Implicit** — what follows from P1, P2 and the frame Definition with no measurement, no units, and no drawing. The variables below are of this kind. X and Y are not names; they are the slots two conjugate modes occupy.
- **Explicit** — anything requiring a rendering to state: coordinates, a metric, a sign convention, a quantity, an instance. Physics, biology, and any named pair (有/無, up/down) live here.

The implicit register fixes the structure; it fixes no parameters. Two systems can instantiate one structure and differ in every number — a branching law with exponent two and one with exponent three are the same logic under different parameters, and the implicit register is what they share. It follows that no quantity is ever owed by this chain, and no quantity ever confirms it.

This division is not a defence built after the fact. A claim is implicit only if it can be derived without a chart; anything else is explicit whether it succeeds or fails.

**Accuracy and precision.** Every quantitative statement is denominated in some frame's unit (§4). A unit is the frame's own constitutive magnitude — what the frame measures with, not something it discovers — so a statement made at frame n's resolution is frame n's indexed statement of the invariant, not a closer approach to it.

It follows that refinement does not converge. A finer frame does not produce a better version of the coarser frame's claim; it produces a different claim, exact at its own resolution and denominated in its own unit. The two are not two approximations of one statement. They are two statements, each complete where it stands, of one invariant that neither contains.

Call the invariant's presence in all of them **accuracy**, and each statement's resolution **precision**. Accuracy belongs to the structure and does not vary. Precision belongs to the statement and varies by frame. The relation between them is not approach: no accumulation of precision yields accuracy, because accuracy is not the limit of a refinement sequence — it is what every member of the sequence indexes.

**This is not approximation.** Approximation posits a true value approached imperfectly. Here the coarse statement is not defective: it is exact at its resolution, and what a finer statement adds is not correction but another index. The same claim at two precisions is two claims.

**Consequence.** A structural claim can be complete while stating no quantity, and adding quantity does not complete it further — it states something else.

**Parametric expression.** Only in the implicit register is the structure a perfect expression. Every obtained expression happens within a parametric environment — the finite parameters the pattern expresses within. The recursion is therefore not self-similar repetition at every scale: implicitly there is one gradient, one balance, one paradox; explicitly there are as many meetings as the environment's parameters give. Whether a parametric environment is itself derived, or is simply what *explicit* means, is an open item.

## Premises

**P1 — the Conditional.** Reality is infinitely vast and infinitely divisible: no terminal resolution in either direction — no edge outward, no floor inward.

**P2 — the Bridge.** What obtains is distinguishable. Contrapositively: what is not distinguishable does not obtain.

**Definition (frame).** A frame Rₙ is the coinherence of one distinction: two conjugate modes, their reciprocal relation, their balance, and their origin — arriving together or not at all. One constitutive pair per frame.

**Corollary.** Every distinction constitutes a frame; distinction beneath a frame's unit is therefore another frame, not a smaller item within this one.

Everything below is what P1 and P2 jointly require. Where a further assumption operates, it is named in the open items.

## 1. The total paradox: P₀

P₀ is the master conjugate pair at equality: **distinction and indistinction**, each constituted by the other, standing equal and cancelling. The same condition says itself three ways — distinction equals indistinction; obtainedness equals non-obtainedness; coherence equals incoherence. It is not indistinction: indistinction is one side of the pair, and P₀ privileges neither.

It cannot obtain, because at the total cancellation nothing is distinguishable and obtaining would not differ from not obtaining. It cannot be asserted without contradiction, since stating the equality distinguishes its terms. And it is not a state that fails to obtain but a condition that fails to cohere.

Its presenting faces are the **empty face** (collapse via emptiness) and the **full face** (collapse via fullness). Neither is a number. In native coordinates the empty face sits at nothing and the full face at the unbounded end — never at any frame's unit. The two faces are the two counterfactual terminals of one journey along the gradient: toward all-form-no-void and toward all-void-no-form. Neither is reached; the journey is one. Both are excluded in-frame, at the gradient's two ends, by the law's own shape (§4).

**Pₙ is the same species at frame scope:** a frame's own constitutive pair at equality. Indistinction does not obtain in a frame built on distinction. Where presence equals absence there is no reality. Where difficult equals easy there is no task. Where long equals short there is no measure. Where high equals low there is no height. Where tone equals voice there is no sound. Where before equals after there is no time. In each case the frame *is* the distinction, so equality removes what the frame was — and note the strength of that: no height, not height zero. Height zero is a value inside a frame that has height.

P₀ and Pₙ are not two scopes of one comparison. P₀ is the impossibility — beneath the register split itself, since the split is decidable for claims and P₀ is not a claim. Pₙ, with n as variable, is the implicit register's object: the structural requirement, unindexed. Any particular index is explicit — P₁ is the first instance's paradox, and an instance is the explicit register's business. There is nothing to differentiate, because there are not two things of one kind.

## 2. The ground

P₀ cannot obtain. Nor can indistinction on its own: it is one side of the master pair, and by P2 what is not distinguishable does not obtain. Both are excluded, so what obtains is distinguishable — at least one distinction, an origin, a frame: O₁, R₁. The step runs through P2 alone. P1 is the Conditional and asserts no existence. The ground relation is the recursion relation at zero scope: every origin is a paradox read from below, and O₁ reads P₀. Because P₀ is unframed, R₁ has no parent: each obtained tree is rooted. The ground's mechanism (identity through opposition — required directly by the impossibility) differs from every later frame's (generation at a paradox); both are real, and the structure needs both.

**At least one** is what the impossibility gives. Nothing here counts the roots, and nothing here excludes more than one.

**Vantage.** A locus does not carry its own reading. Whether a locus reads as an origin or as a paradox depends on which frame the statement is made from.

From frame n, looking along its own gradient toward the balance: the approach continues without arriving, and the locus is the frame's own paradox — entailed, never obtaining. From frame n+1, the same locus is the origin its two axes cross at, and everything in that frame is measured from it. One locus. Two frames. Two readings, and neither is the locus's own.

This is why the recursion can be stated two ways without contradiction. **O₍ₙ₊₁₎ reads Pₙ** is not a claim that something changed; it is the same locus indexed from the other side.

The same holds of the gradient's two ends. Motion along the gradient toward a mode's small end is in-frame and unbounded. Constitution of a child is across-frame. These are not two relations that happen to share a word — they are the frame-n and frame-n+1 readings of one relation, and a statement that does not name its frame will run them together.

## 3. The coinherence

- A distinction entails its two co-obtaining modes (X, Y) — one distinction, two modes, each constituted by the other; nothing prior was divided.

- **The modes are magnitudes. The conjugate of a mode is another mode, not its negative.** A negative would name something that both obtains and anti-obtains; what holds instead is that a mode is incoherent without its contrasting mode. A sign is an **indexed comparison** — this, compared to what it is being compared to — and its implication varies with the reference chosen. Sign therefore belongs to the explicit register throughout.

- A mode can be noticed without its conjugate having been measured; its **amount** exists only as a ratio to its conjugate. Existence-dependence is not measurement-dependence.

- Both modes are measured from one reference — the point at which both would vanish. That is the origin O: a locus, never a position; nothing stands there.

## 4. The frame; the unit; the floor

**1ₙ is the constitutive distinction's own magnitude** — the conserved product of *the* pair that constitutes frame n — and simultaneously the frame's minimal unit: nothing beneath the constitutive magnitude obtains *within* frame n, because any distinction beneath it is, by the Corollary, another frame. "Sub-1ₙ" is unit-relative typing, not size: magnitude comparison across frames is not defined, and the child is not smaller — the child is the approach that does not finish, continuing. The floor is the boundary of the frame's own definition, not an added restriction.

**The law.** The two modes' amounts are locked: their product is conserved, X·Y = 1ₙ. A conserved sum fails twice — it admits the terminals (one mode at nothing, the other at everything: P₀'s faces as attainable values) and it caps both modes, contradicting infinite reach. The product's curve has no endpoints and no ceilings; the exclusion of the impossible is the law's own shape, not a rule imposed on it. The constant is the unit, and the unit is the frame's reality: things differ in their proportion of the modes; reality is the invariant the proportions hold.

**Scale.** Within a frame, scale is motion along the curve: rescaling one mode by λ and the other by 1/λ, product invariant. No rescaling reaches the center. Scale is within-frame; the lineage is not scale.

## 5. The two centers, and the line between them

**O — the co-vanishing locus.** Where both modes would reach operational absence together. Nothing on the curve approaches it; the curve bends around it.

**Bₙ — the balance line.** The whole locus Xₙ = Yₙ. It reaches from Oₙ outward and passes through the gradient's paradoxical center. **No point of B obtains.** At any point of B the two modes stand in no proportion, so nothing distinguishes them; by P2 the condition does not obtain. B is a limit locus along its whole length: approached, never occupied.

**Pₙ — the paradox.** Where the gradient meets the balance line. It is the gradient's own center in the sense the origin is not: O is the center the curve bends around and never reaches, Pₙ is the center the curve has — its one point fixed by the mode-swap. Pₙ does not obtain, for the reason B does not. What frame n has instead is a resolution limit: **at frame n's resolution the two amounts are not distinguishable near Pₙ.** That is a fact about the frame's discrimination, not about a locus being reached. Beneath the floor the difference is there and the frame cannot state it.

**The disconnection.** Crossing from the X-dominant region to the Y-dominant region requires passing through the condition X = Y — and that condition is the mode-collapse, which does not obtain. The transit condition is itself the excluded condition. Within frame n the two mode-dominant regions are *related* — each is the other under the mode-swap — but no traversal joins them.

## 6. The engine

Continuity entails the balance line, and the gradient meets it. Denial A (origin): the locus cannot be frame n's origin — one constitutive pair per frame, and n has no spare pair to grant it. Denial B (exactness): the condition cannot obtain at all, since at exact equality the frame is not there. Simultaneously, locally.

The demand is satisfied in the one mode of existence left to such a center: **existence-as-organizing-locus** — in-frame, as the locus circulation organizes around; trans-frame, as the origin a new structure organizes around. **The child frame is the pursuit of exactness continuing below the floor:** the residue of imbalance beneath 1ₙ — the not-quite-balance the frame cannot state — is sub-unit distinction, hence a frame, whose origin is this very locus read from below. Recursion is exactness, permanently deferred.

**Generation has no address.** Constitution is not sited and not timed. The residue of imbalance is standing, so the child is entailed, not triggered. Nothing occurs *at* exact balance, because there is no there.

**Rotation.** The frame's two mode-dominant regions are related without being connected (§5), and the relation is carried by descent (§7) — the divider becomes the child's axis — whether or not any motion realizes it. What the structure additionally admits is revolution: **Gₙ revolving about Bₙ.** A half-turn about B exchanges the modes — the mode-swap performed as motion rather than as relabeling — so revolution relates the two regions with nothing passing through the locus that cannot be occupied. "No through, only around." The plane's own exchange is reflection across B, which reverses orientation and is no motion; realizing the swap as orientation-preserving continuous motion is what requires a direction the plane does not have. Orientation is linear-structure content, not measure content, so the requirement is stated without a metric.

**Rendering guard.** A drawing shows a smooth curve through an unproblematic point; the implicit facts (no origin here; nothing obtains here) have no rendering. The drawing's verdict never overwrites the logic's.

## 7. Recursion

**Recursion.** A child frame is constituted by **two readings** of parent structure:

- **yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎ reads Bₙ**
- **xAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎ reads Gₙ**

The assignment is fixed by the role each plays in the parent. Bₙ is the asymptotic line that splits Gₙ into its two mode-dominant regions, exactly as yAxisₙ is the asymptotic line that splits xAxisₙ into its two arms. The splitter is read as the splitter; what is split is read as what is split. Swapping them would give the child a frame in which the divider is the divided.

**The origin follows.** By the Definition, a frame's origin is where its two axes cross. Under the two readings that crossing is Gₙ ∩ Bₙ — which is Pₙ:

**O₍ₙ₊₁₎ = xAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎ ∩ yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎ = Gₙ ∩ Bₙ = Pₙ**

This is the vantage rule (§2) at the recursion: one locus, read from the parent as its paradox and from the child as its origin. The identity is derived from the readings rather than posited beside them.

The identification is of type as well as position: an axis is a locus where a mode is exactly zero and which therefore does not obtain; B is a locus where the modes are exactly equal and which therefore does not obtain. Same species of object. What the parent cannot resolve is what the child measures from.

**The parent persists entire.** Gₙ and Bₙ remain the parent's own structure throughout; the child has a reading of them, not a portion of them. Nothing is transferred, consumed, or vacated — which is why the relation is constitutive and standing rather than an event.

**Orientation.** B is not selected by the parent — Pₙ lies on B by construction, so a line through the child's origin is constituted, not chosen. What remains free is everything about that line: the rotational freedom about B is the **ring** of orientations, no one privileged. What obtains is always one selection (all at once would be saturation), and different selections are different branches. Every obtained frame therefore has at least one orientation, and it is the inherited one.

The nesting is a tree, not a ladder — and a rooted tree: index-relationality holds within it (every frame reads itself as "n"; the labels are gauge), but no relabeling makes a root a child, because a root's parent-side is P₀, an impossibility, not a frame.

**What a child shares with its parent.** Not magnitude (comparison across frames is undefined), not orientation about B (nothing selects it), not content, not substance. What is shared is the frame-form — every frame is a frame — and, between this child and this parent, the paradox itself: the parent's inexactness is the child's reference. The relation is constitutive and standing, not a transfer and not an event.

## Words that assert what the framework denies

A standing list, not a procedure. Agentive verbs for structural facts. Transfer-language for non-events. Removal narratives for constitutive exclusions. Staging conjunctions for simultaneous constitution. Reified infinities. Occupancy language for the loci. Address-language for constitution. "Negative" for a conjugate. Any noun that turns a paradox into a place.

**The involution.** Two are available: the mode-swap (X ↔ Y, fixed set B) and the sign-flip (componentwise negation, fixed point O). The mode-swap is structural; the sign-flip is explicit. A negative would name something that both obtains and anti-obtains, whereas a mode is incoherent without its contrasting mode. And a half-turn about B sends X → Y and Y → X — the mode-swap is a rotation, a motion the structure admits; componentwise negation reverses orientation and is no rotation at all.

## Open items

1. **The open closures** — the reciprocity requirement's native-magnitudes step; the ground's self-sufficiency candidate.
2. **Anchoring-by-constitution — resolved negative, consequences open.** Sub-unit position does not establish descent (a branch off one trunk is not a branch off a separate trunk, though both can obtain in one rendering). Lineage is established by shared ancestry, not relative scale. What relation, if any, holds between frames that share a rendering without sharing ancestry is open.
3. **The cross-frame unit relation** 1ₙ ↔ 1₍ₙ₊₁₎ — whether any quantitative relation accompanies the constitutive share of §7. An explicit-register question.
4. **Dimension count** — a circle is all potential points equidistant from a shared origin in a plane; a sphere is all potential points equidistant from a shared origin in three dimensions; with no orientation preferred, the structure's directions form the sphere, which requires three dimensions and requires no fourth. "At least three" stands on this route. The premise (no preferred orientation) is register-clean; the construction as stated uses equidistance, which is measure-content, and the quadraticity of the measure is open. "Exactly three" is open.
5. **Branching beyond the balance.** Generation has no address, so the question is whether any relation other than the constitutive pair can be inexact in the way that constitutes a child. Candidate, unruled: it cannot — the frame has one constitutive pair, any conjugate equality within it reduces to the same balance, and the other points of B belong to other frames.
6. **The parametric environment** — derived, or constitutive of the explicit register as such? What fixes a frame's environment is not settled.
