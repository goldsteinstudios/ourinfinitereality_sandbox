This is the **current working set** for the Recursive Structural Model. It supersedes v9, and through
it v7.7, v7.6-candidate and v7.5 (all kept in place as history). All four documents close
**"Not sealed."**

**Provenance.** Four PDFs added 2026-07-29, `.md` transcriptions added the same day by the author.
Both forms are in place here; the `.md` is the version-controlled form. Transcription was checked
against the PDFs at the load-bearing points — the tag sets, every re-tagged theorem in the math chain,
the retired conjugation entry, and all three open-item lists (structural 4, math 8, DDJ 6 checksums).
Clean.

**Filename note.** These files use spaces and `v9_2` (`structural v9_2.md`), where every prior set used
underscores and a dotted version (`structural_v9.md`). Nothing depends on the pattern — the lint globs
by extension — but it is the first set to break it.

## Premises

Unchanged from v9.

- **P1 — the Conditional.** Reality is infinitely vast and infinitely divisible: no terminal
  resolution in either direction.
- **P2 — the Bridge.** What obtains is distinguishable.
- **Definition (frame).** A frame `Rₙ` is the coinherence of one distinction: two conjugate modes,
  their reciprocal relation, their balance, and their origin — arriving together or not at all. One
  constitutive pair per frame.
- **Corollary.** Every distinction constitutes a frame; distinction beneath a frame's unit is
  therefore another frame, not a smaller item within this one.

Everything else is what P1 and P2 jointly require. Where a further assumption operates, it is named
in the open items.

## What changed from v9

**The involution fork is closed, and the bill is paid.** This is the spine of the release. v9 carried
it as structural open item 6 and math open item 9: is the mode-swap `σ_B` or the sign-flip `ν` the
structural involution? v9.2 rules **`σ_B` structural, `ν` chart** (structural §8) on three grounds —
grammar, chart, and motion. On the DDJ side the corpus supplies *resistance*: across every 非 token,
**no token negates between conjugate opposites**, so the text refuses the mirror-negative reading.

What matters as much as the ruling is that v9.2 collects the debt v9 named in advance. v9 said that if
the sign is chart, "both i² routes, orbit-necessity, the minimal traversal, and the ν-centroid
instances" are rendering. v9.2 does exactly that:

The physics flagship `1ₙ :: ℏ/2` downgrades *itself*: its floor is saturated by the minimal traversal,
which lives in the signed chart, "so on that axis the correspondence runs between a physical result and
a result about the framework's chart." Physics §4 keeps the spin-½ debt open and **declines to enter**
the candidate account, because it has no break-condition.

Other changes:

- **The Amplitude Floor promoted** from a clause to the name of the mode-expressible domain
  (`a ≥ √1ₙ`). The circle slice *as a complete object* is `[rendering]`. This is what carries the
  involution ruling.
- **"Occupiable" is gone.** v9 §1 called `Pₙ` "occupiable at resolution" while v9 §5 said occupancy
  language does not type these loci — a live self-contradiction, and the seating error's own word.
  v9.2 reads "met at resolution" in both places, and in the math Notation.
- **"Where the two infinities live" → "The two senses of smaller."** **Fineness** (in-frame, along the
  gradient) versus **descent** (across-frame, at a joint), because two relations must not share a word.
  Knock-on handled: v9's "divisibility excludes |0|, vastness excludes |1|" is dropped, and
  face-exclusion is restated as the gradient's *two ends*.
- **"Generation has no address"** (structural §6, mirrored in math). Constitution is not sited and not
  timed; the child is entailed, not triggered. This propagated as a repair — v9's DDJ read "only a held
  relation has an address"; v9.2 reads "only a held relation is able to be referred to."
- **"What a child shares with its parent"** (§7), new: *what the parent cannot state is what the child
  measures from.*
- **The strike list grew**: the grammar test now also names occupancy language for the loci,
  address-language for constitution, and "negative" for a conjugate.
- **DDJ: the etymology guard**, new — recovering an earlier *attested* state is scoreable; inferring a
  pre-attested sense from a graph's picture is not. The 萬 scorpion-pictogram argument is **declined**
  on principle rather than used.
- **Open items: structural 6 → 4, math 9 → 8.** Both drops are earned by the involution ruling.

**One item was dissolved rather than closed.** v9's structural open item 5, "priority and uniqueness of
the first distinction," is gone from the list; §2 now reads *"**At least one** is what the impossibility
gives. Nothing here counts the roots, and nothing here excludes more than one."* That withdraws the
claim rather than answering the question. It propagated consistently — "each obtained tree is rooted,"
picked up in the DDJ 天地不仁 entry — but it is the one drop not paid for by an argument.

**One ground is weaker than its two siblings.** The involution's third ground (*Motion* — a half-turn
about the balance axis sends X → Y, while componentwise negation reverses orientation and is no
rotation) appeals to a swept rendering and to orientation. Using a rendering fact as one of three
*independent* grounds for a structural ruling is the move the chart test exists to catch. It may
survive — the claim is about which involution the structure admits as *motion* — but it is presented as
coequal and is not marked.

## Guards for anyone (human or AI) working here

- **The chart test:** does the claim survive changing the drawing? Perpendicularity,
  straightness-as-driver, ambient space for the sphere, and the sign on a mode **each failed it**.
- **The grammar test:** does the word's grammar assert what the framework denies? Both failures look
  like clarity from the inside; that is why the tests are written down.
- **The characteristic AI failure mode is *find a matching set, declare an identity*.** See
  `../../../audit/divergence_ledger_r1.md`. Its complement is equally live: a false closure carrying
  *no* flag, reading as rigorous (Entry 003).
- **Verify geometry against `../../../audit/checks.py` and `../../../audit/cross_model_checks.py`** —
  both exit non-zero on a false claim. **But note:** they compute in a *signed* chart, and v9.2 has now
  settled that the sign-flip is chart. A `[PASS]` there is a rendering fact **outright** — no longer one
  awaiting adjudication. That is a stronger caveat than v9's, not a weaker one.
- **Occupancy language does not type these loci.** Met/unmet are frame-reading statuses. Run
  `python3 rsm/audit/site_vocab_lint.py --chains` before relying on any chain text; it now defaults to
  this set.
- **玄 is paradox; 牝 is generative; 玄牝 is the generative paradox.** The compound is compositional,
  which is what makes the `P₀` assignment motivated rather than brute — so "generative" must not be
  folded into 玄. Site copy that glossed `Pₙ` as "the generative crossing" was corrected 2026-07-29,
  and the lint now enforces it.
