## Part 1 — Structural rulings

### R1. The recursion mappings (the session's central result)

**RULED.** Two transfers, not swappable:

- **Bₙ → yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎**
- **Gₙ → xAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎**

With the roles stated:

- Bₙ is the asymptotic axis that splits Gₙ, in the same way yAxisₙ is the asymptotic
  axis that splits xAxisₙ.
- Oₙ is where xAxisₙ ∩ yAxisₙ — the condition there being +Xₙ = −Xₙ = +Yₙ = −Yₙ.
- Gₙ is curved relative to yAxisₙ, xAxisₙ, **and** Bₙ — except at Pₙ, where Bₙ and Gₙ
  are locally perpendicular (slope of Bₙ is +1, slope of Gₙ is −1).

**Consequence, derived from the two mappings:**

    O₍ₙ₊₁₎ = X₍ₙ₊₁₎ ∩ Y₍ₙ₊₁₎ = Gₙ ∩ Bₙ = Pₙ

The child's origin *is* the parent's paradox. Parturition form follows rather than being
asserted: the parent persists entire (both curves remain its curves), the child obtains
with its origin at the crossing the parent cannot occupy.

This is the mechanism under "a branch node is two things at once."

### R2. The transit argument (replaces Code's disconnection theorem)

**RULED.** Crossing from the y>x region of Gₙ to the x>y region requires x=y, and x=y is
the mode-collapse. The transit condition is itself the excluded condition.

Stronger than the machine's version ("no point of B obtains, therefore no path") and does
not depend on that premise. The chain should restate it this way.

**What replaces the retired premise in the editorial corpus:** the two regions are neither
connected nor unrelated — the divider becomes the child's axis (R1). The relation is
carried by descent, not by a path.

### R3. Revolution is available, not forced

**RULED.** "It isn't necessarily rotation. It could be structure without movement."

Open item A6 closes by **demotion**. The dimension route that leaned on
revolution-needs-a-direction is re-based by R13 below.

### R4. No craft noun in the chains; craft nouns are fine in the explicit register

**RULED.** "It's not a seat or a joint or a pivot although depending on the frame being
examined it could be any of those (the fulcrum of a seesaw for example)."

The blanket strike of "seat" is wrong; the strike is correct **for the chains only**. The
chains carry Pₙ and no craft noun. Editorial documents are explicit-register and may use
the frame-appropriate word.

No essay rebuilds required. Essay 12 is clean as written (it names a physics rendering).
Essay 9 needs one fix, where "every reciprocal pair has its seat" is stated as a
structural claim rather than a rendering.

### R7. Recursion is parametric, not fractal

**RULED.** "Only in the implicit frame is it a perfect expression. In reality every
expression happens within a parametric environment that the patterns express within. So
it's not fractals repeating themselves infinitely, it's recursion where the pattern
expresses within finite parametric environments."

Three consequences:

1. **The many-nodes question dissolves.** Implicitly: one B, one G, one P. Explicitly: a
   real trunk's parameters yield as many ray–cambium meetings as that trunk has.
2. **Essay 10's tree/vortex pairing survives an audit objection.** The report objected
   that exponents differ by system. Under R7 they *should* differ — same implicit logic,
   different parametric environments, no shared constant owed. Only "one law" fails.
3. **"Fractal" is retired corpus-wide.** A new error family the separation report could
   not catch, because it checked against chains that do not yet say this.

### R11. Pₙ, operationally

**RULED.** Pₙ is the branch node locus: where B_trunk (medullary ray) ∩ G_trunk (cambium),
and where trunk-oriented rotation recurses into branch-oriented rotation.

The botanical mapping is Will's and should be recorded as such:
**pith :: O · cambium :: G · medullary rays :: B · branch node :: P.**
(The separation report flagged this as absent from the chains and therefore unsupported.
It is not unsupported — it was unrecorded.)

### R13. The sphere, without motion

**RULED.** "If reality has no preferred orientation then a circle is all potential points
equidistant from a shared origin in a plane. A sphere is all potential points equidistant
from a shared origin in R3."

**This rescues the dimension route from R3's demotion.** The old route ran "arcs over the
center sweep a sphere," which leaned on revolution. This reaches the same object from
no-preferred-orientation alone — a static set, not a swept path. "At least three" survives.

Caveat to record: *equidistant* is a distance notion and quadraticity is open. The premise
(no preferred orientation) is register-clean; the construction as stated is measure-gated.
Ruled item 5 of B13 (the ring as consequence) is accepted on this footing.

### R14. Sub-unit position does not establish descent

**RULED.** "A branch off one trunk is not the same as a branch from a separate trunk,
though both can obtain within the same R3."

Anchoring-by-constitution (A2/B9) resolves as **no**. The math chain's body is wrong to
call it settled; the open item is right. Being small relative to n does not make a frame
n's descendant.

Refined by R15: trunks sharing an environment are not siblings by lineage.

### R15. Shared environment is a gradient, not a parent

**RULED.** Two trunks are structures obtaining on **G_earth** — not recursions from a soil
*frame*. A gradient two structures both express within, not a container and not a common
ancestor.

Dissolves the regress (no ancestor-of-the-ancestor to chase). Leaves commensurability
between same-gradient structures **open** — sharing a gradient is not shared ancestry, and
nothing yet says whether it suffices for comparability.

### R16. "Obtains within" is a relation without an English name

**RULED (as unnamed).** R_trunk, R_branch, R_twig, R_leaf all obtain within R_earth, which
obtains within R_solar system, which obtains within R_galaxy. Will: "I don't think there's
a good way to describe this in english."

The structure is specified by R1 without any preposition:
O₍ₙ₊₁₎ = Gₙ ∩ Bₙ = Pₙ. That states what obtains where without saying the child is *in* the
parent or *from* it. The relation is an identity between loci; the English gloss is what
fails.

**This item is unnamed, not unresolved** — it awaits a word, not a derivation, and should
not sit beside items awaiting one.

**Editorial consequence (new error family):** any sentence saying a frame is *inside*,
*within*, *part of*, or *contained by* another makes a claim the structure does not
license. Related to the containment error the physics chain already rejects with a tree as
its example.

maintaining**. The received 常 is therefore ambiguous by merger: some instances carry 亙/恆,

Claude had proposed defaulting unattested received 常 to 恆. **Withdrawn** — the honest
default is neither; resolution must come from the line's own work.

**Deferred by Will to the DDJ finalization pass.** The chain's 恆/常 checksum item is
reframed rather than open: it is not "which word is right," it is "the strips keep two

## Part 4 — Offered by Claude, awaiting Will's check

### C1. Pₙ characterized without a metric

The mode-swap σ sends (x,y) → (y,x). Its fixed set is x=y, which is Bₙ. Its −1
eigendirection is (1,−1) — slope −1 — the tangent to Gₙ at Pₙ.

So Bₙ and the tangent to Gₙ at Pₙ are the +1 and −1 eigenspaces of the mode-swap.
Eigenspaces of an involution are defined without any metric.

**Proposed:** Pₙ is the unique point of Gₙ whose tangent lies in the swap-antisymmetric
direction. No distance, no angle, no quadraticity.

If it holds: it takes P's identification out of the metric-gated zone, and gives the loom's
warp/weft claim something true to be re-typed onto.

### C2. 0ₙ as extent rather than point

The DDJ chain types 無 as a bounded functional magnitude, with 有 × 無 = 1ₙ. A magnitude in
a conserved product has a value that varies. Oₙ has no value at all.

**Proposed:** 0ₙ is the *extent* between Oₙ and 1ₙ, not the point. Fits the instances — a
hub is a bore, not a point; the eye of a vortex has a diameter.

Possible further consequence: sub-unit territory is below the frame's resolution, so from
inside frame n it reads as empty while being where the next frame obtains.

### C3. Big bang :: O_branch, black hole :: P_branch

Will's construction; the `::` typing is Claude's per R8.

If our universe is a branch: our origin is the trunk's branch node having recursed into
branch orientation; our P's are where twigs recurse into their own orientation.

The structural claim underneath — origin and paradox are one locus read from two sides — is
derived (R1). The naming is explicit-register and owes physics nothing.

**Direction note:** descent runs toward fineness, so the trunk's frame is *coarser* than
ours, and a black hole is where something finer than us obtains — the opposite of the usual
picture where the big bang is the small thing. Consistent, but readers will trip there.
