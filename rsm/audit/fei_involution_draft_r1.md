## In plain terms first

Two operations in the framework both get called symmetries. One swaps the two modes — form and void —
and lives inside a single curve. The other flips sign, and relates the two curves to each other.
Structural item 6 asks which of them is doing structural work, and says the answer decides what 非
means.

**The answer is the sign-flip, but the question's shape was wrong.** Both operations are real. They
just do different jobs. The sign-flip is what carries the difference between the two curves — because
*nothing else does*. Every quantity the framework recognises reads identically on both curves. The
mode-swap isn't demoted by this; it stays what it always was, the law's own even-handedness about
which mode you call which.

**And 非 tracks the sign-flip.** Its middle is the origin — the point nothing reaches, which both
modes are nonetheless measured from. Not the seat, where the balance actually obtains.

**What's worth knowing is that the text says this too, independently of the geometry.** The chain's
own survey of 非 records that it *never* stands between a conjugate pair, and that where it is copular
it stands between **two readings of one term**. Both facts point away from the mode-swap — form and
void *are* a conjugate pair, and they are two different modes rather than two readings of one thing.
That was in the chain already; nobody had connected it to the involution question.

**The main caveat:** every copular instance of 非 is in Chapter 1, which is *not* in the Guodian
strips. So the whole reading rests on received-text material, and the graph analysis hasn't been
checked against facsimiles. That prices it as a candidate, not as attested.

## Claim A — the sign-flip carries the signed register

### Prior results used

- **Q2** — the two components are genuinely two; the second is not the chart's image of the first.
- **T1** *(verified)* — `{Q_j = 1ₙ}` has two components, `Pₙ` and `ν(Pₙ)` in different ones.
- **Reciprocity joint, clause 2** — *"the law is symmetric under X ↔ Y."*
- **The measure's parity constraint** — *"Parity (ν): M(−z) = M(z) — kills all odd degrees."*

### The argument

**A1 — Nothing but the sign distinguishes the branches.** *[checked]* On corresponding points of the
two branches, every even quantity is identical: the conserved product (+1ₙ on both), the ratio of the
modes (4.000 on both at the worked point; 1.000 at both seats), the squared distance from `Oₙ`. Only
odd quantities — a coordinate taken alone, the sum of the two — differ, and only in sign. Further, the
framework's own measure is **forced even** by parity, so the measure *structurally cannot see* which
branch it is in.

**A2 — The branches are nonetheless two.** Q2.

**A3 — Therefore the distinction between them is structural and is carried by the sign.** By A2 there
is a real difference; by A1 the only candidate carrier is the sign; the operation that relates the
branches is ν. *[derived, given Q2]*

**A4 — σ_B is not demoted by this.** The reciprocity joint's exchange symmetry is a claim about the
**law**: X and Y are interchangeable in the equation. Q2's denial is that two **loci** are
feature-identical at every depth. Different objects; both stand. σ_B remains the law's own exchange
symmetry and remains chart-independent in that sense.

**A5 — So item 6's framing was the wrong shape.** It asks *"which is structural"* as an either/or.
Both are. They carry different loads: **ν carries the branch distinction — the signed register — and
σ_B is the law's evenhandedness between the modes.** Item 6 is answered by dissolving its disjunction,
not by choosing a horn.

> **Conclusion A.** The involution that carries the signed register is **ν**, whose fixed point is
> `Oₙ`. *[derived, given Q2]*

## Claim B — 非 tracks it

**Register note.** The DDJ is a rendering; its success criterion is translation-fidelity, and no entry
carries evidential weight for the derivation in either direction. Nothing below feeds Claim A.

**B1 — The graph flagged a candidate distinction.** *[notation layer — legitimate]* 非's form is two
diverging wings with space between them. **[W]:** *"how can the emptiness between the two diverging
wings in fei be 'met'? it can be held as reference but it's the limit at the center, like the 0 that
sits between -1 and +1."* Under the chain's instrument rules this is exactly what text→math at the
notation layer is licensed to do: flag, not establish.

**B2 — The math verified with its own resources.** Required by the same rule. Claim A above, argued
without reference to 非 at any step.

**B3 — Two textual facts, already in the chain's survey, point the same way.** Neither had been
connected to the involution question.

- *"**no token negates between conjugate opposites**"* — and 有/無, form and void, **are** a conjugate
  pair. If 非 tracked the mode-swap, the corpus should show it standing between such a pair. It never
  does. **Evidence against the mode-swap reading.**
- *"非 stands between **two readings of one term**"* — 可道 非 常道 is two readings of 道. One term,
  two readings. That is ν's structure — one structure at two orientations, identical in every even
  quantity — and not σ_B's, which relates two *different* modes.

**B4 — The middles agree.** The chain glosses 非's middle as *"the invariant both flanks share."*
`Oₙ` is *"the reference from which both modes are measured — the point at which both would vanish… a
locus, never a position; nothing stands there."* **Shared, and unoccupied.** Will's phrase *"held as
reference"* is what makes those one statement rather than two.

> **Conclusion B.** 非 :: the sign-flip ν; its middle is `Oₙ`. *[candidate — see stratum limits]*

## The obligation the write-up must carry

**The exchange-symmetry false conflict.** A4 exists because the first objection anyone raises will be:
*"Q2 denies the mirror, and your own reciprocity joint asserts exchange symmetry — pick one."* The
answer is that the joint speaks of the law and Q2 speaks of two loci. If A4 is dropped from the final
text, the objection lands.

## Weak joints — targets for the adversarial read

**W1 — everything is conditional on Q2.** A3 depends on it, and B's verification is A. If Q2 falls,
this falls with it. Q2 has not had its own adversarial read yet, and its weakest joint (the
chart-generation objection) is unaddressed.

**W2 — the Ch. 1 reconciliation. Likeliest failure point.** B4 has 非's middle as `Oₙ`, an unmet
locus. But in Chapter 1 the flanks are 可道 and 常道, and the invariant they share is **道**, which the
chain glosses as *"the recursive structural model itself"* — a structure, not a locus, and not
obviously unmet. *"Held as reference"* reconciles *shared* with *unoccupied* in the abstract, but it
does not by itself make 道 into `Oₙ`. **Does the 非-middle correspondence hold uniformly — always
`Oₙ` — or vary by instance?** If it varies, B is weaker than stated.

**W3 — attestation.** See stratum limits. `[R]`-only copular instances; unverified graphs.

**W4 — the graph reading is a reading.** Two-diverging-wings is one paleographic analysis of 非. The
project has withdrawn graph-evidence before (the 名 case, v7.7). B1 is a flag and is priced as one,
but a reader may take it as more.
