### Q1 — A word. (Backing: §1)

Two chains say `Pₙ` is "**occupiable** at resolution." The same two chains also say "occupancy
language does not type these loci." Both sentences cannot stand.

The *idea* behind the phrase is not in dispute — the balance really does hold at the frame's own
resolution, and only exactness is denied. That is already what v9 says. So the question is only which
sentence gives way:

- **Keep the rule**, and say "met at resolution" instead — the phrasing the math chain already uses a
  few lines further down; or
- **Keep the word**, and narrow the rule in writing — e.g. "occupancy language does not type `Oₙ` or
  `P₀`," leaving `Pₙ`-at-resolution describable that way.

*One thing to weigh:* "met at resolution" describes a **relation** — the two modes' amounts stand
equal, as far as the frame can tell. "Occupiable" describes a **place** — somewhere a thing could
sit. The chains deny anything sits there ("n has no spare pair to grant it"). The seating error was
exactly the slide from the first to the second, which is why the word is worth a deliberate choice
rather than a tolerance.

## 1. "Occupiable at resolution" — the content stands, the word is the question

Relates to: `v9_comparison_r1.md` §2, ruling 1.

**[W]** on why the phrase might be defensible:

> An infinite gradient has a limit at it's center rather than a discrete point, but that limit only

**[W]** sharpening it, and correcting a mistake of mine:

> it's all relational. 1_n is not an integer, it's the minimual unit of distinction within frame n

> I think 1n is supremely accurate and completely without precision, or something like that.

**[checked] — the content is already v9's.** Structural §5 says *"balance at frame resolution
obtains… What cannot obtain is exactness."* So this is not a re-import of the seating error at the
level of claim. The disagreement is lexical.

**[checked] — my threshold analysis was in the wrong register, and is withdrawn.** I offered three
candidate thresholds for "frame n cannot state this imbalance": the `Q_i` excess (`|b| < √(1ₙ/2)`),
the amplitude unit (`|b| < √1ₙ`), and the mode difference (`|b| < √1ₙ/2`). All three are **additive**
quantities. v9 §3 says *"its amount exists only as a ratio to its conjugate"*; §4 says *"things differ
in their proportion of the modes."* Proportion, not difference. All three candidates are therefore
mistyped.

**[checked] — the relational coordinate, and what it shows.** On the gradient the conserved product
never moves (`XY = 1ₙ` at every b — verified). What varies is the ratio. Its additive form is the
rapidity:

```
X = √1ₙ·e^φ ,  Y = √1ₙ·e^−φ    →   XY = 1ₙ  (fixed),   X/Y = e^2φ ,   seat at φ = 0
```

This is the framework's own split action: v9's *"rescaling one mode by λ and the other by 1/λ,
product invariant"* is exactly a **shift in φ** (λ = 2 shifts φ by ln 2 ≈ +0.693; λ = 7.5 by +2.015;
product invariant in both — verified). Re-expressed in φ, my three thresholds land at 0.481, 0.659,
0.881 — nothing round, which is the signature of a coordinate artifact.

**[checked] — a flag that resolves downward.** Threshold three yields `X = φ_golden` exactly, because
`X − 1/X = 1` is φ's defining equation. That is a restatement, not a discovery: any conserved product
with a unit difference produces it, and the content sat entirely in choosing a *difference* threshold
— the wrong register. **Recorded so it is not re-found and mistaken for a result.** (The repo's
`_between-e-and-phi.md` is underscore-prefixed and does not ship; not assessed.)

**[checked] — an error of mine, struck.** I called the resulting neighbourhoods "large" (`X/Y ≈ 2.6`
at the tightest) and treated that as evidence against them. That is a cross-frame magnitude
comparison, which v9 rules out — *"magnitude comparison across frames is not defined"* — and I made
it two paragraphs after quoting the prohibition. The judgment is withdrawn; it was not evidence of
anything.

**What Will's position implies.** If 1ₙ is *supremely accurate and completely without precision*,
there is no numeric threshold to find: 1ₙ is not a value with digits, it is the resolution. So the
question "what ratio does frame n call indiscernible?" asks frame n to do something only frame n+1
can do. By the Corollary, sub-unit distinction is not a fine reading *within* frame n — it **is**
frame n+1. The discrimination is not frame n's to perform.

**[open] — the ruling itself.** Two routes, and Will has not chosen:

- **(a)** Replace the word. The math chain already carries the right phrasing two bullets after the
  offending line: *"Balance status: met-at-resolution, unmet-absolutely."* Adopting that in both
  chains clears three of the four lint hits and loses nothing above.
- **(b)** Narrow the ban explicitly — e.g. "occupancy language does not type `Oₙ` or `P₀`," leaving
  `Pₙ`-at-resolution describable that way. Requires editing the prohibition sentence, not just
  tolerating the collision.

What cannot stand is the present state, where the same document asserts both.

**A caution on (b), for the record.** "Met at resolution" is a status on a *relation* — the two modes'
amounts stand equal to within what the frame can state. "Occupiable" is a modal predicate on a
*locus* — this place admits a tenant. v9 denies the tenant on the same page: *"P cannot serve as frame
n's origin: one constitutive pair per frame, and n has no spare pair to grant it."* The seating error
