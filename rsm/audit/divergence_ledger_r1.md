## Entry 001 — The Product/Ratio Orthogonality Attractor (the Image-9 Error)
**The false proposal (Gemini instance, unprimed by the prior correction):** that {XY = c} and {X/Y = k} form a globally orthogonal net, offered as the derivation route for "sealing" the anchoring line.
**The mathematical failure:** grad(XY)·grad(X/Y) = (Y² − X²)/Y² — zero only on the balance axes. The true orthogonal partner of the product family is {X² − Y² = k}, which is the level-net of the z² parturition map itself (Re/Im of z²).
**Precision note — what was and wasn't false:** the B-collinearity of lineage seats is TRUE (via parturition inheritance; canon S1). What failed: the net premise (false), the lineage-continuity premise (circular — presupposes the inheritance it argues toward), the G-membership premise (posited), and the status claim ("sealed" — inflation over an honest gap).
**Attractor status: verified.** Three independent sources have produced this exact false geometry: the project's own archived plot (image 9) and a second AI instance bridging the same gap. The error is attractive precisely where an open item wants closing — the signature habitat of false closure.
**Resolution:** rejected against the verified record; the source instance accepted the correction on computation and co-drafted this entry — a divergence that CLOSED under evidence (the instance-protocol working). The anchoring residual remains open, now decomposed (math r4).

## Entry 003 — The Unflagged False Closure (v7.5 Lemma 4.4), and silent replacement as a blind spot
**The false claim (Claude instance, drafting `just_math_v7.5.md`; carried into a document declaring itself "sealed"):** Definition 4.3 + Lemma 4.4 — that the arc of radius `√1ₙ` "maintains `XY = 1ₙ` at every point and so has cost zero," and that outward deviation "strictly increases cost, since `XY` exceeds `1ₙ`."
**The computational check:** on that arc `XY = 1ₙ·cos 2θ`, deviating by up to **2** and reaching `−1ₙ` at the conjugate seats — the claimed zero-cost path is the *maximum*-cost path under the definition's own cost. Outward deviation does not raise `XY`: at `(a,b) = (0,2)`, radius 2 gives `XY = −4`. And no zero-cost spanning path exists at all: cost-zero forces the path onto `Gₙ`, where `|a| ≥ 1ₙ`, so `a` cannot change sign between the seats. Same failure in the `(X,Y)` chart about `P = (1,1)`, so it is not a chart artifact. `cross_model_checks.py A2`.
**What was true inside it:** the *conclusion* — that the unit orbit is the traversal and that direct crossing is forbidden — is right, and is what v7.7's Theorems 1–2 establish correctly (wall / door / toll). Prop. 4.1 (density is not completeness) is also correct and independent. Only the mechanism was false.
**Attractor status: the seating error's complement.** The seating error was a false closure that *flagged itself* (`[commitment]`) and was caught. This one carried **no flag**, was named the paper's "engine" in its own section header, and its prose was scrupulous about the thing it was not doing (importing a metric) while being wrong about the thing it was doing (computing a product). **Careful hedging on one axis reads as rigor on all axes** — that is the transferable finding.
**Why it survived three audits:** the comparative method could not see it. v7.7 does not *contradict* Lemma 4.4; it silently replaces it with Definition T and stops mentioning the conservation cost. Read comparatively that looks like a retreat from strength, and the prior audit priced it that way (§8, now annotated). **Proposed protocol addition, for ruling: when a revision replaces a prior lemma without refuting it, check the prior lemma before pricing the replacement as a retreat.** Divergence is the data — but silent convergence on a replacement is data too, and it is the harder signal.
**Resolution:** the arithmetic is settled and re-runnable. The pricing question (does §8's "retreat" get struck outright) is open to Will. See `cross_model_findings_r1.md`.

**The check — protocol and structure, not computation.** Two failures, either sufficient. The parallelism is rhetorical, carried by the phrase, not by a shared invariant. If anything the mapped structures are opposites: deferred exactness vs. enforced exactness.

## Entry 006 — The Conjugate/Antipodal Weld (Euler paper rebuild), and a guard applied only to one's own edit
**The false proposal (Claude instance, Cowork session, primed with the full repo; drafting `staging/editorial/paper-euler-single-operation.md` §3, 2026-08-06):** that A-n13's polynomial `z² − 2cos θ·z + 1 = 0` is the family Euler's identity belongs to — "Euler's identity is this family at **θ = π**," with the pentagon at θ = π/5, framed as "where the content actually sits" and "the law reappearing, not a postulate added." Filed **OFFERED / CHECKABLE**.
**The computational check (re-run at withdrawal):** the polynomial's roots are `e^{iθ}, e^{−iθ}` — the **conjugate** pair; sum `2cos θ`, product **always +1**. Every other part of the thread runs on `e^{iθ}, e^{i(θ+π)}` — the **antipodal** pair; sum **0**, which is the centroid the whole reading depends on. At θ = π the conjugate family gives a double root at −1, polynomial `(z+1)²`, pair-sum **−2** — no centroid anywhere in it. And `{+1, −1}` has product −1, so it is never a root pair of a family whose product is +1. Two symmetries — reflection versus point inversion — welded by a shared θ.
**The sharper form of the failure, offered by the drafting instance at withdrawal and NOT acted on** *(flagged for the soundness lane; if it holds it makes the charge heavier, so it must not be adopted by the instance that made the error)*: in the chain's own coordinates σ_B fixes a and negates b, which on the circle slice is complex conjugation; ν negates both, which is antipodal. On that identification the conjugate family is the **σ_B** orbit and the antipodal family the **ν** orbit — and v9.2 ruled these different in kind, σ_B structural, ν explicit, at a cost of six re-tags, a retired physics entry and a self-downgraded flagship. The weld would then be a re-fusion of a fork closed by ruling.
**What was true inside it (always stated):** the pentagon arithmetic is sound and re-verified — 2cos(π/5) = φ, 2cos(2π/5) = 1/φ, product 1, difference 1. The falsification it performs is **correct and is a genuine catch**: the "only equation with coefficients from {−1,0,1}" claim dies because the middle coefficient is −φ. Those are facts about conjugate pairs and survive the withdrawal intact. The bridge to Euler is the whole of the error.
**Attractor status: the CHECKABLE stamp as the tell.** The failure is not that a wrong thing was written — it is that it was written, self-vouched with a tag meaning *anyone can verify this by computation*, and then not verified. Same habitat as every prior entry: the payoff line, the unification, the place a reading wants to close. Distinct from Entry 005 in one respect worth recording: 005's miss was an identity asserted in prose, catchable by grammar; this one was arithmetic, and no amount of `::` discipline would have caught it. **The grammar guards do not cover the soundness lane.**
**A second finding from the same pass — a guard applied to the edit rather than to the page.** The instance gave 非 the full Shuowen-is-Han exposure in essay 3 (correctly), while leaving the structurally identical Han-projection claim two lines above — 有/亡 as "a grasping hand for presence and a fled-thing for absence" — on a bare `[candidate]` tag. ## Entry 007 — Verifying a claim in the claim's own coordinates (the glyph-corpus backup)
**The false claim (this corpus's own `archive/INVENTORY.md`, finding F, 2026-08-01; repeated without independent check by the Cowork cloud instance, 2026-08-07):** *"`data/` is gitignored on every branch, and `git ls-tree` confirms zero branches track a single glyph PNG … `data/ddj/Guodian Strip Glyphs/` (1,717 files) is the sole copy."*
**The check that settled it (Claude Code instance, `wills-imac-local`):** `wip/claude-design-system` tracks **1,073** of the files at **`ddj/archaeology/Guodian Strip Glyphs/`**, verified blob-for-blob byte-identical to the working-tree copies, a clean subset with nothing present there and absent here. **The genuinely unbacked count was 644, not 1,717.** All other figures in the finding held.
**Why the cloud instance's verification failed, and this is the transferable part.** It did run a per-branch check before repeating the claim. The pattern was `git ls-tree -r --name-only $b | grep -c '^data/ddj.*\.png'` — anchored on `data/ddj`, **which is the path the claim itself assumes.** On the branch that had the files, they live at `ddj/archaeology/`. So the search returned 0 on every branch and was read as confirmation. **A check written from the claim's own coordinates cannot falsify the claim; it can only agree with it.** The correct pattern was content-anchored — `grep -i 'Guodian Strip Glyphs/.*\.png'` — which finds them regardless of where they sit, and which returns 1,073 immediately.
**Attractor status: third instance of one shape, now named.** Ledger Entry 006 recorded a guard *"applied to the edit rather than to the page."* `site_vocab_lint.py`'s own header records a guard that *"only watches the downstream copy — watches the wrong end."* This is the same failure at the search layer: **the scope of the check inherited the scope of the thing being checked.** All three are cheap to repeat and invisible in review, because the check runs, exits clean, and looks like diligence.
**What was true inside it (always stated):** 644 files genuinely had no backup anywhere, `data/` genuinely is gitignored at `.gitignore:19`, the counts and sizes were right, and the underlying risk was real — a single drive held the only copy of 644 irreplaceable images. The finding's *action* was correct even though its *arithmetic* was not.
**Resolution:** accepted, not defended. Corrected in place at `archive/INVENTORY.md` finding F. Fixed by `1eaee9f` (all 1,717 force-added to `research-archive`). **Divergence closed under evidence with the correcting instance holding the machine and the erring instance holding the corpus** — the first entry in this ledger where the two ran on different hosts at the same time.
**Left open, not ruled:** the corpus now exists at two paths on two branches — `data/ddj/` on `research-archive`, `ddj/archaeology/` on `wip/claude-design-system`. Which is canonical is a placement decision, and placement decisions are Will's.
