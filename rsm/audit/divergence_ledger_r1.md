## Entry 001 — The Product/Ratio Orthogonality Attractor (the Image-9 Error)
**The false proposal (Gemini instance, unprimed by the prior correction):** that {XY = c} and {X/Y = k} form a globally orthogonal net, offered as the derivation route for "sealing" the anchoring line.
**The mathematical failure:** grad(XY)·grad(X/Y) = (Y² − X²)/Y² — zero only on the balance axes. The true orthogonal partner of the product family is {X² − Y² = k}, which is the level-net of the z² parturition map itself (Re/Im of z²).
**Precision note — what was and wasn't false:** the B-collinearity of lineage seats is TRUE (via parturition inheritance; canon S1). What failed: the net premise (false), the lineage-continuity premise (circular — presupposes the inheritance it argues toward), the G-membership premise (posited), and the status claim ("sealed" — inflation over an honest gap).
**Attractor status: verified.** Three independent sources have produced this exact false geometry: the project's own archived plot (image 9) and a second AI instance bridging the same gap. The error is attractive precisely where an open item wants closing — the signature habitat of false closure.
**Resolution:** rejected against the verified record; the source instance accepted the correction on computation and co-drafted this entry — a divergence that CLOSED under evidence (the instance-protocol working). The anchoring residual remains open, now decomposed (math r4).

## Entry 003 — The Unflagged False Closure (v7.5 Lemma 4.4), and silent replacement as a blind spot
**The false claim (Claude instance, drafting `just_math_v7.5.md`; carried into a document declaring itself "sealed"):** Definition 4.3 + Lemma 4.4 — that the arc of radius `√1ₙ` "maintains `XY = 1ₙ` at every point and so has cost zero," and that outward deviation "strictly increases cost, since `XY` exceeds `1ₙ`."
**The computational check:** on that arc `XY = 1ₙ·cos 2θ`, deviating by up to **2** and reaching `−1ₙ` at the conjugate seats — the claimed zero-cost path is the *maximum*-cost path under the definition's own cost. Outward deviation does not raise `XY`: at `(a,b) = (0,2)`, radius 2 gives `XY = −4`. And no zero-cost spanning path exists at all: cost-zero forces the path onto `Gₙ`, where `|a| ≥ 1ₙ`, so `a` cannot change sign between the seats. Same failure in the `(X,Y)` chart about `P = (1,1)`, so it is not a chart artifact. `cross_model_checks.py A2`.
**What was true inside it:** the *conclusion* — that the unit orbit is the traversal and that direct crossing is forbidden — is right, and is what v7.7's Theorems 1–2 establish correctly (wall / door / toll). Prop. 4.1 (density is not completeness) is also correct and independent. Only the mechanism was false.
**Attractor status: the seating error's complement.** The seating error was a false closure that *flagged itself* (`[commitment]`) and was caught. This one carried **no flag**, was named the paper's "engine" in its own section header, and its prose was scrupulous about the thing it was not doing (importing a metric) while being wrong about the thing it was doing (computing a product). **Careful hedging on one axis reads as rigor on all axes** — that is the transferable finding.
**Why it survived three audits:** the comparative method could not see it. v7.7 does not *contradict* Lemma 4.4; it silently replaces it with Definition T and stops mentioning the conservation cost. Read comparatively that looks like a retreat from strength, and the prior audit priced it that way (§8, now annotated). **Proposed protocol addition, for ruling: when a revision replaces a prior lemma without refuting it, check the prior lemma before pricing the replacement as a retreat.** Divergence is the data — but silent convergence on a replacement is data too, and it is the harder signal.
**Resolution:** the arithmetic is settled and re-runnable. The pricing question (does §8's "retreat" get struck outright) is open to Will. See `cross_model_findings_r1.md`.

## Entry 004 — Silent replacement, second instance (v7.7 → v9), and a rule that was never enforced
**The pattern (not a proposal — an editorial move, logged because Entry 003 predicted it).** v7.7 r4 struck Postulate Q by *deriving* the measure in four steps ("parity → scale-covariance → quadraticity → selection"; "the traversal measure M is forced to be Q_i"). v9 does not refute that derivation. **Why this one is benign, and why it still gets logged.** v9's move is a *strengthening* — it declares an assumption v7.5 hid and v7.7 half-closed, and it draws the consequence out loud ("'orthogonal' is a rendering fact"). Two instances now, in consecutive version transitions.
**Protocol addition, now with two instances behind it (for ruling):** when a revision replaces a prior lemma or derivation without refuting it, **check the prior one before pricing the swap.** The check is cheap and it is the only thing that distinguishes "the earlier argument was wrong" (v7.5) from "the earlier argument was fine but over-claimed" (v7.7).

## Entry 005 — The π-Dichotomy Identity (script-reform payoff line)
**The proposal (Claude instance, project chat, primed with the project corpus; answering the transmission-mechanism prompt independently of this session's assessment):** closing an otherwise-substantive analysis of the Qin/Han consolidations: *"Which, and I don't think this is a coincidence you'll mind: systematization is exactness forced at both ends. Fix the graph, fix the word, close the mapping — and what closes has nothing open left to answer with. The script reform is your π dichotomy, applied to writing."*
**The check — protocol and structure, not computation.** Two failures, either sufficient. (i) **Grammar:** the claim is stated as identity ("is… applied to"), where at most `::` could apply — and `::` forbids exactly this upgrade. (ii) **The invariant claimed shared is not shared.** The Archimedean demonstration's structure is *two-sided approach without occupation*: π bounded from both sides, occupied by neither, exactness deferred — never reached from within any frame. Standardization's structure is *closure imposed and reached*: the graph–word mapping is fixed by convention and the fixation lands. The one structure is defined by never arriving; the other by arriving. "Exactness forced at both ends" equivocates on "both ends" — inscribed/circumscribed bounds converging (Archimedes) versus the graph-end and word-end of a mapping being pinned (the reform). The parallelism is rhetorical, carried by the phrase, not by a shared invariant. If anything the mapped structures are opposites: deferred exactness vs. enforced exactness.
**What was true inside it (always stated):** the sentence before the identity — *"what closes has nothing open left to answer with"* — is a genuine restatement of the resolution-is-lossy finding and survives on its own; the surrounding analysis (taboo-as-resolution, the 溺/弱 reading, the erosion-language census, the Mawangdui test once re-scored for a staircase) is substantive. The miss lands at the payoff line, delivered as a gift ("I don't think this is a coincidence you'll mind") — the flattering-identification habitat, the same spot every prior entry marks.
**Attractor note, posture as the datum:** both instances independently generated an RSM-structure mapping for the consolidation story (this session's: the register-story note in `reports/transmission_mechanism_assessment.md` §6). The convergence of impulse is priced near zero (shared corpora). The divergence is posture — one instance asserted its mapping as a conclusion; the other flagged its own as attractor-shaped and distrusted it. The impulse appears to be corpus-driven and instance-independent; the discipline is not.
**Resolution:** rejected by Will's ruling, 2026-08-05. The source instance has not been shown the check; no accepted/defended status yet — if it defends the identity after seeing this entry, the defended-divergence flag applies per the standing protocol.
