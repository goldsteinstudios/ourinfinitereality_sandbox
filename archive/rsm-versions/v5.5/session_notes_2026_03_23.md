## Key New Structural Observations (need to cure before carving)

### The |1| Cascade (Dimensionality Derivation)

Tightest statement of the dimensionality argument:

- |0| can't hold ≡ distinction obtains (一) ≡ two-sided (二)
- Two sides infinitely separated = |1| can't hold
- Requires perpendicular turn (first orthogonal axis)
- Plane recreates |1| one dimension up
- Requires second perpendicular turn
- Three axes span all directions — no remaining |1|
- Sphere = ⟂³ = the first complete closure with no inaccessible regions

Each step is an identity, not a production. 道生一一生二二生三三生萬物 as one fact at increasing resolution, not a creation sequence.

**Status:** Candidate for §8.0 or §8 replacement in core theorem. Needs to cure.

### Two Configurations of Euler's Identity

- **Hold the center, circumference varies:** The living tree. e drives growth. Center actively maintained by circulation/water/pressure. Configuration 1.
- **Hold the circumference, center is paradoxical:** The sphere. π governs closure. Center is the hollow, forbidden point. Configuration 2.
- Both fixed = P₀ (frozen). Both free = P₀ (dissolved). Exactly one must be held.
- The transition from Configuration 1 to 2 = the tree dying. e stops. π remains as record.
- Euler identity reread: 0 = passage of world through center, 1 = boundary as act, e = rate of self-maintenance, i = turn from expansion to circulation, π = ratio of one full cycle of closure.

**Status:** Observation-level. :: reading through biological instrument. Needs to cure. Future essay.

### 一二三 Reading

- 一 = this. The something that must exist because nothing can't.
- 二 = this and that. Co-emergent. The gap between the two lines IS the structural content.
- 三 = this and that, connected without merging. Minimum structure for interaction across a gap.
- Stroke combinatorics confirm: one stroke = 4 forms, two strokes = dozens, three strokes = explosion. Three is where closure first becomes possible and the space exceeds what you can hold.

**Status:** Reading needs paleographic checking. The logic is clean. The mapping onto graphic forms is empirical.

### The Winnowing Refinement

- Wind sorts (separates already-loose chaff from grain).
- Crashing breaks (loosens chaff that's still attached to grain through repeated impact).
- Both operations are needed. The wind alone can't remove attached chaff.
- Cross-system testing = crashing (multiple angles, accumulated cracks at attachment points).
- "What if" questions = the toss (both wind and crash simultaneously).
- The methodology is winnowing by "what if" — identify concept, find wrong shelf, find right shelf, frame relocation as "what if," let the system sort.
- 棄 as inverted infant: what returns from the toss is pre-categorical perception, the mind stripped of framework-accretions.

### The Prompt Sequence

Four "what if" questions, each targeting a specific category error:

1. "Can void exist without not-void to be void of?" → relocates void from "possible state" to "incoherent" → clears §1–§2.2
2. "What if conservation is logical and not physical?" → relocates conservation from "physics" to "logic" → clears §2.3–§2.4
3. "Is mathematics a language that describes structure, or is it the structure it describes?" → relocates geometry from "modeling" to "identification" → targets §2.5
4. "Do dimensions exist before the things that require them, or do the things that require them generate the dimensions?" → relocates dimensions from "container" to "consequence" → targets §8

Prompts 1–2 are tested and proven effective across multiple independent systems. Prompts 3–4 are untested candidates.
