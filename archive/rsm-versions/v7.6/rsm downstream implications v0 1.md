## Tier 1 — Derivation-adjacent: the logic seems to force these

### 1. The co-frame: every frame comes paired *[derivation-adjacent; possibly derives Postulate R]*

Postulate R invokes the conjugate conservation structure {Q_j = −1ₙ} — in mode coordinates, **Xₙ·Yₙ = −1ₙ** — only to calibrate γ. But look at what that object *is*: a two-variable conserved-product relation at unit magnitude, with two branches exchanged by a symmetry (ν∘σ_B, verified), its vertices on Mₙ, sharing Oₙ and the orbit with the original. By every structural criterion the chain uses to call something a frame, **the conjugate structure is a frame** — rotated a quarter-turn, reading the parent's mediator seats as its mode seats and the parent's mode seats as its mediators. Its native measure is −Q_j; the orbit invariant Q_i = u + v is *shared*.

Two consequences:

- **Postulate R may be derivable.** R says the conjugate vertices carry one unit. But if the conjugate structure is a frame, that's just *seat calibration applied to it* — the same step that gave α = 1. R reduces to a frame-universality principle: any unit-magnitude conserved-product structure is a frame subject to the same calibration. That is more primitive than "parity," and it is internally motivated by the chain's own definitions. One circularity check required: the argument must not use the metric (which R helps generate) to identify the conjugate structure — and it doesn't appear to: XY = −1ₙ, its branches, and its exchange symmetry are all pre-metric objects. **This is the most promising attack on open item #3.**
- The orientation choice ±i (which mediator half the traversal takes) becomes the choice of *which co-frame you pair with* — the sign ambiguity acquires structural content.

Physics shadow: in the oscillator instance, xp = −const is simply the other pair of phase-space quadrants — the co-frame is physically inhabited in the flagship instance already. *[candidate]*

Structural footnote (verified): the four seats carry two order-4 groups — the Klein group {id, σ_B, ν, νσ_B} (the frame's symmetries) and the cyclic Z₄ generated by i (the traversal); together they generate the dihedral group of the square. The symmetry algebra of a frame is D₄, previously unstated.

### 2. Postulate F falls to the resolution horizon — and the forbidden disk gets an interpretation *[derivation-adjacent; attacks open item #2]*

F (Q_t ≥ 1ₙ pointwise) has resisted derivation. Try this: 1ₙ is *defined* as frame n's minimal unit of distinction. The byproduct is better than the derivation. The standoff interior {Q_i < 1ₙ} — currently just "forbidden" — becomes **the resolution horizon**: the region where distinction exists but not at frame n's grain. And that is exactly where the recursion says finer structure lives: from the parent's view, a child frame's entire ℝ³ is unresolvable, located "at" a point. The forbidden disk is not empty; it is *where the children are*, as seen by a parent that cannot resolve them. The unoccupiable center and the finer-scale worlds are the same fact at two descriptions. This unifies the standoff, the recursion, and the no-terminal-resolution premise into one picture, and it has a clean physics shadow: what sits below a regime's resolution floor is not nothing but the next regime (sub-Planck structure as child-frame content, not forbidden territory). *[interior reading: candidate; F argument: derivation-adjacent]*

### 3. Winding classes: quantization falls out — and it forces a correction to the chain *[derivation-adjacent; chain correction]*

The framework's geometry predicts exactly this class of phenomenon: quantized circulation wherever an unoccupiable center organizes flow. *[candidate; verify against sources; sharp]*

Then elapsed structural time is *the winding count registered at the aperture* — cyclic motion, linear accumulation. This resolves, rather than papers over, the ancient cyclic-vs-linear tension the chains inherited: **the orbit repeats; the genealogy doesn't.** The frame's natural clock is its own traversal through its paradoxical centers, and "linear succession" (already derived from irreversibility) acquires a discrete metric: ticks = aperture crossings. *[derivation-adjacent for the structure; the physical-time identification candidate]*

### 4. In three dimensions, topology sorts persistence into two kinds — deriving the toroidal through-line *[derivation-adjacent; upgrades physics predictions 1 and 6]*

The winding argument works because a punctured *plane* has π₁ = ℤ. In ℝ³ the complement of a **point** is simply connected — no path can be topologically trapped around a point in three dimensions. The complement of a **line** has π₁ = ℤ. Therefore, given the no-crossing premise:

**Topologically protected persistence in ℝ³ requires the unoccupiable center to be extended — a curve, codimension 2 — not a point.** The generic topologically persistent form is then flow around an axis: the torus. Through-line 9 (toroidal structures), currently a catalogue of instances, becomes a *theorem-shaped consequence*: tokamak axes, vortex filaments, flux tubes, magnetospheric axes, the pith, the gut — all line-centers, as they must be.

And a point-center in ℝ³, unable to protect topologically, can only persist **dynamically** — by an energy floor (prediction 6, zero-point costs). So persistence splits into a derived taxonomy with *different observable signatures*:

| Class | Center | Protection | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Topological | line (codim 2) | winding class | **quantized** circulation/flux; discrete decay (reconnection events) |
| Dynamical | point | energy floor | continuous spectra above a **non-zero minimum**; thermal decay |

Prediction 1 ("no persistent structure without a paradoxical center") sharpens into two falsifiable sub-predictions, each with its own failure mode. This is the single sharpest new claim in this note. *[the topology is certain; the taxonomy as physics is candidate and testable]*

The constant λ is then the framework's single free scale parameter, with the curvature anchor (standoff = radius of curvature = √1ₙ) as the natural place to compute it.
