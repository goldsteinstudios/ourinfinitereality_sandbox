## Verbal and stamp discipline

In prose without `::`, correspondence is preserved by "to me, that looks just like…", never "X is Y." `::` forbids substitution, upgrade to identity, and congruence by default. 也 (Guodian 也者…也, the definitional stamp) :: the derivation's own ≡; the multi-century stripping of 也 (Guodian → Mawangdui → Wang Bi) is the received tradition committing the framework's named CAVP failure — treating a theorem as an observation — on the text itself, one copyist at a time.
