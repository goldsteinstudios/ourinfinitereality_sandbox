# RSM — Parallax Reconsidered: The Evidential Unit Is the Forced Correction

**Working note v0.1.** Machine-drafted (Claude, Fable 5) at Will's request. Analysis register, not canon. This note takes the v7.5 parallax document's own honesty clauses seriously enough to push on them, uses the v7.6 consolidation session as data, and proposes a restructured evidentiary standard for the parallax rewrite. Nothing here weakens the derivation; everything here concerns what the convergence is evidence *of*.

-----

## 1. What parallax claims, and where the analogy breaks

Astronomical parallax works because the baseline is *known*: two viewpoints at a measured separation, and the measured angular shift converts to distance precisely because the viewpoints' independence is quantified. The v7.5 document already concedes the framework's version lacks this — "convergence among registers assembled by one author" does not substitute for external verification — but treats the concession as a caveat appended to a method that otherwise stands.

The fresh claim of this note: it is not a caveat; it is the method's central unsolved problem, and it is *measurable*. The registers' baseline — their actual degree of independence — is currently unknown, but it is not unknowable. Section 5 gives the instruments.

## 2. The consolidation session as data

The v7.6 session is the best probe of the method that exists so far, because it produced a correction record under observation. Three architectural corrections landed (Oₙ/Pₙ, the parturition form, the ground; then the big-bang slotting), and the record shows an asymmetry worth taking seriously:

**The lag asymmetry.** Twice, the math chain was corrected *toward* structure the DDJ chain already carried: the parturition form (生: motherwoman + child, nothing consumed) and identity-through-opposition as the ground (P₀ and the recursion it grounds; not parturition). The DDJ register was never corrected toward the math register on architecture; the flow ran one way.

Three readings of the asymmetry:

- **(i) The strong reading.** The DDJ compression genuinely encodes the structure at a resolution the formalization is still catching up to. This is the reading the project wants, and the asymmetry is consistent with it.
- **(ii) The deflationary reading.** All registers express one underlying structural intuition (Will's). The DDJ chain, being a reading rather than a derivation, is the register where that intuition is expressed with the *least resistance* — nothing in a reading can fail the way a proof fails. The math chain is the register where errors are detectable. So corrections necessarily flow math → alignment-with-DDJ, and the asymmetry measures falsifiability differentials between registers, not the DDJ's insight. Under this reading the "convergence" is one model being debugged toward its own least-checkable expression.
- **(iii) The instrumental reading.** The registers are not three witnesses; they are one intuition, one falsifiable formalization, and one ancient compression against which reach is tested. The asymmetry then reflects a real division of labor: the math register's job is to be the place where things can *break*.

The session cannot decide between (i) and (ii) — that is exactly the unknown-baseline problem. But it can score the individual corrections, which is more informative than the aggregate:

## 3. An independence audit: three grades, not one

"Independent registers" flattens a distinction the method needs. Each register mixes an independent component with an authored component, and the evidential force lives only in the independent part:

- **DDJ register.** The *received text* is independent — ancient, fixed, not authored by Will, and (via the Guodian strips) physically anterior to the framework by two and a half millennia. The *readings* are not independent: they are authored, LLM-assisted, and produced with the framework in view. The text can testify only where it *resists* a wanted reading or where a reading was committed before the formal result existed.
- **Physics register.** The *facts* are independent — λf = c, the uncertainty floor, Sp(2,ℝ) ≅ SU(1,1), the manifold excision of the initial singularity are true regardless of the framework. The *selection* is not: a large corpus was searched for fits, and the misses are not logged. Unlogged selection converts a convergence claim into a multiple-comparisons problem. The register's honest instruments are the correction record (Boyle vs the ideal gas law is a logged miss — good) and predictions sharp enough to fail.
- **Math register.** The *proofs* are independent in the strongest sense available — Theorems 1–3, the γ gauge freedom, and the curvature computations check under symbolic verification that has no access to intent. The *choice of what to formalize* is not independent. The math register's unique value is that it is the only register that has repeatedly said **no** to its own author: density-not-completeness, the γ postulate, the seat/center conflation, mirror osculation.

Summary: **the text is independent but the reading isn't; the facts are independent but the selection isn't; the proofs are independent but the agenda isn't.** A rewritten parallax document should say this in exactly this form, because it locates precisely where evidence can and cannot come from.

## 4. The reframe: forced corrections as the evidential unit

Under shared authorship, convergences are cheap: the same mind, assisted by the same class of pattern-completing tools, will find the same shape everywhere it looks — the v7.5 document's own warning about `::` pattern-completion pressure. What is *expensive* under shared authorship is a register refusing the author: a proof that fails, a text that won't yield the wanted line, a physical relation that fits only after being weakened (PV = nRT → Boyle). Expensive signals are informative signals.

**Proposal: the parallax document's evidentiary core should be a forced-corrections ledger, not a through-line list.** The through-lines remain as the map of the convergence; the ledger is the argument that there is a territory. The framework already possesses a substantial ledger — it has simply been distributed across version footers instead of being read as evidence:

1. Density, not completeness — the infinity conditional refused to supply continuity (v2.1 Prop 4.1).
2. PV = nRT downgraded to Boyle's law — the general form refused the mapping.
3. The γ gauge freedom — the construction refused to fix the mediator scale; Postulate R had to be named and paid for.
4. The seat/center conflation — the geometry refused the orbit about Pₙ (v0.1 Theorems 1–2).
5. Mirror osculation — the computation refused the osculating-circle claim, against the drafting model's assertion.
6. The unit floor and traversal continuity — the corrected order refused to let two premises stay implicit (Postulates F, T).
7. The big-bang ground-slotting — the chains' own no-terminal-regime claim refused the placement.
8. E = mc² held at candidate — the equivalence refused to be an inverse conservation.

Eight instances of a register saying no. **A register that can say no is a register that can testify.** This is the framework's genuine evidential asset, and it is currently underweighted relative to the convergence catalogue.

Corollary for the findings: re-ranked under this lens, the framework's strongest results are (1) the forced downgrades themselves, as demonstration that a constraint-bearing object is under study; (2) the mode-disconnection theorem, because it is the sharpest thing the formal register has produced and it *upgraded* a DDJ entry (相生) rather than the reverse — the only clean counter-instance to the lag asymmetry; (3) the oscillator instance, pending source verification, as the densest single retrodictive convergence; (4) the manifold-excision fit for the parturition origin. The weakest findings remain the candidate-stack through-lines (toroidal persistence, energy-as-misalignment) where every leg is a candidate and no leg has yet refused anything.

## 5. Measuring the baseline: five controls

The unknown-baseline problem is empirical. Each of these is cheap relative to what it buys:

**5.1 The control corpus.** Run the identical `::` procedure, with identical effort, against a comparably compressed ancient text *not* used in construction — candidates: Heraclitus fragments, the Yijing Xici commentary, Parmenides' poem, Nāgārjuna's MMK. Two outcomes: if correspondence density is much lower than the DDJ's, the method has discriminative power and the DDJ convergence means something; if comparable density appears everywhere, the convergence has been measuring the method's generosity, not the DDJ's structure. **This is the single most powerful test available to the project.** (It also has a win-win character: high density in a genuinely independent second text would itself be a parallax result — a fourth register — if it survives the same discipline.)

**5.2 The permutation control.** Run the reading procedure against a chapter-shuffled or line-permuted DDJ. Structural correspondences that survive permutation were never about the text's structure.

**5.3 Preregistered readings.** Before formalizing the next math result, commit — in writing, versioned — the prediction of which DDJ material should carry it and how. Then check. Postdicted fits (the current default) are P2 evidence; predicted fits are P1 (see §6). The 玄 dual-reading proposal currently pending is a natural first case: the geometry motivated it before the Guodian layer has weighed it.

**5.4 Blind reformalization.** Hand the foundational conditional plus the gradient premise — nothing else — to an independent mathematician, or to a fresh LLM instance with no RSM context, and ask what geometry follows. Convergence with the chain from a contextless start is baseline-independent evidence; divergence localizes exactly which steps are forced and which are authored. (The v0.1 note's demand that "nothing enter canon without independent checking" is this test in miniature; this session's symbolic verification is a weak version of it.)

**5.5 One sharp prediction.** The six falsifiable predictions are commitments in form but not yet in force: "paradoxical center" and "persistence" are not operationalized to the point where a measurement could embarrass them. Pick one — the power-law nesting prediction is the most operationalizable — define the observables, and state the number that would falsify it. Until one prediction can actually fail, the physics register contributes retrodiction only, and the parallax stands on two legs and a decoration.

## 6. Provenance tagging: a third axis for CAVP

CAVP separates accuracy from precision. The audit above shows a third axis doing silent work: **provenance**. Proposal — every `::` entry and every physics correspondence carries one of:

- **P1 — predicted.** The pointing was committed before the formal result or before the passage was consulted. (Currently rare; §5.3 manufactures them.)
- **P2 — found.** The pointing was identified after the formal result existed. (The current default: the oscillator instance, manifold excision, sum/difference invariants.)
- **P3 — imported.** The register was adjusted to match another register or the shared model. (The parturition notation; honest to say so.)

P1 entries carry evidential weight against the shared-authorship objection; P2 entries carry reach; P3 entries carry coherence only. Tagging costs one token per entry and converts the method's central vulnerability from an unmeasured caveat into a tracked variable. Over versions, the P1:P2:P3 ratio becomes the framework's measured baseline — the thing the parallax analogy has been missing.

## 7. Propagation to the v7.6 parallax rewrite

Independent of the reframe, the through-lines need re-basing on the corrected architecture: TL2 (center required-but-unoccupiable) splits across Oₙ (unoccupiable orbital center) and Pₙ (paradoxical-generative center) with the cross-frame identification as the through-line's new spine; TL3 carries the parturition form and gains the ground as its base case; the master skeleton inserts the ground before the frame apparatus and replaces the Pₙ → O₍ₙ₊₁₎ arrow; a candidate new through-line — the generative proportion, Pₙ : O₍ₙ₊₁₎ :: P₍ₙ₋₁₎ : Oₙ :: P₀ : O₁ — is available and is the most parallax-shaped result the framework now has (one ratio, three registers already pointing at it: the proportion in math, the three forms of generative relation in the DDJ, the persisting-parent regime structure in physics).

Recommended structure for the rewrite: invariant-first through-lines retained; a **Forced Corrections** section elevated to co-equal evidentiary status (§4's ledger); a **Controls** section with the status of §5's five tests; provenance tags throughout per §6; the independence audit of §3 replacing the current single-paragraph concession.

## 8. What the method establishes, restated

If the controls come back favorable — control-corpus density low, permutation kills the fits, P1 entries accumulate, blind reformalization converges — then the parallax method establishes what v7.5 claims, with a measured baseline where it currently has a disclaimer. If they come back unfavorable, the framework does not lose its derivation (the math stands or falls on its own), but the convergence catalogue loses its status as evidence and becomes what the deflationary reading says it is: one model, three costumes.

Either outcome is worth more than the current state, in which the question is politely acknowledged and structurally unasked. The project's stated standard — credibility through explicit downgrade-and-repair — applied to the method itself, is this note.
