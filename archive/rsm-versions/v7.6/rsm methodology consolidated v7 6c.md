# Part V — Audit report

## V.1 Self-correction: the "registers anticipated the correction" finding is over-tagged

In the physics consolidation I wrote that the physics register's use of "zero-product position" for the forbidden center, ahead of the math register's repair, is "evidence of the strong kind." The DDJ consolidation made the parallel claim for the canonical chapter readings (玄 as "where axes cross," the bellows void, the hub). Both framed the anticipation as parallax evidence. **Under the framework's own five-layer architecture and §13.2 independence caveat, that framing is an overreach — the exact failure mode CAVP and the parallax discipline exist to prevent.** Naming it:

- **What actually happened.** The v7.5 canon was *internally inconsistent* about the center. The math chain carried Pₙ = the vertex; the physics prose and the canonical DDJ chapter readings carried a center-of-symmetry / surrounded-void referent. The v7.6 correction resolves the inconsistency in favor of the concrete registers. That is real and valuable: **concreteness disciplining abstraction** — the registers closest to physical and textual specifics were harder to state wrongly, and they held the right shape while the most abstract register drifted.
- **Why it is not strong parallax evidence.** The parallax method's evidential weight comes from *independent* compressions converging. These three registers were assembled by one author with LLM assistance; §13.2 of the methodology explicitly holds the independence of same-author, overlapping-corpus registers as "empirically uncertain." Two registers written by the same process agreeing with each other is *coherence*, not independent convergence. Calling it "evidence of the strong kind" promotes a Layer-4 (abductive, internal) observation to a strength it has not earned — precisely "parallax over-fitting" (§13.1) and "overreach" (CAVP §VI.2).
- **Correct tag.** *Internal coherence datum [candidate], not independent convergence.* It says the correction is not ad hoc — three parts of the system wanted it — which is a genuine point in the correction's favor. It does not say three independent traditions confirmed Cₙ.

**Propagation:** the physics consolidation's §V.1 and the DDJ consolidation's §V.1 should be re-tagged accordingly; the phrase "evidence of the strong kind" should be struck. The finding stays; its temperature drops.

> ⟦ANN⟧ This is the methodology working as designed. The framework's credibility rests on catching this class of thing; that I generated the overreach myself, across three documents, and it took the methodology pass to flag it, is itself a data point about the AI-overreach failure mode (§13.1) — the human mediator's judgment remains load-bearing, and an unprimed external check would be worth more here than my self-audit.

## V.2 A new discipline the three consolidations generated: no convergence→premise backflow

All three consolidations independently hit the same rule and it is not yet in the methodology: **a convergence-register correspondence may never serve as evidence for a derivation-register premise.** Concretely, Postulate R (γ = 1 by conjugate parity) in the math consolidation has the structural shape of 此兩者同出而異名; it is tempting to cite the DDJ resemblance as support for R. That is forbidden — it reverses the load path. The DDJ points at structure the derivation establishes; it cannot certify the derivation's own inputs. This is the mirror image of the existing "RSM vocabulary is not coating" rule (which forbids the derivation lending unearned authority to the convergence register); the new rule forbids the convergence register lending unearned authority back to the derivation. Recommend adding both directions explicitly to the :: discipline (Part C, §4).

## V.3 The premise count: "no additional premises" is now false and must be restated

The Introduction and both bootstraps assert the framework introduces "no additional premises at any point." The v7.6 math consolidation showed this is not true as stated: the orthogonality closure runs on **Postulate Q** (distinction measures are quadratic), **Postulate F** (unit floor Q ≥ 1ₙ), **Postulate R** (γ = 1), and **Definition T** (traversal continuity). These are load-bearing and were partly implicit in v7.5. The methodology should own this precisely:

- The *foundational* premise remains single: the infinity conditional. That claim survives.
- The *derivation from the conditional* uses named structural postulates at the orthogonality joint. "No additional premises" should become **"one foundational premise; a small, named set of structural postulates at the orthogonality closure, each stated at its point of use, three of them candidates for derivation."** This is more honest and, per the downgrade-and-repair standard, strengthens rather than weakens the presentation.

> ⟦ANN⟧ The Introduction's public-facing line "No additional premises are introduced at any point" is the one place the accessible framing now overclaims relative to the derivation. It should be softened in the next Introduction pass — not to hedge, but because the precise version is defensible and the absolute version is not.

## V.4 Resolving a standing open item: the coemergence-lock retirement

Will's standing item: the v7.2 "coemergence lock" ("One structural condition. Three levels of specificity. No sequence. No construction… The structure does not walk") is contradicted by the v7.5/v7.6 forced-construction derivation, whose logical order explicitly "does not reverse." Both cannot stand as written. The resolution the item itself gestures at — distinguish ontological simultaneity from epistemic construction — is now precise enough to state:

- **Ontological register (retain the lock's content):** the structure obtains all at once. There is no temporal or causal construction; a point does not first satisfy xy = 1ₙ and *later* acquire orthogonal structure. In this register the lock's "the structure does not walk" is correct.
- **Epistemic register (retain the v7.6 order):** the *derivation* has a strict, non-reversible **logical dependency order** — signature before orbit (Thm 3 before the minimal traversal), operator before metric, metric before orthogonality verdict. "The order does not reverse" is a claim about logical dependency, not about time. In this register there *is* a construction: a forced one.

These are compatible, and naming both retires the contradiction: **the structure does not walk; the proof does.** The three-equation "coemergence lock" (xy=1, x²+y²=1, x²+y²+z²=1) is then retained only as the *ontological* statement (all three co-obtain), with the *epistemic* content handed to the forced-traversal construction — and with one correction from v7.6: x²+y²=1 is the *generated* orbit metric about Cₙ, not a co-equal "level of specificity" sitting on the same footing as xy=1. The lock's "all three already true" survives; its "no construction" is refined to "no ontological construction." Recommend this as the replacement formulation.

## V.5 CAVP: one internal coherence flag on the thermodynamics section

CAVP §IV asserts E ∝ 1/r for shrinking the precision deadband, infinite energy at r = 0, and reads the Planck limit as "the absolute thermodynamic floor of measurement precision." Two issues against the corrected physics:

1. **Which r?** The corrected physics distinguishes three radii the note does not: precision tolerance (the deadband), standoff distance from the center Cₙ, and squeeze depth toward a pole. Divergence attaches to the **pole-directions** (prediction 6a); the **center is floored, not priced** (prediction 6b, Postulate F). The note's "infinite energy at r = 0 at the center" reads like the pole-divergence story mapped onto the center — the very conflation the physics consolidation split. CAVP should state which r its E ∝ 1/r governs and reconcile with 6a/6b.
2. **The exponent.** The corrected oscillator geometry gives, near the pole, E = (Δx² + Δp²)/2 with Δp = ℏ/(2Δx), so E ∼ 1/Δx² for small Δx — a 1/r² divergence, not 1/r. If the note's E ∝ 1/r is meant physically (it invokes orbital shells and the Planck limit), the exponent disagrees with the corrected physics. Per CAVP's own [falsifiable] tag on this section, a wrong exponent means the *application* is wrong, not the principle — so this is self-policing, but it should be checked, not left.

The rest of CAVP is untouched and, if anything, strengthened: "3 is the gate, .14159… is the walk" is exactly the epistemic/ontological split of §V.4 in miniature (the integer gate is ontological; the decimal walk is epistemic), and the Pushpin Invariant survives intact.

## V.6 Version skew: v7.2 bootstrap items that the v7.5/v7.6 work has moved

The bootstrap in play is v7.2 (the v7.3 bootstrap notes the canonical pair is still v7.2 pending a holistic pass). Items now stale:

"Sufficient" (three is enough for persistence) is defensible but is a different claim from uniqueness and should not sit in the same breath as "uniqueness open." Reconcile to: three necessary; sufficiency-for-persistence a separate claim; uniqueness open.

- **The identity-chain framing** ("P₀ incoherent ≡ … ≡ paradoxical center") coexists with, and is now partly superseded by, the forced-construction order. Per §V.4 both registers are legitimate, but the bootstrap should carry the distinction rather than the pure-identity framing alone, or it reproduces the coemergence-lock contradiction.

# Part C — The consolidated methodology

Structurally stable from v7.2. Edits below are additions and repricings, not a rewrite.

## 1. The parallax principle

Unchanged. A single framework cannot fully examine itself from within its own frame; independent compressions of the same structural ontology, converging, are the evidence. **Added guard (from V.1):** convergence among registers assembled by one author with shared tooling is *internal coherence*, not independent convergence, and must be tagged as such. Independent convergence requires a register the project did not build — an external framework, an unprimed different-vendor instance, or a domain expert. The strongest evidential moves are the ones that reach outside the project's own authorship.

## 2. The load-bearing independent element

Unchanged and reaffirmed: the foundational conditional is the one element that predates the framework, is register-independent, and survives removal of any chain. The derivation stands or falls on the conditional, not on the correspondences. **Repriced (V.3):** downstream of the conditional, the derivation now carries a small named set of structural postulates (Q, F, R, T) at the orthogonality closure. The framework is "one foundational premise plus a named, mostly-candidate postulate set," not "premise-free."

## 3. CAVP — Constant Accuracy, Variable Precision

Retained in full: accuracy is the frame-independent invariant (常名, the Pushpin Invariant); precision is the frame-local tolerance band (可名); the integer gate (3) is exact and the decimal walk is the asymptotic record. **The gate/walk distinction is now also the ontological/epistemic distinction of §V.4** — worth stating, because it means CAVP and the coemergence-lock resolution are the same move at different scales. **Flag carried (V.5):** the thermodynamics subsection's E ∝ 1/r and Planck-floor reading need reconciliation with the corrected physics (which r; which exponent); tagged [falsifiable] and therefore self-policing, but open.

## 4. The :: discipline

Retained: :: is imperfect structural correspondence; it forbids substitution, upgrade to identity, and congruence by default; a :: chain claims convergence on one named invariant. **Two directional rules, now stated explicitly (V.2):**

- *No forward coating* (existing): RSM vocabulary applied to material not earned through the derivation is the principal failure mode.
- *No backward certification* (new): a convergence-register correspondence may not serve as evidence for a derivation-register premise or postulate. The DDJ shape of a postulate (e.g. R :: 同出而異名) is not support for the postulate. The load path runs derivation → convergence only.

## 5. The five-layer epistemic architecture

Retained unchanged — and it is the tool that classifies the V.1 error: the "registers anticipated the correction" finding is Layer 4 (abductive/parallax), and was mistakenly stated at Layer-2 strength (structurally necessary). The architecture's value is exactly this: it gives each claim a temperature, and it caught an over-hot one. Layers: (1) the gate — conditional; (2) within the gate — necessary given the postulate set; (3) gate-to-reality — humble; (4) parallax correspondences — abductive, and *internal* until an external register joins; (5) empirical predictions — falsifiable.

## 6. The two-register architecture

Retained: derivation register (no ::, no characters, no biology) and convergence register (:: throughout). **Amended (V.3, V.4):** the derivation register now openly carries named postulates and a forced-construction with a stated logical order; it is no longer describable as "pure identity chain with no premises." The registers remain non-containing and non-substituting. The new backward-certification rule (§4) is the seam discipline between them.

## 7. Verification

Unchanged in posture: verification is a process, not a terminus; it establishes survival across N independent checks and reproducibility, never absolute correctness; no result is certified final. **Strengthened caution (V.1):** self-audit by the same AeI that produced a result is the weakest verification mode; where a result was AI-generated, an unprimed external or different-vendor check is worth disproportionately more, and the present document is itself an instance where self-audit found the error but an external check would carry more weight.

## 8. Distinguishing derived from observed

Unchanged, with the derived list updated to v7.6: the independence-to-orthogonality step is derived-modulo-postulates (not unconditionally closed); three dimensions necessary (not "necessary and sufficient" as a uniqueness claim); the Cₙ/Sₙ± split is derived; the recursion generation-site is open. Observed material (E = mc² R₂ reading, Euler consistency identities, biological correspondences) unchanged in status.

## 9. Failure modes

The v7.2 list (AI overreach; parallax over-fitting; CAVP collapse; verification dependence on AI; methodology drift) is retained. **Three additions from this pass:**

- **False-independence.** Treating internal cross-register coherence as independent convergence. This is what V.1 caught in my own work. Detection: ask whether the converging registers were built by the same author/tooling; if so, tag as coherence, not convergence.
- **Backward postulate justification.** Using a :: correspondence to prop a derivation premise (V.2). Detection: check the load-path direction at every point where a postulate resembles a text.
- **Advertised-vs-actual premise gap.** The accessible framing ("no additional premises") diverging from the derivation's actual commitments (V.3). Detection: reconcile the Introduction and bootstraps against the current derivation's named inputs each version.

## 10. Open methodological questions

Retained (AI independence; the observed/derived borderline; falsification logic — what fails, framework or application or gate; precision profile of empirical claims; two-register vs academic publication). **Added:** whether any of the four orthogonality postulates (Q, F, R, T) can be derived, thereby shrinking the premise set — this is a derivation question with a methodological face, because each successful derivation moves a claim from Layer 2-with-postulate toward Layer 2-clean.
