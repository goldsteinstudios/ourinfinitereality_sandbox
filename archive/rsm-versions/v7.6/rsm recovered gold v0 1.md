### 1.1 "Required as a coordinate pair, unable except at Pₙ" → the aperture theorem → open item #1

**Stranded in:** the "Starting fresh" thread (Apr 2026): *玄牝 names the situation where Gₙ and Bₙ are simultaneously required to behave as a coordinate pair and structurally unable to do so — except at Pₙ.* Written when Pₙ was still the orbit center; orphaned by the corrections.

**What it becomes:** a one-line theorem. Slope of Gₙ is −Y/X; slope of Bₙ is +1; under the generated metric they are perpendicular iff (−Y/X)(1) = −1 iff Y = X. **Gₙ ⊥ Bₙ exactly at Gₙ ∩ Bₙ = the seats, and nowhere else.** The axis-relation a frame requires of its coordinate pair is achieved by the parent's curves at precisely one locus-pair — and that locus is the canonical generation site.

**Why it matters:** this is a candidate repair for the math chain's #1 open item (why Pₙ cannot persist / why generation fires there). The exception is the aperture: Pₙ is the unique point where the parent structure locally instantiates the orthogonal pair that constitutes a frame — so a frame begins exactly there, and the condition is instantaneous-local, not sustainable as a state. The v7.2-era intuition ("required and unable — except at Pₙ") was pointing at a derivable statement all along; the old architecture just had it attached to the wrong object. Pair with **成而弗居** (A17, Guodian-attested): completes yet structurally-cannot-dwell — the ancient term for exactly this. *[derivation-register candidate; needs writing to theorem standard; DDJ pairing convergence-only]*
