## 生 and 相生 must not be conflated

**生 — parturition with prior state.** Canonical structure: woman(fetus) 生 motherwoman + child. The prior state already contains the structural ground of what is to be born. The output is a relational pair co-constituted by the event: the motherwoman (persisting, with reconfigured relational identity) and the child (structurally new). Nothing is consumed. Recursive self-similarity is preserved — the child carries the same generative potential as the parent, **literally: the recursive instance stands in the ground's own ratio (the generative proportion).** Applies to Pₙ → Pₙ + O₍ₙ₊₁₎ for n > 0.

**相生 — mutual parturition.** 有 and 無 each give birth to the other through the conservation constraint. No prior state. No priority of either side. Not 無生有. Not 有生無. Applies to 有 ⇌ 無 within Rₙ — through the orbit register only.

Three distinguishable forms of generative relation, **each now anchored in the derivation register:**

1. Identity through opposition (P₀ and the recursion it grounds; no parent frame; not parturition) :: **the ground, P₀ : O₁** (math chain r3).
2. Parturition with prior state (n > 0) — 生 :: **Pₙ → Pₙ + O₍ₙ₊₁₎** (math chain r2).
3. Mutual parturition (有 ⇌ 無 within Rₙ) — 相生 :: **realizable only through the orbit register** (Theorem 1).

The two chains are aligned form for form.

**A third conflation guard.** The chain has always guarded 生 against 相生. The ground adds a symmetric guard on the other side: the entailment-生 of 道生一 (identity through opposition, form 1) must not be conflated with parturition-生 (form 2). 道生一 is not a birth event with a persisting parent — 道 is not a motherwoman and P₀ has no prior state. The proportion holds the three forms in one ratio precisely so that the mechanisms need not be identified. *[discipline; new in this version]*
