Derivation register. No correspondences, no characters, no biology. Each step is a structural identity, not a causal sequence.

- **0ₙ, 1ₙ** — operational limit-positions within frame n. 0ₙ ≠ |0|; 1ₙ ≠ |1|. Both are limit-positions, not occupiable states. 1ₙ is the frame’s minimal unit of distinction, identical with the conserved product.
- **Oₙ** — structural origin of frame n.
- **Xₙ, Yₙ** — the frame’s two co-obtaining mode-variables. The two modes are manifest (1) and conjugate (−1), both equally obtained; neither is latent.
- **Gₙ** — the gradient curve: Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ.
- **Bₙ** — the balance axis: Xₙ = Yₙ.
- **Pₙ** — the paradoxical center: Gₙ ∩ Bₙ.
- **j** — split-complex unit, j² = +1, parameterizes the gradient Gₙ.

## Foundational conditional

If reality is infinite, it has no terminal resolution: no minimum scale, no maximum extent.

This is a conditional. The chain derives what follows if it holds. No additional premises are introduced.

## Excluded terminal condition

P₀: absolute indistinguishability. What is not distinguishable does not obtain.

The conditional is symmetric. Infinite divisibility (no floor) and infinite vastness (no ceiling) are two directions of one condition. The two faces are therefore the same incoherence:

- |0| — absence-direction
- |1| — saturation-direction

|0|, |1| ∉ R.

## Local frame values

Within frame n:

- 0ₙ — operational absence
- 1ₙ — operational distinction

Both are limit-positions, not terminal states. They co-require one another: 0ₙ ↔ 1ₙ.

Oₙ is the structural origin of frame n.

## Point as limit

Every apparent position on any gradient is approached asymptotically and does not terminate as a discrete integer location. Pₙ is the clearest instance; the rule applies to all positions on any gradient.

The conditional yields density, not completeness. Infinite divisibility states only that between any two positions lies a third; the rationals already satisfy this while remaining disconnected. Connectedness of the position structure therefore cannot be drawn from the scale structure. It is supplied instead by the traversal structure (§“Forbidden crossing; standoff; forced traversal”): a position is fixed not by completing a value-line but by its angle on the forced orbit, fixed globally by the closed traversal. The center that makes the angle definable is the center that cannot be occupied.

## Gradient modes

The frame expresses two co-obtaining modes Xₙ and Yₙ — the varying expressions of distinction within the frame. 0ₙ and 1ₙ are the frame’s boundary limit-positions; Xₙ and Yₙ are the frame’s internal variation.

## Mode independence

Xₙ and Yₙ must be independent modes for Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ to isolate the inverse relationship cleanly: non-independent modes would not yield a conserved product.

Structural independence does not by itself fix a geometric angle. Absent a metric, two independent directions subtend no determinate angle. The passage from independence to orthogonality is not taken here. Independence is the input; orthogonality is derived downstream as the output of the forced-traversal construction and the metric it generates (§“Forbidden crossing; standoff; forced traversal”, §“Generated metric; orthogonality as theorem”), not assumed.

## Reciprocal gradient

Gₙ: Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ

The minimal algebraic expression of the conserved inverse relationship between two co-obtaining modes within frame n.

1ₙ is not externally imposed. It is the frame’s minimal unit of distinction — identical with the conserved product.

Equivalently:

- Xₙ = 1ₙ / Yₙ
- (Xₙ · Yₙ) − 1ₙ = 0ₙ

Asymptotic pole-directions:

- Xₙ → 0ₙ (along Yₙ-axis)
- Yₙ → 0ₙ (along Xₙ-axis)

Unreachable limit-positions; each approaches a face of P₀.

## Split-complex parameterization of Gₙ

Gₙ is the unit hyperbola of split-complex algebra. Under z = a + jb with j² = +1, and the coordinate substitution Xₙ = a + b, Yₙ = a − b, the modulus condition a² − b² = 1 yields Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ directly. The asymptotes of this hyperbola are the excluded limit-positions 0ₙ along each mode-axis.

The orbit around Pₙ is governed by a distinct algebra, i, derived below. These are two algebras serving two structural functions in one framework: j slides along the gradient; i pivots around the center.

## Balance condition

Bₙ: Xₙ = Yₙ

The exchange-symmetry axis of Gₙ — the locus where swapping Xₙ and Yₙ leaves the position unchanged. At 45° to each mode-axis.

- NOT an asymptote of Gₙ within frame n.
- NOT globally perpendicular to Gₙ.

At Pₙ the slopes are slope(Gₙ) = −1 and slope(Bₙ) = +1 — curve facts, metric-independent. That these constitute perpendicularity is a theorem under the generated metric (§“Generated metric; orthogonality as theorem”), not an assumption here.

Bₙ becomes yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎ at the child’s scale when Pₙ generates a child frame.

## Paradoxical center

Pₙ = Gₙ ∩ Bₙ

Substituting Xₙ = Yₙ into Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ:

Xₙ² = 1ₙ → Xₙ = Yₙ = √1ₙ

**Pₙ = (√1ₙ, √1ₙ)** — a limit-position; the clearest instance of the rule that all positions are limits.

## Local perpendicularity at Pₙ

slope(Gₙ) at Pₙ: dYₙ/dXₙ = −1ₙ / Xₙ² = −1.

slope(Bₙ): +1.

These slopes are properties of the curves, independent of any metric. Whether they constitute perpendicularity is a metric question, deferred: the metric that licenses it is generated by the orbit operator, and orthogonality is established as a theorem once i is fixed (§“Generated metric; orthogonality as theorem”). Local to Pₙ only.

## Required but unoccupiable

Required: gradient transition between modal directions demands a balance position.

Unoccupiable: at Xₙ = Yₙ the weighting that constitutes the gradient becomes structurally indistinct; occupation reduces to P₀ at the gradient’s level.

The frame requires a symmetry center that cannot persist as an inhabited state.

Pₙ ∉ Fₙ. Pₙ ≁ |0|. Pₙ ≁ |1|.

## Forbidden crossing; standoff; forced traversal

Direct traversal through Pₙ does not obtain.

**Standoff.** Distance from Pₙ measures the surviving differential weighting of the two modes. The weighting cannot fall to the indistinct limit without becoming P₀. The minimal sustained distinction is the conserved unit 1ₙ. The closest sustainable approach is therefore at radius √1ₙ, with the modes +1 and −1 seated at that radius. The standoff is not stipulated; it is the conservation relation read as a radial floor — inward of √1ₙ is not costly but forbidden.

**Forced traversal.** Spanning the modes (+1 → −1) under the standoff, with the deviation of Xₙ · Yₙ from 1ₙ as the intrinsic cost, forces the path. A traversal holding radius √1ₙ throughout maintains Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ at every point and carries zero cost; any admissible deviation is outward (inward is forbidden) and raises Xₙ · Yₙ above 1ₙ, carrying positive cost. The zero-cost minimizer is the arc of radius √1ₙ about Pₙ, and under closure the full circle S¹. Its existence, radius, and connectedness are derived, not posited: no external metric defines “shorter”; the selected path is the one that holds 1ₙ everywhere. Inhabited traversal occupies a subset of S¹.

Four cardinal limit-positions of S¹, at π/2 intervals:

- +1 departure
- +i apex
- −1 opposite
- −i reflected

i names both the quarter-turn operation and the limit-position reached by applying that quarter-turn to +1.

## The orbit algebra: i² = −1 by elimination

Denote the orthogonal distinction at a point as i — the minimal structural direction perpendicular to the modal axis. The value of i² must satisfy two requirements established above.

**Closure.** There must be a finite m such that iᵐ returns to the starting element. If no such m exists, composition cascades into an infinite sequence of novel elements; a point whose determinacy requires the completion of a sequence that does not complete does not obtain.

**Spanning.** The closed cycle must contain both modes: 1 (manifest) and −1 (conjugate). A cycle that closes without reaching −1 leaves the conjugate mode disconnected from the orthogonal structure — P₀ applied locally.

Five logically possible cases. Each fails by the same standard that eliminates P₀ — inability to sustain a two-sided, co-present, conserved distinction.

- **i² = 1.** Closes as a 2-cycle {1, i}, but double application leaves the system unchanged, so i is not a genuine second direction — a relabeling of the first. Spanning also fails: −1 is never reached.
- **i² = 0.** Annihilation: the orthogonal direction compounded with itself produces zero distinguishability — P₀ directly. Neither closure nor spanning obtains.
- **i² = i.** Idempotent: i³ = i, and the system reaches i and stays there. Closure fails (no return to 1); spanning fails (−1 never reached).
- **i² = −i.** Then i³ = i: a 2-cycle {i, −i} that never recovers 1. Closure fails in the strongest sense; spanning fails (−1 never reached).
- **i² = −1.** Generates 1 → i → −1 → −i → 1 (i³ = −i, i⁴ = 1). It closes. It contains both 1 and −1. It connects them through orthogonal intermediates. It preserves distinction at every phase. It does so with the minimum structure: four phases is the smallest enumeration that closes, spans both modes, and maintains orthogonal independence between the modal axis (1 ↔ −1) and the orthogonal axis (i ↔ −i).

Only i² = −1 satisfies both requirements.

The method is elimination, not selection. As with P₀, the other four values are not candidates ranked against a criterion; each is shown structurally incoherent. What remains is the only coherent possibility, not the winner of a competition.

## Algebraic enumeration and structural traversal

Two descriptions of the same object, kept separate to avoid an apparent tension with closure-minima elsewhere.

**Algebraic enumeration.** Composing i with itself produces four elements {1, i, −1, −i}; under i⁴ = 1 this is the algebraic closure. Four elements. This is what the algebra counts.

**Structural traversal.** The operative cycle has three positions: departure (1), opposite (−1), return-as-completed (1). The intermediates i and −i are path, not waypoints.

Four algebraic elements, three structural positions. No contradiction.

e^(i·0) and e^(i·2π) are both numerically 1 but structurally distinct: one has not circulated; the other has completed an orbit. Same value, different structural depth.

## Generated metric; orthogonality as theorem

The orbit operator i (i² = −1, fixed above by closure and spanning) generates a metric. A magnitude is conserved along the orbit: a varying magnitude would, at some phase, drive the radius below the standoff toward P₀. A conserved magnitude across the closed one-parameter orbit is a quadratic form invariant under the flow θ ↦ e^(iθ). The forms invariant under the full rotation family are exactly the positive multiples of

Q_i(a, b) = a² + b².

Indefinite or degenerate forms are preserved by the hyperbolic flow of j, not the elliptic flow of i, and fail invariance here. Q_i is therefore positive-definite, unique up to the scale set by the radius √1ₙ.

Under Q_i the tangent vectors at Pₙ are t_G = (1, −1) and t_B = (1, +1):

⟨t_G, t_B⟩ = (1)(1) + (−1)(1) = 0.

Gₙ ⊥ Bₙ at Pₙ. **[derived]**

The slope-product criterion that earlier presupposed a Euclidean metric is here a consequence of one; the metric is a consequence of the operator forced on the orbit; the operator is fixed on the circle that minimal traversal forced. The order does not reverse.

## Recursion: generation of new coordinate systems

The joint required-and-forbidden condition cannot resolve within frame n. Neither condition eliminates the other. The structural fact generates a new coordinate system at the parent’s forbidden center.

- Gₙ → xAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎
- Bₙ → yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎
- Pₙ → O₍ₙ₊₁₎

The local ⊥ of Gₙ and Bₙ at Pₙ (established as a theorem under the generated metric) becomes the global ⊥ of the child’s mode-axes.

The child frame is its own ℝ³, not a subregion of the parent’s ℝ³. It extends in its own structural direction from the parent’s Pₙ, with its own Gₙ₊₁, Bₙ₊₁, Pₙ₊₁, and its own positions that can generate further child frames.

Bₙ is structurally saturated as the exchange-symmetry axis within frame n; it cannot serve as a mode-axis within the same frame; it becomes a mode-axis of a new coordinate system generated at Pₙ.

## Branching structure of recursion

The recursion is branching, not linear. The primary derivation states generation of a child frame at the parent’s Pₙ. The generalization — that any position on the parent’s gradient satisfying the required-and-forbidden condition at its own scale can generate a child frame — is a development under test, not yet derived with the rigor of the Pₙ case.

Each child frame is its own ℝ³, extending in its own structural direction. Two child frames at different positions on the same parent’s gradient extend in different directions; their coordinate systems are distinct.

The parent frame is itself a child of some grandparent frame. The parent’s Oₙ is, from the grandparent, the position on the grandparent’s gradient that generated the parent’s coordinate system. The recursion is a branching structure, not a one-dimensional cascade.

## Irreversibility, linear time, and structural recession

**Irreversibility.** Pₙ → O₍ₙ₊₁₎ is one-way. Reversal — a child frame ceasing to be its own origin and resolving back into the parent’s Pₙ — requires the child’s origin to un-obtain. An origin that un-obtains is absolute indistinguishability at that locus: P₀. P₀ cannot obtain. Generation has no inverse.

**Linear ordering.** The one-way generation relation imposes a strict order on the frames along any generative line: each frame is generated from one parent-position and cannot precede it. This irreversible strict ordering is the structural content of linear succession.

**Structural recession.** Each O₍ₙ₊₁₎ is constitutively unreachable from within frame n: it is defined by frame n’s Pₙ, a limit-position asymptotic from within n before it becomes an origin at the child scale. Every subsequent generation adds another unreachable origin between a given frame and any earlier reference. From within any frame, earlier origins are progressively further, and the distance compounds per generation.

*The identification of this ordering with physical time, and of this recession with observed cosmic acceleration, is a convergence-register correspondence and is stated in the physics chain, not asserted here.*

## No terminal frame

No frame is the terminal frame in any direction. No forbidden center can be a final dead point (Pₙ ≁ |0|, Pₙ ≁ |1|). The recursion has no terminus because the conditional has no terminal resolution.

Apparent termination at any scale resolves into further structure: every apparent position contains its own gradient; every apparent frame is a child of some grandparent frame.

1₍ₙ₊₁₎ is determined by the curvature of Gₙ at Pₙ. (Quantitative relationship: open formal work.)

## Cross-frame asymptotic relationship

- G₍ₙ₊₁₎ asymptotic to Gₙ (child’s xAxis)
- G₍ₙ₊₁₎ asymptotic to Bₙ (child’s yAxis)

The child’s gradient approaches the parent’s structural features as its unreachable limit-positions, because the parent’s structural features are the child’s mode-axes.

## Locally flat, globally curved

Gₙ is curved at the frame’s own scale with respect to its asymptotic pole-directions (not with respect to Bₙ). At the child’s scale, the parent’s gradient appears locally flat from within the child’s coordinate system. Curvature and flatness are frame-relative.

## Substrate level

No preferred orientation around any center. The forced orbit S¹, taken over all orientations at fixed radius, gives S². Unbounded extension → ℝ³. Each frame locally recovers ℝ³.

Three dimensions necessary. Uniqueness remains open formal work: S² also embeds in ℝ⁴ and higher; whether the framework structurally excludes higher-dimensional embeddings is not settled.

The substrate is the self-sufficient non-framed structural condition under which frames obtain — not a frame above the recursion, but the structural condition that no frame is terminal.
