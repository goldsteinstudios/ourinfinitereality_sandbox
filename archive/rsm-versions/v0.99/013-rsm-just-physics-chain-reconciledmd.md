Convergence register. Physics vocabulary names structural facts the derivation has independently established. The convergence is the evidence; physics is one of three registers in which the same structural ontology is compressed.
Each correspondence carries an explicit status, per CAVP (Constant Accuracy, Variable Precision):
[derived] — a structural fact established in the math chain, here named in physics terms.
[candidate] — a convergence-register correspondence not yet earned as a :: claim.

Integer-precision structural claims and variable-precision empirical correspondences are not interchangeable. A correspondence stated at one level is not a claim at the other.

Foundational conditional
If physical reality has no terminal resolution in either direction, then neither a minimum length nor a maximum extent obtains.
Excluded terminal condition
Two faces of P₀:
A terminal minimum scale (e.g. a Planck floor read as an absolute smallest length) — structurally excluded.
A terminal maximum extent (a largest possible scale) — structurally excluded.
Within any physical regime, operational absence and operational distinction are limit-states approached asymptotically, not terminal conditions. The vacuum is not absolute nothing; apparent scale-limits are the boundaries of currently inhabited frames, not structural termini. [derived]
Inverse conservation: the Gₙ form in physics
The structural form is Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ: two co-varying quantities whose product is the regime’s conserved unit, neither reducible to the other. Two physical relations instantiate it cleanly, as equalities:
Wavelength × frequency = c (electromagnetic radiation in vacuum). λ and f co-vary inversely; the conserved product is c. [good fit]
Pressure × volume = constant (Boyle’s law, at fixed temperature). P and V co-vary inversely; the conserved product is the regime’s characteristic unit. [good fit at fixed T]
The full ideal gas law PV = nRT is three-variable and is not an instance of the inverse form except in the isothermal case. Stating it as the general conserved product is imprecise; the honest mapping is Boyle’s law. [correction to prior draft]
In each case the conserved product is not externally imposed: it is what the regime conserves.
Bound relations and forbidden centers
The uncertainty relations are not equality conservations. They are floors:
Δx · Δp ≥ ℏ/2
ΔE · Δt ≥ ℏ/2
The product is bounded below and never reaches zero. The zero-product position — simultaneously precise conjugate quantities — is the forbidden center: required by the structure of the conjugate pair, unoccupiable in fact. This is the physical reading of Pₙ ∉ Fₙ, not of the conservation curve. The two structures (equality conservation, bounded floor) must be kept distinct. [derived: forbidden center; the specific operators are candidate]

Mass and energy
E = mc². Within a child frame (R₂), mass and energy read as the two modes and c² as the frame’s conserved unit 1₂. This is an observed correspondence, not a derived inverse-form conservation: as written, E = mc² is an equivalence with c a constant, not an inverse co-variation of two modes. Its placement as a Gₙ instance is convergence-register, not core. [candidate; observed, not derived]
Lorentz form
The split-complex parameterization of Gₙ gives the Lorentz form directly. Under Xₙ = a + b, Yₙ = a − b, the conservation Xₙ · Yₙ = 1ₙ becomes a² − b² = 1 — the 1+1 invariant interval X² − T² = 1. The formal correspondence xy = 1 ↔ X² − T² = 1 is exact. [derived; formal correspondence exact]
The extension to 3+1 dimensions, X² + Y² + Z² − T² = 1ₙ, combines the spatial S² recovery with the Lorentz signature. Whether this carries physical significance beyond the structural analogy is open. [formal; physical significance open]
Time enters through the signature, not as a fourth spatial axis. The sign on T² preserves the distinction: space is orientation (where you are); time is traversal (that you are moving).
Time and recession
The generation relation Pₙ → O₍ₙ₊₁₎ is irreversible (its reversal would require an origin to un-obtain, which is P₀). This irreversibility is the structural content of the arrow of time, and the strict ordering it imposes is linear succession. [derived]
Each generated origin is constitutively unreachable from within the frame that produced it, and every further generation interposes another unreachable origin between a frame and any earlier reference; the separation compounds per generation. The candidate physical reading: what is observed as accelerating cosmological recession is the structural recession of origins, not the spatial expansion of a substrate — recession without an expanding medium, distinct from a dark-energy term. This is the strongest cosmological correspondence the framework offers and it is a candidate, not a derivation; its distinguishing empirical signature against ΛCDM is open work. [candidate]
Locally flat, globally curved
General relativity’s local-Minkowski / global-curved structure is one instance of the chain’s locally-flat / globally-curved dynamic at the spacetime scale. Spacetime is locally Minkowski and globally Lorentzian (pseudo-Riemannian); the positive-definite “Riemannian” label is imprecise for the spacetime case. [candidate; strong]
Three-dimensional substrate at every scale
Physical space is ℝ³ at every regime’s scale; each regime locally recovers ℝ³. The recursion across regimes does not produce higher-dimensional structures (ℝ⁴, ℝ⁵). It produces new coordinate systems, each its own ℝ³, generated at the forbidden centers of parent regimes. Three dimensions necessary; uniqueness open. [derived, with uniqueness open]
Recursion across physical regimes
Physical regimes nest as a branching structure of new coordinate systems generated at parent regimes’ forbidden centers — not a linear cascade from large to small. Cosmological, galactic, solar-system, planetary, chemical, molecular, atomic, subatomic, quark: each is its own coordinate system at its own scale, generated at the forbidden center of some parent regime, with multiple child regimes per parent.
No physical regime is terminal in any direction. A Planck scale is not the smallest possible scale; a cosmological scale is not the largest. Both are the apparent limits of currently inhabited frames. [derived structure; the branching-at-any-position generalization is under test]
Toroidal structures under privileged gradients
Where a single gradient operates and direct crossing is forbidden, persistent structure organizes around an axis or centerline, with the forbidden center along the privileged axis:

Energy as misalignment cost

Zero-point energy is the structural correlate of a system’s inability to rest at the forbidden center: the non-zero ground state is the cost of not being at the center. [candidate]
