## I. GROUND RULINGS (definitions; propagate everywhere)

**G1. Infinity is definitional and two-directional.** Infinitely vast AND infinitely divisible — no maximum extent, no minimum scale. "No terminal resolution" carries this gloss at first use. The antecedent is a stipulated definition; an auditor can decline it but not attack it as an inference.

**G2. The chain is entirely ontological.** "What is not distinguishable does not obtain" is an existence claim. Base form: co-constitution — Nothing cannot obtain without a thing to be no-of; 0 is incoherent without a coinherent 1.

**G3. P₀ is not nothing.** It is the co-constitution incoherence itself — a condition that fails to cohere, not a state that fails to obtain. Explicit guard needed in v8 against the "absence" misread.

**G4. The ground introduces no new premise.** "Because P₀, O₁" = conditional + G2's bridge (contrapositive) + existential import. First-frame cascade, each step biconditional: P₀ → O₁ → (X₁,Y₁) → (G₁,B₁) → P₁.

**G5. Co-constitution is biconditional throughout.** Oₙ ⟺ Gₙ; neither prior. Entailment identities read in derivation order, not productions.

**G6. The conditional is never promoted.** All isotropy/universality claims are consequences of the infinity conditional, not unconditional claims about reality. "Given the conditional, reality-writ-large has no preferred frame/scale/orientation." Whether reality satisfies the conditional is never claimed proven.

- Amplitude language (linear): coordinates, radii; √1ₙ; what i acts on.

## III. THE TWO CENTERS (re-typed)

**C1. Both centers are paradoxical — one genus, two species.** Genus: a paradoxical center is the crossing-point of a co-requiring pair (Oₙ of the axis-pair; Pₙ of the (G,B) pair, in the same way).

**C2. The species distinction is met-vs-unmet, not occupied-vs-unoccupiable:**
- Oₙ = unmet crossing: arms approach asymptotically, never meet; both modes at operational absence together; four-way distinction fails; equality-as-collapse; local face of P₀; inherited by the child as its origin.
- Pₙ = realized crossing: G and B actually intersect; both modes balanced at equal nonzero magnitude (√1ₙ, both seats); distinction intact; equality-as-balance; generative because there is structure to hand down.

**C3. Met-ness is frame-relative.** Oₙ = P₍ₙ₋₁₎ is identity (one locus), not parenthood (two objects); "parent/child" indexes the frame-readings; "generated" = the frame-reading relation, no event. The same locus is realized in the parent's expression and unmet in the child's. Met-from-without, unmet-from-within; curved-pair as parent's P, straight-pair as child's O.

**C4. Center classification by incident pair:** O-type = straight×straight (locally and globally aligned); P-type = straight×curved (locally orthogonal, globally curved-relative).

**C5. Register-unit alignment characterization [candidate]:** Pₙ = the locus where all three 1s coincide at unity (coordinate 1, unit 1ₙ, phase-anchor); Oₙ = where all three coincide at absence/undefined. The centers are the two points where amplitude, measure, and orbit registers align — one at 1, one at 0.

**C6. 0ₙ is a limit-approach, not a point.** Operational absence approached along Gₙ toward Oₙ; type-distinct from Oₙ (process vs. unreachable limit). Doc's "limit-position" mis-describes it.

**C7. The parent-shift holds for n ≥ 2 only.** O₁'s parent-side is P₀ — an incoherence, not a seat. The ground exception is definitional (identity-through-opposition vs. parturition). O₍ₙ₊₁₎ = the child frame's origin, not the child frame.

## IV. THE ENGINE (the chain's crux)

**E1. Pₙ's non-occupiability is the single source of both rotation and recursion.** Pₙ is met but cannot constitute a standing axis-pair (locally orthogonal, globally curved-relative). Traversal cannot pass through → must go around → rotation. Frame-demand cannot be met there → discharges into new structure → recursion. One impossibility, two discharges. The doc's "generates because occupied" inverts the mechanism.

**E2. The seat-phase structure (verified; replaces "aperture"):** seatₖ = √1ₙ·iᵏ, k = 0..3; Q_i = 1ₙ constant; Q_j = (−1)ᵏ1ₙ alternating (double cover in the enumeration). Axis-instantiation exactly at the cardinal phases. The i-cycle IS the enumeration of generation sites.

**E3. The conjugate pair +1/−1 is antipodal, not collinear — and this re-derives i [candidate; strong].** The straight path between conjugates passes through Oₙ (excluded, Thm 1); the realizable path is the half-turn, requiring the perpendicular direction = i. Independent route to i from the co-constitution of unity.

**E4. Lemma A (the demand side):** an infinite gradient obtains ⟺ a globally straight, independent axis-pair holds at its center. Pre-metric core: straightness (the product structure requires global linear coordinates); orthogonality is the derived-metric face. Staging required to avoid metric-before-metric circularity.

**E5. The discharge argument, A-B-C-D structure:**
- A: demand (Lemma A) — closed.
- B: deficit (incident pair at Pₙ globally curved-relative; verified) — closed.
- C: transfer (Pₙ carries the center-role univocally — "in the same way" as theorem, not analogy) — **THE remaining gap.**
- D: discharge (new straight pair entailed; child gradient by A's biconditional) — closed given C.
Open item #1 re-scopes to C: univocity of the center-role.

**E6. Recursion in one line:** P-type → O-type conversion by axis-generation. Canonical componentwise form: Gₙ → Gₙ + xAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎, Bₙ → Bₙ + yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎ ("must" = structural entailment, no time-word).

**E7. The recursion formula is a cross-register sentence (Will's "limit of language").** Arrow = structural entailment across frame-readings; comma = coexistence-with-severance. v8 states the recursion bilingually (parent-sentence + child-sentence; no third sentence contains both), keeping the arrow as glossed shorthand.

**E8. Deflation guard:** "same thing, different frames" = identity at the locus, novelty of the frame. The point is one; the child's co-constitution cluster (axes, G, B, P, unit 1₍ₙ₊₁₎) genuinely obtains and is not re-description — else the cross-frame unit relation (open item 6) would be vacuous. Expressibility-in-parent-coordinates (z² net) ≠ identity-of-frame.
