# RSM — Just-Math Chain (v7.7, r1)

Derivation register. No correspondences, no characters, no physics. This chain is the mathematical realization of the RSM structural chain (v7.7): each section realizes a skeleton entailment in coordinates and proofs. Machine-drafted (Claude, Fable 5); computational claims re-verified symbolically during drafting; nothing enters canon without independent checking.

**Register declarations (house rule — every equation declares its language):**
- **Measure language** (quadratic): units of distinction; 1ₙ is native here — it *is* the conserved product; what i preserves.
- **Amplitude language** (linear): coordinates, radii, distances; √1ₙ; what i acts on. **√ is the translator** between measure and amplitude.
- **Orbit language** (phase): 1 as one end of the conjugate pair {+1, −1}; positions as angles.
- **Skeleton facts are implicit-register** (no motion, duration, occupancy); parametric objects (paths, e^{jφ}, e^{iθ}) are explicit-register. The two share logic, not expression; theorems whose proofs use paths but whose conclusions are implicit are flagged.

Architecture: **Oₙ = Xₙ ∩ Yₙ** (the axes' crossing; the orbit's center; the unmet crossing); **Pₙ = Bₙ ∩ Gₙ** (the realized crossing; the paradoxical-generative center); **recursion Gₙ → Gₙ + xAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎, Bₙ → Bₙ + yAxis₍ₙ₊₁₎** (componentwise parturition); **ground: because P₀ is incoherent, O₁**; invariant proportion **Pₙ : O₍ₙ₊₁₎ :: P₀ : O₁**.

- **Gₙ** — the gradient: Xₙ·Yₙ = 1ₙ ⟺ Q_j := a² − b² = 1ₙ. **Bₙ** — the balance axis: X = Y (b = 0). **Mₙ** — the mediator axis: a = 0.
- **Oₙ** — the structural origin: the mode axes' crossing = the asymptotes' crossing = (0ₙ, 0ₙ) as limit. The unmet crossing; the frame's local face of P₀.

- **j** — split unit, j² = +1. **i** — complex unit, i² = −1 (derived, §6).

## Foundational conditional

If reality is infinite — **infinitely vast and infinitely divisible**: no maximum extent, no minimum scale — the chain derives what follows. The two-directional reading is the *definition* of "infinite" here, not a claimed consequence. Premises beyond the conditional are named at first use and listed at the end.

## Excluded terminal condition; the ground

P₀: absolute indistinguishability. What is not distinguishable does not obtain (the Bridge — ontological, not epistemic). |0| and |1| are one incoherence; the faces pair with the conditional's two directions (divisibility excludes |0|; vastness excludes |1|). **P₀ is not nothing** (guard carried from the structural chain).

**Because P₀ is incoherent, O₁** — existential import + the Bridge (contrapositive) + P₀'s incoherence; no new premise. Identity through opposition; not parturition. The generative proportion: **Pₙ : O₍ₙ₊₁₎ :: P₍ₙ₋₁₎ : Oₙ :: P₀ : O₁** — ratio invariant, mechanism distinguished; each (Pₙ, O₍ₙ₊₁₎) pair for n ≥ 1 is one locus under two frame-readings (met-from-without, unmet-from-within); the first pair alone has no locus on its P-side. *[derived]*

## The frame; the cascade; the unit

The first-frame cascade, each step biconditional (ontological simultaneity, epistemic order): P₀ → O₁ → (X₁,Y₁) → (G₁,B₁) → P₁. Because Oₙ, Gₙ; because Gₙ, Oₙ.

**A frame is the cascade of one constitutive pair** ("the," not "any"): a second independent primitive gradient is either derived or a different frame. Hence 1ₙ — *the* pair's conserved product — is automatically the frame's minimal distinction: anything finer is itself a distinction, and every distinction constitutes a frame (Lemma L1, under audit), i.e., is frame n+1. **Postulate F is dissolved into the definition of frame, contingent on L1.** *[derived given L1]*

## Point as limit; simultaneity and order

Every gradient position is approached asymptotically, not terminated as a discrete location. The conditional yields **density, not completeness**; connectedness comes from **Definition T (traversal continuity)**: a traversal is a continuous path of obtaining positions [explicit register] — definition-level commitment, motivated by the no-jump principle. A position is fixed by its angle on the forced orbit about Oₙ: the center that makes the angle definable is the center never met. *[derived, given T]*

## THE ONE LAW AND ITS TWO SLICES (organizing principle, new in 7.7)

Over ℂ there is **one** equation — the frame's unit modulus, **Q(z) = 1ₙ** — with two real slices:

- b real → **Q_i = a² + b² = 1ₙ**: the circle — the orbit. [verified]
- b ↦ ib → **Q_j = a² − b² = 1ₙ**: the hyperbola — the gradient. [verified]

Parametric face: cosh(iθ) = cos θ — the analytic continuation u → iθ. **The gradient is the orbit:** not two structures sharing a law — one structure; what differs is the slice. The Euclidean drawing is a rendering register that can show only one real slice at a time; the hyperbola/circle "distinction" is a slicing artifact, whole in the algebra, split on paper. *[verified]*

**Where the rendering heals:** the two real slices intersect at exactly {b = 0, a = ±√1ₙ} — **the primary seats** — tangentially (common tangent; equal curvature magnitude, opposite bending: mirror-osculation is this coincidence's curvature signature). The seats are the two points where gradient and orbit are the same even in the broken rendering. *[verified]*

With u = a², v = b²: **Q_i = u + v, Q_j = u − v** — the two algebras' invariants are the sum and difference of one pair. On the orbit, **Q_j = 1ₙ·cos 2θ** (double cover): the invariant returns at π (landing on ν — return-of-structure) while the position returns at 2π (return-of-place) — **two distinct returns** the word "rotation" collapses. *[derived; verified]*

## The seat-phase structure (replaces the former "aperture" section; the term is retired from this register)
