## III. STRUCTURAL TRANSLATION

### **Traditional Academic Translation:**
> "Thirty spokes share one hub; in its emptiness lies the wheel's usefulness. Shape clay into a vessel; in its emptiness lies the vessel's usefulness. Cut doors and windows to make a room; in their emptiness lies the room's usefulness. Therefore, having provides benefit, but emptiness provides function."

### **RSM Structural Translation:**
> "Thirty **Y₁ vectors** converge at one **Pₙ hub**; at the **X₁ dimensional void**, the wheel achieves **Z₁ circulation**.  
> Structure clay into **Rₙ vessel**; at the **X₁ dimensional void**, the vessel achieves function.  
> Create interface boundaries for **spatial system**; at their **X₁ dimensional voids**, the room achieves function.  
> Therefore: **Y₁ presence** serves structural benefit, **X₁ void** serves operational function."

### **Plain English (Structural):**
> "Thirty spokes meet at one hub—at the dimensional void where there's no material, the wheel finds its function. When you shape clay into a vessel, at the dimensional void inside, the vessel finds its function. When you cut doors and windows to make a room, at their dimensional voids, the room finds its function. Therefore, the material presence provides structural benefit, but the dimensional void provides actual functionality."
