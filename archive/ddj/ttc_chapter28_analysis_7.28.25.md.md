## III. STRUCTURAL TRANSLATION

### **Traditional Academic Translation:**
> "Know the masculine, guard the feminine, be the world's ravine. Being the world's ravine, constant virtue does not depart, return to the state of the infant. Know the white, guard the black, be the world's pattern. Being the world's pattern, constant virtue does not err, return to the limitless. Know the honor, guard the disgrace, be the world's valley. Being the world's valley, constant virtue is sufficient, return to the uncarved block. When the uncarved block scatters, it becomes vessels. The sage uses this and becomes a leader. Therefore the great making does not cut."

### **RSM Structural Translation:**
> "Recognize **Y₁⁺ outward**, maintain **Y₁⁻ inward position**, become **universal Pₙ center**.  
> Being universal **Pₙ center**, **∂P_center/∂t = 0** sustains, **→ pre-differentiated potential**.  
> Recognize **manifest aspects**, maintain **hidden capacity**, become **universal template**.  
> Being universal **template**, **∂P_center/∂t = 0** without deviation, **→ P₀ unlimited potential**.  
> Recognize **expanded manifestation**, maintain **contracted position**, become **universal valley**.  
> Being universal **valley**, **∂P_center/∂t = 0** sufficient, **→ uncarved block potential**.  
> **Pure potential → specialized Rₙ forms**, recursion-aware utilizes this **→ organizational centers**.  
> Therefore: **Wu wei formation** preserves wholeness without fragmentation."

### **Plain English (Structural):**
> "Recognize the outward expansive force but maintain your position at the inward center—this makes you a universal drainage point where everything naturally flows. By being this universal center, you maintain paradox preservation and return to undifferentiated potential like an infant before specialization. Recognize what's visible and manifest but maintain connection to what's hidden and potential—this makes you a universal pattern that others can follow. By being this universal pattern, you maintain perfect paradox preservation and return to unlimited possibility space. Recognize achievement and glory but maintain humble positioning—this makes you a universal valley where everything can settle. By being this universal valley, paradox preservation becomes sufficient, and you return to the uncarved block state of pure potential. When this pure potential manifests into specific forms, the wise person uses this process to become an organizational center. Therefore, great formation happens without cutting things apart—wholeness is preserved through the process."
