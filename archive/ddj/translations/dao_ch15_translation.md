# Chapter 15 — Ancient Master Interface Protocols

## Original Chinese + Pinyin

古之善為士者，微妙玄通，深不可識。
*gǔ zhī shàn wéi shì zhě, wēi miào xuán tōng, shēn bù kě shí*

夫唯不可識，故強為之容：
*fū wéi bù kě shí, gù qiáng wéi zhī róng*

豫兮若冬涉川；
*yù xī ruò dōng shè chuān*

猶兮若畏四鄰；
*yóu xī ruò wèi sì lín*

儼兮其若容；
*yǎn xī qí ruò róng*

渙兮若冰之將釋；
*huàn xī ruò bīng zhī jiāng shì*

敦兮其若樸；
*dūn xī qí ruò pǔ*

曠兮其若谷；
*kuàng xī qí ruò gǔ*

混兮其若濁。
*hùn xī qí ruò zhuó*

孰能濁以靜之徐清？
*shú néng zhuó yǐ jìng zhī xú qīng?*

孰能安以動之徐生？
*shú néng ān yǐ dòng zhī xú shēng?*

保此道者，不欲盈。
*bǎo cǐ dào zhě, bù yù yíng*

夫唯不盈，故能蔽而新成。
*fū wéi bù yíng, gù néng bì ér xīn chéng*

## 🎭 Layer 1: Traditional Correspondence

The excellent scholars of old were subtle, wondrous, mysterious, and penetrating; they were so deep they could not be known.
Because they could not be known, I am forced to describe their appearance:
Cautious, as if crossing a river in winter;
Hesitant, as if afraid of their neighbors;
Grave, as if they were guests;
Flowing, like ice about to melt;
Simple, like uncarved wood;
Open, like a valley;
Mixed, like muddy water.
Who can make muddy water clear by stilling it?
Who can bring the tranquil to life by moving it?
Those who preserve this Way do not want to be full.
Because they are not full, they can be worn out and yet newly made.
