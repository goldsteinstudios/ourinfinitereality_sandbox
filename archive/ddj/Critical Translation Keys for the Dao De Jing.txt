# Critical Translation Keys for the Dao Jing 

## Axis-Defining Characters

**可 (kě)** = explicit, expressible, articulable, parametric
- What can be pointed to, measured, named
- Frame-dependent manifestations

**常 (cháng)** = implicit, underlying, structural
- NOT "constant/eternal" in the temporal sense
- What underlies articulation but cannot itself be directly articulated
- Frame-independent structure

**無 (wú)** = nothing, void, absence, potential
- Not "nonexistence" but structural emptiness
- The conditions, the gaps, the space

**有 (yǒu)** = something, form, presence, manifestation
- Actualized content
- What's actually there

## Observation-Defining Characters

**欲 (yù)** = orientation, focus, directed attention
- NOT "desire" in the moral sense
- Where you point your observational stance
- The vector of attention

**妙 (miào)** = subtle patterns, relationships, connections
- What becomes visible when observing 常無 (implicit nothing)
- The relational structure

**徼 (jiǎo)** = boundaries, edges, limits, distinctions
- What becomes visible when observing 常有 (implicit something)
- The categorical structure

## Structure-Defining Characters

**異名 (yì míng)** = named-difference
- The emergent property of having two axes
- Distinction itself
- The ability to parse reality into parts

**玄 (xuán)** = the paradox, the preserved tension
- The origin point where axes cross
- Where opposites meet without resolving
- Zero on both axes simultaneously

**道 (dào)** = the pattern, the structure, the way
- Often = 常無名 (the implicit named-nothing)
- The origin of the coordinate system
