## A. Chain-level (closes with a derivation)

### A1. The native-magnitudes step (chart anchoring)
- **Question:** the uniqueness-up-to-reparametrization argument for the product law runs through a
  chart whose native coordinates are "fixed by the impossibility itself" — the chart is anchored by
  the very thing it excludes. Does the law hold in the modes' own magnitudes without that anchoring?
- **Lives:** math chain, "The reciprocity requirement" ¶4 ("The native-magnitudes step is
  unsettled") + math open item 1; structural open item 1.
- **Depends on it:** the product law's standing as *derived* rather than chart-adopted; everything
  §4 (frame/unit/floor) builds on the law.
- **Closes with:** a derivation of the product form in native magnitudes, or an argument that the
  anchoring is legitimate.

### A2. Anchoring-by-constitution
- **Question:** why every frame beneath n's unit and in relation to n is parent-seeded.
- **Lives:** structural open item 1; math chain "The engine (realized)" — Anchoring ¶ (the typing
  argument: "a free-floating sub-unit frame is a contradiction in the typing").
- **Depends on it:** rootedness claims; the tree structure of the nesting.
- **Closes with:** a derivation — **note the bookkeeping conflict is separately open** (see B9: the
  math body's typing argument and the open item both claim it; the change log says one or the
  other, and leaves the choice to Will).

### A3. The ground's self-sufficiency candidate
- **Question:** whether the ground needs, and has, self-sufficiency (nothing beneath an
  impossibility to stand on).
- **Lives:** structural open item 1 (third clause); DDJ chain renders it as 道法自然.
- **Closes with:** a derivation or a demotion to permanent candidate.

### A4. Cross-frame unit relation 1ₙ ↔ 1₍ₙ₊₁₎
- **Question:** whether any *quantitative* relation accompanies the constitutive share of
  structural §7. An explicit-register question by the chains' own typing.
- **Lives:** structural open item 2; math open item 2.
- **Depends on it:** physics §10 (the Bekenstein sort — "if nothing passes, the framework makes no
  contact with holography in either direction"); physics §11 (quasiperiodicity — "conditional on
  that relation existing at all"); quantitative complementarity's reach.
- **Closes with:** derivation of a relation, or a proof that none obtains (either closes it).

### A5. Dimension — "exactly three", and uniqueness of embedding
- **Question:** "at least three" has a route (no orientation privileged → the arcs sweep a sphere →
  three needed, no fourth *required*). "Exactly three" is open: that nothing requires a fourth is
  not yet that nothing *can use* one. Two rival derivations, unadjudicated.
- **Lives:** structural open item 3; math open item 3; change log §B items 3–4 (the route is partly
  machine inference).
- **Depends on it:** the spin-½ orientation-space account (physics §6, second candidate — "register-
  clean once dimension lands at three"); the signature strain (physics §3: slice gives (1,1),
  physics has (1,3), "which the dimension question inherits").
- **Closes with:** adjudication of the two rival derivations, plus an argument closing the
  fourth-dimension use (embedding uniqueness).

### A6. Revolution as *forced* — the modal gap
- **Question:** Will ruled that rotation gives the recursion; the *necessity* argument (the relation
  between the disconnected regions **must** be carried by revolution, not merely can be) is the
  machine's. Is "must be carried" derivable, or does the chain hold only "can"?
- **Lives:** math chain Theorems (Revolution); structural §6 (Rotation); change log §B item 3.
- **Depends on it:** the dimension route (A5 cites revolution-needs-a-direction); the ring of
  orientations as *consequence* (B item 5).
- **Closes with:** a derivation of the necessity step, or demotion of "forced" to "available."

### A7. Definition T (traversal) — restatement in the mode-expressible domain
- **Question:** traversal as previously stated runs in the signed chart; the disconnection theorem
  now lives in the mode-expressible domain, where Definition T is not yet stated. Not attempted in
  v9.3 (change log §C).
- **Lives:** math open item 5.
- **Depends on it:** T2 (crossing), the two returns, the minimal traversal, the complementarity
  floor — all currently rendering results partly *because* T is chart-bound; any future traversal
  claim in native magnitudes.
- **Closes with:** the restated definition.

### A8. Branching beyond the balance / beyond Pₙ
- **Question:** can any relation other than the constitutive pair be inexact in the constituting
  way? Candidate, unruled: it cannot — every conjugate equality reduces to x = 1ₙ/x, and other
  points of B belong to other frames. The step needing adversarial read: whether *derivative
  pairings* qualify.
- **Lives:** structural open item 4; math open item 7; change log §C ("candidate stated, unruled").
- **Closes with:** derivation of the reduction + a ruling on derivative pairings (mixed closure).

### A9. Quadraticity of the measure
- **Question:** whether the measure's quadratic form is forced rather than adopted. A non-quadratic
  candidate satisfying the coarse constraints exists and fails only the parallelogram condition.
- **Lives:** math chain, "The measure."
- **Depends on it:** everything metric — "orthogonal"/perpendicularity (currently rendering; the
  skeleton says *independent*), the circle as complete object, π/sphere renderings, the
  complementarity floor's geometry. Also the editorial loom document's perpendicularity claim
  (see B6) — it becomes stateable as structural only if this closes.
- **Closes with:** a derivation forcing quadraticity, or adoption-by-register stated permanently.

### A10. Root non-uniqueness (withdrawn, with standing consequences)
- **Status:** not an open item — a **withdrawal**. v9.2 dissolved "priority and uniqueness of the
  first distinction"; v9.3 §2 carries it verbatim: "at least one is what the impossibility gives.
  Nothing here counts the roots, and nothing here excludes more than one."
- **Lives:** structural §2.
- **Standing consequences:** "each obtained tree is rooted" survives (per-tree, not globally);
  prose anywhere must not say "*the* first distinction"; the tree-question (A12) and cross-frame
  expression (A11) inherit the possibility of multiple roots. Older editorial documents (e.g. One
  Distinction §XII) still list this as a *debt* — it is not; it is withdrawn.
- **Closes with:** nothing — reopens only if a counting derivation appears.

### A11. Cross-frame expression

- **Lives:** math open item 4.
- **Closes with:** a derivation (or a proof of impossibility, which would itself be a result).

### A12. The tree-question
- **Question:** sibling structure and commensurability — shared ancestry as the only common register.
- **Lives:** math open item 6.
- **Closes with:** a derivation.

### A13. Quantitative complementarity
- **Question:** the winding-measure premise; the non-star-shaped extension. A question about the
  rendering, by the chain's own note.
- **Lives:** math open item 8; complementarity-floor theorem note.
- **Closes with:** derivation of the winding measure (or permanent rendering status).

### A14. Physics open exposures (each closes with an account or a break)
- **The s-orbital strain** — QM places every s-state's density maximum *at the nucleus*; either the
  origin's exclusion is not positional in the atomic rendering, or the correspondence class is
  weaker than it looks. Lives: physics §7. Closes with: an account of exclusion-as-non-positional,
  or a recorded weakening.
- **The two arrows** — parturition-irreversibility vs frame-scale shear as rival renderings of the
  thermodynamic arrow; break-test named: within-frame entropy increase with no generation. Lives:
  physics §9. Closes with: adjudication against the break-test.
- **The Bekenstein sort** — whether the framework has any quantitative cross-frame relation at all
  (ties to A4); currently "not threatened, and not testable there." Lives: physics §10.
- **The Planck floor** — if reality has a minimum length, P1's antecedent is false *as physics*;
  "the closest thing the framework has to a falsifier-shaped exposure." Lives: physics §8.
- **The spin-½ 2π phase** — measured; owed an account. Two candidate accounts recorded *as shapes,
  not claims* (parturition preimages; orientation-space double-connectedness, gated on A5). Lives:
  physics §6. Closes with: a derivation making one candidate an account.
