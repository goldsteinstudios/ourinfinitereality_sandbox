## Substantive changes beyond the flags

1. **Physics §1 rewritten** (the R2/R3 propagation patch set 2 explicitly deferred). Old: "The
   implicit register now forces revolution rather than positing it. Bₙ does not obtain, so the two
   mode-dominant regions… the relation between them is carried by Gₙ revolving about Bₙ…" New:
   "The two mode-dominant regions of a frame have no path between them — **the transit condition
   is itself the excluded condition** — and the relation between them is **carried by descent**.
   What the structure admits besides is revolution…" Matches structural §5/§6 exactly.

2. **Math, the two-returns theorem: the single-operation Euler identity reinstated** ("typed in an
   earlier chain and dropped in transit — reinstated here"). Since 1 = e^{i·0}:
   **e^{iπ} + e^{i·0} = 0** — "not five constants in miraculous conjunction but one operation at
   two angles"; general form e^{iθ} + e^{i(θ+π)} = 0; no single θ produces 0 — the center is named
   only as the sum of a conjugate pair, "the ν-centroid's most famous instance." Entered as
   rendering, explicitly. (Replaces the shorter "e^{iπ} = −1… most celebrated equation" remark.)
   Note: the earlier-corpus form of this identity was written e^{iπ} + e^{i2π} = 0 (essay 15, site
   copy); v9.4.2 uses the equivalent θ = 0 form.

3. **Math open item 3 (Dimension) substantively expanded.** The two rivals for "exactly three" now
   have names — the **binding-extortion schema** and the **|1| cascade** (|0| cannot hold →
   distinction → two sides; two sides infinitely separated → |1| cannot hold → perpendicular turn;
   the plane recreates |1| one level up → second turn; three axes span all directions; "each step
   an identity, not a production") — with a candidate adjudication, unruled: the binding schema
   rests on the binding requirement, now chart, so **the cascade may be the only implicit-register
   candidate standing**, converging with the sphere-of-arcs route. "Exactly three" remains open.

4. **DDJ: the scope-note hypothesis removed.** The "Attestation, carried" paragraph now ends "…and
   then shows the pairs." Deleted: "Candidate reading of the difference: a scope note gets
   prepended when readers have begun taking the 可 for the 常 — the same drift the 也-erosion and
   the staged 有生於無 record elsewhere." This was change-log §B item 9 (a machine inference,
   marked candidate in the text). **Its removal closes open item B11 by withdrawal** — reported
   here so the register catches it on regeneration.

5. **R5/R6 comment placement**: the two pending-rewrite comments now sit immediately *above* the
   有無相生 and Chapter 40 section headings rather than below them. Flags stand; both entries
   otherwise unchanged (the rewrites remain session work).
