Method: every passage in the four chains doing something other than stating a derivation step,
categorized as **illustration**, **project commentary**, **reader address**, or **register
commentary**, with a removal-dependency verdict. The physics chain's charter is "rendering — map +

inside the spine itself, but both are dependencies a future edit could silently break.**

## ⚠ Loud flag 1 — structural §1's instance triplet is both ruled canon and load-bearing for physics §2
- **Passage** (structural §1): *"Indistinction does not obtain in a frame built on distinction.
  Where up equals down there is no height. Where before equals after there is no time. Where void
  equals form there is no reality."*
- **Category:** illustration — by §0's own typing these are named pairs, i.e. explicit-register
  instances, sitting in the implicit-register chain.
- **But:** the change log §A records this line as a **ruling of Will's** applied to structural §1;
  and **physics §2 builds directly on the temporal instance** ("Where before equals after there is
  no time — that is a frame's own collapse" is the opening move of the chain's "closest structural
  fit" entry).
- **Removal verdict:** the structural chain's general claim ("equality removes what the frame
  was") stands without the instances, so no spine step breaks — but extraction would orphan
  physics §2's ground and would remove ruled text. Not an editorial candidate without a ruling.

## ⚠ Loud flag 2 — the DDJ chain carries the notation warrant for all four chains
- **Passage** (just_ddj, register-declaration section): *"That licenses the notation these chains
  use. 有 and 無 are names — 可名, an indexed instance; X and Y are not names of form and void;
  they are the slots the two modes occupy."*
- **Category:** project commentary (a justification of the project's own notation).
- **Removal verdict:** **the warrant exists nowhere else.** Structural §0 states the slot fact but
  not the license derived from the text. If this paragraph is ever moved to the editorial layer,
  the chains' X/Y-vs-有/無 discipline loses its stated ground. Flag for a ruling before any move.

## Dangling reference — the editorial record
- All four chains close: *"v9.3 — the chains state the framework as currently known; the editorial
  record holds how it got here."* **No editorial record exists for this set** (v8 had one).
  Category: project commentary; removal breaks nothing; the reference is currently false. Also
  logged as open item B14.
